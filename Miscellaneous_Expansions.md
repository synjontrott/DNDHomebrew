# **Legends of the Elsaither: Miscellaneous Expansions & Homebrew Compendium**

## **Introduction**
Welcome to the *Miscellaneous Expansions & Homebrew Compendium* for **Legends of the Elsaither** (LotE). This supplementary volume houses modular expansions, experimental player options, rare ecological anomalies, and auxiliary rules designed for the D&D 2024 ruleset. 

These options are intentionally preserved in this module as standalone supplements, ready to be introduced into campaigns when players or Dungeon Masters seek unique options beyond the core campaign guide.

# **Chapter 1: Supplementary Species**

## **Mycorites (Sentient Fungal Colonies of Ninji)**

> *"When Crystalarium radiation seeped into the subterranean mycelium beds of Ninji, the earth did not merely mutate—it awakened. Four feet tall, capped in neon vibrancy, and humming with elemental resonance, a Mycorite is neither animal nor spirit. They are living memories of the soil, loyal protectors of our farms, and ancient friends forged in trade and spore."*  
> — **Master Farmer Taelon of the Ninji Highlands**

---

### **Origin & Crystalarium Resonance**
Mycorites are a rare, sentient species of fungal humanoids that develop when ambient **Crystalarium radiation** permeates the early developmental stages of subterranean fungal colonies. While most commonly discovered on the fertile, bio-dense world of **Ninji**, isolated colonies have occasionally been uncovered across derelict orbital wrecks and mineral-rich cavern systems throughout the Elsaither galaxy.

Unlike the Krenpowen, humans, or other humanoid species of the cosmos, Mycorites do not possess traditional spiritual power spirits. Instead, they share a biological and metaphysical connection with the planetary ecosystem—much like ancient ironwood trees, resonant crystal blooms, and indigenous beasts. Through this natural connection, every Mycorite is born permanently infused with **elemental resonance**, harmonizing directly with one of the sixteen cosmic elements of the Elsaither universe.

### **Physical Appearance & Elemental Chromatics**
Standing roughly **4 feet tall**, Mycorites possess a compact, sturdy humanoid physiology composed of dense, spongy, and fibrous fungal tissue. Their heads are crowned with broad, domed, or flared mushroom caps, often speckled with bioluminescent nodules that softly pulse in rhythm with their emotional state.

A Mycorite's coloration directly reflects its innate elemental affinity. Their pigmentation ranges from brilliant, luminous neon hues to soft, earthy pastels:
*   **Fire:** Radiant shades of orange, ember, and volcanic vermilion.
*   **Water:** Deep azure, oceanic blue, and translucent cerulean.
*   **Ice:** Frosted teal, glacier cyan, and pale periwinkle.
*   **Earth:** Ochre, terracotta, rich loam brown, and mossy stone.
*   **Air:** Ethereal translucent white, gossamer grey, and sky mist.
*   **Lightning:** Electric lemon, sparkling gold, and vibrant amber.
*   **Laser:** Vivid crimson, ruby red, and focused beam-magenta.
*   **Gas:** Toxic emerald, chartreuse, and hazy chartreuse green.
*   **Metal:** Lustrous silver, burnished bronze, and gunmetal grey.
*   **Bone:** Pale ivory, chalk white, and bleached alabaster.
*   **Shadow:** Deep twilight violet, indigo, and velvet obsidian.
*   **Death:** Ash charcoal, slate black, and decaying sepia.
*   **Poison:** Neon magenta, venomous lime, and bruised purple.
*   **Knife (Cold Laser):** Prismatic icy silver and razor-sharp lavender.
*   **Darki:** Abyssal void-black with shimmering dark amethyst sheen.
*   **Sky:** Cloud-white with swirling storm-grey and electric highlights.

### **Biology, Metabolism, & Reproduction**
*   **Lifespan & Maturity:** Mycorites live for up to **70 years**. Spores mature into fully developed, sentient juveniles within 3 to 5 years, rapidly absorbing the collective knowledge of their colony.
*   **Hyperactive Decomposition:** Mycorites do not possess conventional digestive tracts or mouthparts for chewing. Instead, they sustain themselves through a process of **hyperactive decomposition**. By temporarily rooting their lower rhizomes into the soil adjacent to organic material (such as fallen fruits, wild vegetation, or small animal carcasses), they release rapid enzymatic secretors that break down and absorb nutrients in minutes.
*   **Natural Fertilizer Generation:** A miraculous byproduct of their digestive rooting is the generation of enriched, mineral-dense **elemental fertilizer**. Soil rooted by a Mycorite is revitalized with nitrogen, trace Crystalarium minerals, and elemental vitality, allowing adjacent crops and orchards to flourish at three times their standard rate.
*   **Asexual Spore Reproduction:** Mycorites have no biological sexes or genders, nor do they form romantic pair-bonds. When a colony reaches optimal health and resource stability, individuals shed microscopic spores in communal germination chambers, birthing the next generation.
*   **Communal Bonds:** Every Mycorite in a colony regards every other member as a cherished, lifelong friend. Hostility within a colony is virtually unheard of. Nearby colonies generally maintain cordial, cooperative relations, trading resources and sharing regional scouts.

### **E-Type Resonant Telepathy**
Because they lack vocal cords and lungs, Mycorites communicate with one another and external species using **specialized e-type electromagnetic brain waves**. 

Their complex neural networks transmit gentle, resonant psychic vibrations directly into the minds of nearby beings. While their cognitive faculties are slightly simpler than humanoids—resembling an exceptionally clever, tool-using primate with deep emotional empathy—they are enthusiastic learners capable of mastering nuanced languages, tactical concepts, and artisan crafts.

Mycorites dwell in close-knit communities of **100 to 200 members**. They favor sheltered, defensible environments—most notably **crashed starship hulks**, overgrown volcanic caverns, and deep moss-groves beneath the canopy of Ninji's ancient forests. 

A standard colony organizes itself into distinct, cooperative castes:

| Caste | Population | Primary Role & Responsibilities |

| **Workers** | 1–20 | The builders, excavators, and architects. They reinforce cavern walls, repurpose starship scrap metal, clear debris, and maintain colony infrastructure. |
| **Magicians** | 1–50 | Practitioners of innate elemental resonance. They assist hunters and guards by casting supportive cantrips, igniting forge fires, creating freshwater, or treating injured colony members. |

| **Scouts** | 1–25 *(Large Colonies)* | Solitary, stealthy pathfinders who map distant territories, track rival predator migrations, establish contact with neighboring colonies, and request aid from friendly Krenpowen. |

### **Symbiosis with Krenpowen & Ninji Culture**
One of the most heartwarming cultural dynamics on Ninji is the generations-old **symbiosis between Krenpowen farmers and Mycorite colonies**:

1.  **Garden & Farm Protectors:** Krenpowen farmers often intentionally foster conditions for Mycorites near their agricultural homesteads. The Mycorites provide round-the-clock defense against wild vermin, enrich the topsoil with their unique bio-fertilizers, and assist with harvest processing. In return, the farmers provide an endless supply of organic compost, orchard trimmings, and secure shelter.
2.  **Childhood Mentors & Companions:** Because of their gentle nature and natural elemental resonance, Mycorite magicians frequently play with young Krenpowen children, demonstrating harmless cantrips and helping them unlock their innate elemental gifts.
3.  **Generational Skill Transmission:** Mycorites possess an astonishing capacity for skill specialization. When a Ninji blacksmith, chef, or martial artist trains a generation of Mycorite workers or guards, those Mycorites faithfully encode the techniques into their collective colony memory, passing the exact smithing recipes, weapon forms, and cooking techniques down to succeeding generations.
4.  **Predator Defense:** When massive apex predators of the Ninjonian cluster—such as **Meites** (giant ape-like armored beasts), **Vortzhen** (massive 8-foot-tall wolf-bear hybrids), or **Serraks**—threaten a region, Mycorite scouts are dispatched to nearby Krenpowen settlements to summon warrior militias, fighting side-by-side with their humanoid allies.

## **Mycorite Species Traits**

Your Mycorite character has the following species traits.

*   **Creature Type:** Plant
*   **Size:** Small (approximately 3 to 4 feet tall)
*   **Speed:** 30 feet

### **Traits & Abilities**

*   **Age:** Mycorites mature rapidly within 3 to 5 years of spore germination and live up to 70 years.
*   **Elementally Charged:** You choose your elemental affinity at character creation from the 16 Elsaither elements (Fire, Water, Ice, Earth, Air, Lightning, Laser, Gas, Metal, Bone, Shadow, Death, Poison, Knife, Darki, Sky). You gain **Resistance** to your element's primary damage type. You also learn **one cantrip of your choice** corresponding to your element from the Savaroen spell list. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this spell (choose when you select this species).
*   **E-Type Resonant Telepathy:** You can communicate telepathically with any creature within **30 feet** of you that you can see using specialized e-type brain waves. You do not need to share a language with the creature, but it must be able to understand at least one language.
*   **Hyperactive Decomposition:** You do not need to eat standard rations. Instead, during a Short Rest, you can root into fertile soil, fallen vegetation, or organic matter to digest nourishment and sustain yourself for the next 24 hours. When you complete a Short Rest in this way:
    *   The soil within a 10-foot radius of you is infused with hyperactive elemental fertilizer for 24 hours. Any forage, herbal harvest, or crop yield gathered from this area within that period yields double the normal amount of materials.
    *   You have **Advantage on saving throws against the Poisoned condition**, and you have Resistance to Poison damage from ingested toxins.
*   **Generational Specialization:** You inherit the specialized teachings and collective muscle memory of your colony's lineage. Choose one of the following specializations at character creation:
    *   **Colony Royal (Coordinator):** You gain proficiency in the **Insight** or **Persuasion** skill. Additionally, as a Bonus Action, you can take the **Help action** to assist an ally within 30 feet of you on an attack roll or ability check. You can use this Bonus Action a number of times equal to your **Proficiency Bonus**, regaining all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
    *   **Colony Magician (Elemental Weaver):** You gain proficiency in the **Arcana** or **Nature** skill. You also learn **one additional cantrip** of your choice from the Savaroen spell list matching your primary element or one of its secondary damage types.
    *   **Colony Guard (Spore Defender):** You gain proficiency in the **Athletics** or **Intimidation** skill. When you take the **Dodge action** on your turn, your Armor Class increases by an additional **+1** until the start of your next turn.
    *   **Colony Hunter (Wild Tracker):** You gain proficiency in the **Perception** or **Survival** skill. In addition, your base walking speed increases by **5 feet** (to 35 feet).
    *   **Colony Scout (Cavern Pathfinder):** You gain **Darkvision** out to a range of 60 feet. You gain proficiency in the **Stealth** skill, and you have Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made to hide in caverns, subterranean ruins, or dense foliage.
    *   **Colony Worker (Ninji Artisan):** You gain proficiency with **Smith’s Tools**, **Carpenter’s Tools**, or **Tinker’s Tools**, and proficiency in the **Athletics** skill. Whenever you make an ability check using your chosen artisan tool, you add double your Proficiency Bonus to the check.

# **Chapter 2: Druid Subclass & Variant — Circle of Wildcrafting**

## **Overview & Concept**
In the natural world, true mastery of the wilds does not solely belong to those who take on the shape of beasts. It belongs equally to the **Wildcrafters**—druidic alchemists, herbalists, and botanists who practice the ancient, uncultivated art of foraging, harvesting, and distilling the latent magical energies of flora, fungi, roots, and minerals.

A Wildcrafter reads the forest floor like an open apothecary. By communing with wild ecosystems and channelling primal magic, they conjure translucent, crystalline potion flasks from thin air, filling them with shimmering botanical draughts. Their elixirs grant surging vitality, speed, invisibility, elemental resilience, and deadly toxins to their companions.

This chapter presents the **Circle of Wildcrafting** as both:
1.  **A Standard D&D 2024 Subclass:** Seamlessly integrated with the standard Druid class progression at Levels 3, 6, 10, and 14 using the *Wild Shape* resource engine.
2.  **An Alternate Class Overhaul (The Dedicated Wildcrafter):** An optional variant for players and DMs who wish to replace beast transformation entirely in favor of dedicated herbal alchemy.

## **Druid Subclass: Circle of Wildcrafting**

### **Level 3: Circle Spells**
Your intimate study of botany and natural vitality infuses you with primal magic. You always have the following spells prepared, and they do not count against the number of spells you can prepare each day.

| Druid Level | Prepared Spells |
| :---: | :--- |
| **3rd** | *Detect Poison and Disease*, *Goodberry*, *Purify Food and Drink* |
| **5th** | *Barkskin*, *Lesser Restoration* |
| **7th** | *Plant Growth*, *Protection from Energy* |
| **9th** | *Greater Restoration*, *Mass Cure Wounds* |

---

### **Level 3: One with the Wild**
You have spent your life studying uncultivated nature and gathering wild reagents.
*   You gain proficiency in the **Nature** and **Survival** skills, as well as proficiency with the **Herbalism Kit**.
*   If you already have proficiency in either of these skills, you gain **Expertise** in that skill (adding double your Proficiency Bonus to checks made with it).

---

### **Level 3: Wildcrafting (Primal Draughts)**
You gain the ability to forge crystalline potion flasks out of thin air and fill them with potent primal draughts using wild plants, roots, berries, and minerals:

*   **Brewing Draughts:** You can spend **10 minutes** foraging and brewing to create one Wildcraft Potion of your choice from the *Wildcraft Potions Table*. Alternatively, in the heat of combat, you can magically synthesize one potion as an **Action by expending one use of your Wild Shape**.
*   **Capacity & Potency:** You can maintain a number of active Wildcraft Potions equal to your **Wisdom modifier + your Proficiency Bonus** (minimum 2). If you create a potion that exceeds this maximum, the oldest potion loses its potency and evaporates into harmless dew. A potion retains its potency until you finish a Long Rest.
*   **Consuming & Administering:** As a **Bonus Action**, a creature can drink a Wildcraft Potion or administer it to a willing or incapacitated creature within 5 feet.
*   **Scaling Potency:** The numerical values of your potions (damage, healing, or duration) are determined by your Druid level, as shown on the **Wildcraft Potions Table**.

---

### **Level 3: Witch Doctor**
Your mastery of botanical medicine maximizes the restorative impact of all elixirs:
*   Whenever a creature drinks a healing potion you created, or whenever you administer any healing potion or restorative elixir to another creature, that creature regains **additional Hit Points equal to your Druid level**.

---

### **Level 6: Growing Stronger & Accelerated Infusion**
Your connection to wild alchemy deepens, granting you faster brewing and enhanced delivery methods:
*   **Additional Use:** You gain one additional use of Wild Shape (or can create an additional potion per Short Rest).
*   **Thrown Draughts:** You can throw a potion (such as a Poison or splash draught) up to **30 feet** as a Bonus Action, targeting a creature or an unoccupied 5-foot space.
*   **Invigorating Residue:** Whenever an ally drinks one of your duration-based Wildcraft Potions (*Resistance*, *Speed*, *Water Breathing*, *Climbing*, or *Strength*), they gain **Temporary Hit Points equal to your Wisdom modifier + your Druid level**.

---

### **Level 10: Building Immunities**
Constant exposure to wild fungi, reactive botanicals, and venomous secretions has permanently altered your physiology:
*   **Total Immunity:** You gain **Immunity to Poison damage** and the **Poisoned condition**, and you are immune to all non-magical and magical diseases.
*   **Inoculating Tonic:** Whenever you finish a Short or Long Rest, you can distribute an herbal tonic to up to a number of willing allies equal to your **Wisdom modifier**. Until their next rest, each affected ally gains **Advantage on saving throws against Poison and Disease**, and gains Resistance to Poison damage.

---

### **Level 14: Wisdom of the Wild**
You achieve total sensory communion with the plant kingdom:
*   **Botanical Omniscience:** You passively sense all flora, fungi, and herbal resources within **120 feet** of you. You instantly know each plant's exact species, botanical properties, medicinal applications, and elemental affinities. There is no natural or magical plant you cannot immediately identify.
*   **Master Synthesis:** When you use Wildcrafting to brew a potion, you can combine the effects of **two different potion types** into a single dual-action elixir.
*   **Apex Die Scaling:** All dice rolled for your Wildcraft Potions increase from **d6s to d8s** (e.g., an 18th-level potion rolls **8d8** instead of 8d6).

### **Potion Descriptions & Mechanical Rules**

#### **1. Potion of Resistance**
*   **Ingredient:** Hardy plants, desert succulents, or bark from ironwood trees.
*   **Effect:** The drinker gains **Resistance to one damage type of your choice** (Acid, Cold, Fire, Force, Lightning, Necrotic, Poison, Radiant, or Thunder) for a duration in minutes equal to the die roll (minimum 1 minute).

#### **2. Potion of Vitality**
*   **Ingredient:** Invasive weeds, creeping ivy, and relentless wild vines.
*   **Effect:** The drinker immediately regains Hit Points equal to the die roll, and is cleansed of **one level of Exhaustion** or the **Paralyzed** or **Poisoned** condition.

#### **3. Potion of Water Breathing**
*   **Ingredient:** Seaweed, river reeds, or freshwater algae.
*   **Effect:** The drinker can breathe underwater and gains a **swimming speed equal to their walking speed** for a duration in minutes equal to the die roll (minimum 1 minute).

#### **4. Potion of Speed**
*   **Ingredient:** Tart wild berries, quickthorn seeds, or sun-ripened briars.
*   **Effect:** The drinker’s base walking speed is **doubled**, and they can take the **Dash action as a Bonus Action** on each of their turns for a duration in minutes equal to the die roll (minimum 1 minute).

#### **5. Potion of Poison (Toxic Draught)**
*   **Ingredient:** Nightshade berries, hemlock stems, or toadstool caps.
*   **Effect:** Can be applied to a weapon as a Bonus Action (coating up to 3 successful strikes) or thrown as a splash up to 30 feet. The target takes **Poison damage equal to the die roll** and must succeed on a **Constitution saving throw against your Spell Save DC** or gain the **Poisoned condition** for 1 minute. An affected creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the condition on a success.

#### **6. Potion of Invisibility**
*   **Ingredient:** Sapling flowers, twilight orchids, or translucent ghost petals.
*   **Effect:** The drinker gains the **Invisible condition** for a duration in minutes equal to the die roll (minimum 1 minute). The effect ends early if the creature makes an attack roll, deals damage, or casts a spell that deals damage or forces a saving throw (at 14th level, casting non-damaging utility spells does not break this invisibility).

#### **7. Potion of Climbing (Earthstrider)**
*   **Ingredient:** Smooth river stones, cliff-side lichens, or mountain moss.
*   **Effect:** The drinker gains a **climbing speed equal to their walking speed** and has **Advantage on Strength (Athletics) checks** for a duration in minutes equal to the die roll (minimum 1 minute).

#### **8. Potion of Healing (Restorative Elixir)**
*   **Ingredient:** Rare floral blooms, mountain blossoms, or crystal-water petals.
*   **Effect:** The drinker regains Hit Points equal to the rolled dice + flat bonus (e.g. 1d4+1 up to 8d6+8) **plus your Druid level** (via *Witch Doctor*).

#### **9. Potion of Strength (Primal Might)**
*   **Ingredient:** Magic beans, iron-pod legumes, or sprouted oak acorns.
*   **Effect:** The drinker gains **Advantage on Strength checks and Strength saving throws**, and adds a bonus to **melee weapon and unarmed damage rolls equal to your Wisdom modifier** for a duration in minutes equal to the die roll (minimum 1 minute).

## **Optional Class Overhaul: The Dedicated Wildcrafter Variant**

For campaigns and players who prefer to replace beast transformation entirely in favor of an alchemical, botany-centric core class, use the following replacement rules:

### **Level 2: Wildcrafting (Replaces Wild Shape)**
*   You do not gain the standard *Wild Shape* feature. Instead, you gain a dedicated pool of **Wildcrafting Uses** equal to **2** per Short or Long Rest. This pool increases to **3 uses at Level 6** and **4 uses at Level 17**.
*   You spend these uses to brew Wildcraft Potions as an Action or throughout a 10-minute rest.
*   At 2nd level, you are dedicated exclusively to the **Circle of Wildcrafting**.

### **Level 18: Timeless Body & Potent Chemistry (Replaces Beast Spells)**
*   **Timeless Body:** The primal magic within you slows your aging process. For every **10 years** that pass, your physical body ages only **1 year**.
*   **Alchemical Spellcasting:** You can infuse any 1st- through 5th-level spell you have prepared that has a casting time of 1 Action into a drinkable potion flask as part of a 10-minute ritual. Any creature that drinks the potion immediately triggers the spell with the drinker as the target or center of origin.

### **Level 20: Master of the Craft (Replaces Archdruid / Subclass Capstone)**
*   You can use your **Wildcrafting an unlimited number of times**.
*   Whenever you roll Initiative at the start of combat, you can immediately and freely synthesize up to **two Wildcraft Potions of your choice** into your hands without expending any actions or resources.

# **Chapter 3: Supplementary Species — The Fae Lineages of the Otherworld**

## **Historiographical & Ontological Lore**
The entities known across ancient myths as the **Fae**, **Faerie**, or **Fair Folk** represent a complex, alien, and sovereign ecology originating from a parallel dimension known as the **Otherworld** (or *Tír na nÓg*). Far removed from sanitized, modern nursery tales, the true folkloric fae are beings of a "middle nature" between mortals and celestials—possessing fluid, ethereal forms composed of condensed cloud and twilight air.

Rooted in the ancient Irish chronicles of the *Lebor Gabála Érenn* (The Book of the Taking of Ireland), the high fae trace their lineage to the **Tuatha Dé Danann** (the Tribe of the Gods of Craft). Masters of druidry, architecture, and esoteric combat, the Tuatha Dé Danann retreated into the subterranean *sídhe* (ancient earthen burial mounds) following their wars with mortals, transitioning in mortal memory into the **Aos Sí**—the immortal inhabitants of the Secret Commonwealth.

## **Fae Species Traits (D&D 2024 Standards)**

When creating a Fae character, choose one of the following four distinct morphological lineages reflecting the historical taxonomy of the Secret Commonwealth.

# **Chapter 4: New Class — The Veilweaver**

The **Veilweaver** is an occult, Charisma-based spellcaster and supernatural gish who does not cast spells through academic rote or divine devotion, but by manipulating the ancient contractual laws, sensory glamours, and True Names of the Otherworld.

Bound by the ancient codes of the *Secret Commonwealth*, a Veilweaver weaves reality-altering illusions, extracts metaphysical tithes from fallen foes, rides alongside the Wild Hunt, and invokes the legendary relics of the Tuatha Dé Danann.

## **Class Features**

### **Hit Points & Proficiencies**
*   **Hit Dice:** 1d8 per Veilweaver level
*   **Hit Points at 1st Level:** 8 + your Constitution modifier
*   **Hit Points at Higher Levels:** 1d8 (or 5) + your Constitution modifier per level after 1st
*   **Armor Proficiencies:** Light Armor
*   **Weapon Proficiencies:** Simple weapons, Martial Finesse weapons (Rapiers, Scimitars, Shortswords, Whips), Hand Crossbows, Blowguns, Shortbows
*   **Tool Proficiencies:** One musical instrument or gaming set of your choice, Herbalism Kit or Calligrapher's Supplies
*   **Saving Throws:** Dexterity, Charisma
*   **Skills:** Choose 3 from *Arcana*, *Deception*, *History*, *Insight*, *Intimidation*, *Nature*, *Perception*, *Persuasion*, *Sleight of Hand*, and *Stealth*.

---

### **Level 1: Spellcasting**
You channel the raw, archaic magic of the Otherworld.
*   **Cantrips:** You know 3 cantrips of your choice from the Veilweaver spell list. You learn additional cantrips at higher levels as shown on the class table.
*   **Prepared Spells:** You prepare the list of Veilweaver spells available for you to cast, choosing from the Veilweaver spell list. You prepare a number of spells equal to your **Veilweaver level + your Charisma modifier** (minimum 1 spell), as shown on the table.
*   **Spellcasting Ability:** **Charisma** is your spellcasting ability for Veilweaver spells.
    *   **Spell Save DC** = 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your Charisma modifier
    *   **Spell Attack Modifier** = your Proficiency Bonus + your Charisma modifier
*   **Spellcasting Focus:** You can use a musical instrument, a carved rowan sprig, an antique mirror, or an inscribed bone token as a spellcasting focus for your Veilweaver spells.

---

### **Level 1: Veilcraft & Glamour Pool**
You harness the illusory mist of the Otherworld, represented by a pool of **Veil Points**.
*   **Veil Points:** You have a number of Veil Points as shown in the *Veil Points* column of the Veilweaver table. You regain all expended Veil Points when you finish a **Short or Long Rest**.
*   **Veil Glamour (1 Point):** As a Bonus Action, you can spend 1 Veil Point to weave a sensory illusion. You gain the benefits of the *Disguise Self* spell, or you can force one creature within 30 feet to make a Wisdom saving throw against your Spell Save DC. On a failure, the creature perceives you as its trusted confidant and has Disadvantage on attack rolls against you until the start of your next turn.

---

### **Level 1: The Weight of a Name**
In fae lore, an entity’s True Name or physical token represents absolute metaphysical sympathy.
*   As a **Reaction**, when a creature within 60 feet of you casts a spell, attacks, or speaks, you can invoke the sympathetic resonance of its name or presence. The target must succeed on a **Charisma saving throw against your Spell Save DC** or suffer **Disadvantage on all attack rolls and ability checks** made until the end of its current turn.
*   Alternatively, if an ally is attacking a creature whose True Name or biological token you possess, you can use this reaction to grant the ally **Advantage on the attack roll**.
*   You can use this reaction a number of times equal to your **Proficiency Bonus**, regaining all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest. Alternatively, you can spend **1 Veil Point** to use this reaction again after your free uses are expended.

---

### **Level 2: Iron Aversion & Warded Flesh**
The ancient pacts of the Otherworld grant you supernatural protection against the mortal realm, balanced by the fatal curse of cold forged iron.
*   **Supernatural Warding:** While you are wearing light armor or no armor, your Armor Class equals **10 + your Dexterity modifier + your Charisma modifier**. In addition, you have **Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage** dealt by non-iron weapons.
*   **Cold Iron Aversion:** When you take damage from a weapon forged of pure iron (cold iron) or raw refined steel, you take an additional **1d6 Force damage**, and your *Otherworld Step* and *Veil Glamour* features are suppressed until the end of your next turn.

---

### **Level 2: Otherworld Step**
You can seamlessly step through the liminal seams of reality.
*   As a **Bonus Action**, you can spend **1 Veil Point** to magically teleport up to **30 feet** to an unoccupied space you can see. 
*   When you reappear, you can choose to become **Invisible** until the start of your next turn or until immediately after you attack or cast a spell.

---

### **Level 3: Veilweaver Mantle (Subclass Selection)**
You dedicate yourself to a sovereign mantle of the Secret Commonwealth:
*   **Mantle of the Courts (The Sovereign Courtier)**
*   **Mantle of the Wild Hunt (The Vanguard of the Host)**
*   **Mantle of the Contract (The Pact Broker)**

Your mantle grants you features at **Level 3, Level 6, Level 10, and Level 14**.

---

### **Level 5: Sídhe Geas (Contractual Taboo)**
You can bind foes and oathbreakers with the supernatural weight of a fae decree.
*   When you cast an Enchantment or Illusion spell of 1st level or higher, you can spend **2 Veil Points** to place a *Sídhe Geas* on one affected target. 
*   If the target willingly breaks a spoken agreement, attacks you after you offer parley, or targets you with a hostile action within 1 minute, it takes **Psychic damage equal to twice your Veilweaver level** and must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw against your Spell Save DC or gain the **Stunned condition** until the end of its next turn.

---

### **Level 7: Co-Walker Doppelgänger**
Drawing upon the Scottish folklore documented by Robert Kirk, you can manifest an ethereal phantom twin.
*   As an Action (or as part of using *Otherworld Step*), you spend **2 Veil Points** to create a lifelike illusory duplicate of yourself in an unoccupied space within 30 feet. The duplicate lasts for **1 minute** or until destroyed.
*   The co-walker has an AC equal to your Spell Save DC and Hit Points equal to **three times your Veilweaver level**.
*   On your turn, you can mentally command the co-walker to move up to 30 feet (no action required). It can speak with your voice and mimic your gestures.
*   When a creature attacks you while your co-walker is within 30 feet of you, you can use your Reaction to swap places with the co-walker, causing the attack to target the duplicate instead.

---

### **Level 9: Second Sight**
Your eyes are permanently opened to the hidden dimensions of the cosmos.
*   You gain **Truesight** out to a range of **30 feet** and **Darkvision** out to **120 feet**.
*   You automatically perceive visual illusions, see invisible creatures and objects, and instantly detect shapechangers in their true forms within your Truesight range.

---

### **Level 11: Sovereign Glamour**
Your enchantments pierce mortal and immortal minds alike.
*   Spells and abilities you use that impose the **Charmed condition** bypass immunity to being Charmed. If a target possesses immunity to being Charmed, it instead makes its saving throw with **Advantage**.

---

### **Level 13: Ethereal Transposition**
You can instantly fold space across the veil of worlds.
*   As a Reaction when you or a willing ally within 30 feet of you takes damage, you can spend **2 Veil Points** to swap the positions of yourself and the ally, or teleport the targeted ally up to 30 feet to an unoccupied safe space.

---

### **Level 15: Tír na nÓg Time Dilation**
You manifest the extreme temporal distortion of the faerie realm into the mortal world.
*   As an Action, you can spend **5 Veil Points** (or use this feature once per Long Rest without expending points) to create a **30-foot aura of dilated time** centered on yourself for 1 minute (requiring Concentration).
*   **Allies** within the aura gain a **+2 bonus to Armor Class**, **Advantage on Dexterity saving throws**, **+10 feet to their walking speed**, and can make **one additional weapon attack** when taking the Attack action on their turn.
*   **Enemies** that enter or start their turn within the aura must succeed on a **Wisdom saving throw against your Spell Save DC** or be subjected to the *Slow* spell while remaining inside the aura.

---

### **Level 18: Secret Commonwealth Avatar**
You become an acknowledged sovereign of the Otherworld.
*   You cease aging and are immune to any effect that would age you magically.
*   You gain permanent **Immunity to the Charmed and Frightened conditions**, as well as immunity to possession.

---

### **Level 19: Epic Boon Feat**
You gain an **Epic Boon Feat** of your choice from the standard D&D 2024 selection or the LotE Epic Boons.

---

### **Level 20: Sovereign of the Otherworld (Capstone)**
Your mastery over the veil is total and unyielding.
*   Whenever you roll **Initiative** at the start of combat, you instantly regain **6 Veil Points**.
*   Using your *The Weight of a Name* feature no longer expends your Reaction; you can use it a number of times per round equal to your **Charisma modifier** (minimum once).
*   Your *Otherworld Step* can now teleport you up to **60 feet**.

### **Mantle 2: Mantle of the Wild Hunt (The Vanguard of the Host)**

*   **Spectral Steed:** You can cast *Find Steed* without expending a spell slot or material components. *   When you hit a marked creature or harvest a Soul Shard, you unleash a terrifying spectral shriek.

#### **Level 14: The Green Knight’s Resilience**
Channeling the immortal resilience of Arthurian lore’s Green Knight:
*   When you are reduced to 0 Hit Points, you do not fall unconscious. Instead, you instantly drop to **1 Hit Point**, and your body erupts in an explosion of spectral briars and emerald lightning.
*   Each hostile creature within **30 feet** of you must make a Constitution saving throw against your Spell Save DC, taking **6d10 Force damage** and becoming **Frightened** for 1 minute on a failure (or half damage on a success).
*   Once you use this feature, you cannot do so again until you finish a **Long Rest**.

### **Mantle 3: Mantle of the Contract (The Pact Broker)**
*Channeling Rumpelstiltskin, the tooth-harvesting Bone Fae, and the absolute laws of fae reciprocity, the Pact Broker manipulates the action economy through forced trades, biological tokens, and binding bargains.*

#### **Level 3: Law of Equivalent Exchange & The Bone Tithe**
*   **Law of Equivalent Exchange:** As a **Reaction** when an ally within 30 feet of you takes damage, you can balance the cosmic ledger: redirect up to a number of damage points equal to **your Veilweaver level + your Charisma modifier** to a hostile creature within 30 feet. To use this feature, you must spend **1 Veil Point** or expend **1 Hit Die**.
*   **The Bone Tithe (Sympathetic Tokens):** Whenever a creature within 30 feet takes Bludgeoning, Piercing, or Slashing damage, you can use your Reaction to magically extract a biological token (a tooth, lock of hair, or bone fragment). You can store up to a number of Tithes equal to your **Charisma modifier** (minimum 1).
    *   *Sympathetic Hex:* When you cast an Enchantment or Necromancy spell targeting a creature whose Tithe you hold, you can expend the Tithe to impose **Disadvantage on the target's initial saving throw** against the spell.
    *   *Tithe Consumption:* You can consume a Tithe as a Bonus Action to regain **1 expended Veil Point**, or to cast a 1st- or 2nd-level Veilweaver spell without expending a spell slot.

#### **Level 6: The Rumpelstiltskin Bargain**
You offer a willing companion an astonishing, rule-breaking advantage—at a steep, asymmetrical price.
*   As a **Bonus Action**, you spend **2 Veil Points** to strike a bargain with an ally within 30 feet until the start of your next turn. Choose one of the following boons:
    1.  **Golden Spindle:** The ally's next successful attack before the start of your next turn is automatically a **Critical Hit**.
    2.  **Veil Shield:** The ally gains **Advantage on all saving throws** and **Resistance to all damage** until the start of your next turn.
    3.  **Surge of the Sídhe:** The ally can immediately take an additional **Action** on its turn (a creature can receive this boon only once per Short Rest).
*   **The Fae Debt:** On the ally's subsequent turn, the debt is collected: the ally's speed is reduced by half, and it cannot take Reactions until the end of that turn.

#### **Level 10: Ironclad Literalism & Loophole Mastery**
*   You can never be compelled to tell the truth by magical means, yet you never physically break a sworn word. You gain **Advantage on all Charisma (Deception, Insight, and Persuasion)** checks.
*   **Treaty Defense:** If an enemy attacks you after you have offered parley, or if an enemy attacks anyone under your hospitality, you can use your **Reaction** to immediately cast a prepared spell of 1st through 3rd level targeting that creature without expending a spell slot. You can use this reaction once, and regain the ability to do so when you finish a Short or Long Rest, or by expending **3 Veil Points**.

#### **Level 14: True Name Foreclosure**
You invoke absolute metaphysical mastery over a bound target.
*   As an Action, you speak the conceptual True Name of a creature whose Tithe you hold or whose identity you have fully researched.

*   While dominated, the creature cannot harm you or your allies and must execute your telepathic commands to the absolute letter. The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns and each time it takes damage, ending the effect on a success. Once you use this feature, you cannot do so again until you finish a **Long Rest**.

## **1. Barbarian: Path of Carnage**

*Where other barbarians channel ancestral spirits or primeval fury, the Barbarian of the Path of Carnage is addicted to the sensory rush of total slaughter. Their rage is a visceral bloodbath, turning their weapon strikes into momentum-driven gore.*

### **Level 3: Taste for Slaughter & Visceral Mutilation**
*   **Taste for Slaughter:** When you are in a Rage, whenever you reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points or score a Critical Hit with a melee weapon attack, you can immediately make one additional melee weapon attack as a Bonus Action. In addition, you gain **Temporary Hit Points equal to your Barbarian level + your Constitution modifier**.
*   **Visceral Mutilation:** Once per turn while raging, when you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack using *Reckless Attack*, you can brutally rend its flesh. The target takes additional **Slashing damage equal to your Rage Damage bonus + your Proficiency Bonus**, and its wounds bleed profusely, preventing it from regaining Hit Points until the start of your next turn.

### **Level 6: Sanguine Momentum**
The scent of spilled blood accelerates your slaughter:
*   Whenever you reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points or score a Critical Hit, your walking speed increases by **10 feet** for the rest of your turn, and you can move through enemy spaces without provoking Opportunity Attacks.
*   You have **Advantage on saving throws against being Frightened**. If you are Frightened when you enter a Rage, the condition immediately ends.

### **Level 10: Terrifying Carnage (Gore Display)**
When you fell an adversary, you display its butchery to break the minds of its allies:
*   When you reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points with a melee weapon attack while raging, you can use your **Reaction** to unleash a blood-curdling roar or gory display. 
*   Each hostile creature within **30 feet** of you that can see or hear you must make a **Wisdom saving throw** (DC = 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your Strength or Constitution modifier). On a failure, a creature gains the **Frightened condition** for 1 minute. A frightened creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the condition on a success.

### **Level 14: Endless Bloodbath**
Your addiction to slaughter defies death itself:
*   While raging, if you take damage that would reduce you to 0 Hit Points, you can use your **Reaction** to immediately make one melee weapon attack against an enemy creature within your reach before your Hit Points are reduced.
*   If the attack hits, you drop to **1 Hit Point instead of 0**, you regain **Hit Points equal to the damage dealt by the attack**, and your Rage continues uninterrupted.
*   Once you use this feature, you cannot do so again until you finish a **Short or Long Rest**.

## **2. Bard: College of Darkness**

*Bards of the College of Darkness do not sing tales of valor or romance. They compose hymns to the void, shadow opera, and psychological torment—weaving suffocating gloom, sow paranoia, and drowning their foes in umbral despair.*

### **Level 3: Dark Symphony & Stygian Words**
*   **Bonus Proficiencies & Cantrip:** You gain proficiency in the **Deception** and **Stealth** skills. You also learn the *Chill Touch* or *Minor Illusion* cantrip (it counts as a Bard cantrip for you).
*   **Shadow Verses:** You can weaponize your Bardic Inspiration to drown minds in twilight. As a **Bonus Action**, you expend one use of your Bardic Inspiration and target one creature you can see within 60 feet. Roll your Bardic Inspiration die. The target is enveloped in clinging magical shadows for 1 minute:
    *   *Umbral Hex:* The target must subtract the number rolled on your Bardic Inspiration die from its next saving throw or attack roll made within that minute.
    *   *Suffocating Gloom:* The target's vision is constrained to 15 feet; beyond that distance, it is considered **Blinded**. The target can make a Wisdom saving throw against your Spell Save DC at the end of each of its turns, ending this effect on a success.

### **Level 6: Chorus of the Abyss**
You harmonize with the primeval void:
*   You learn the *Darkness* and *Pass without Trace* spells. They count as Bard spells for you, are always prepared, and do not count against your prepared spells limit. You can cast each of these spells once per Long Rest without expending a spell slot.
*   You can see normally through magical and non-magical darkness out to a distance of **60 feet**.
*   **Eerie Resonance:** Whenever an enemy creature fails a saving throw against an Enchantment, Illusion, or Necromancy spell you cast, it takes **Psychic damage equal to your Charisma modifier** (minimum 1). When this occurs, you can use your **Reaction** to force a second creature within 15 feet of the target to make a Wisdom saving throw or gain the **Frightened condition** until the end of its next turn.

### **Level 14: Requiem of Eternal Night**
You conduct the ultimate opus of cosmic annihilation:
*   As an **Action**, you perform a haunting melody that manifests a **30-foot-radius sphere of magical darkness** centered on yourself for 1 minute (requiring Concentration).
*   While within this sphere:
    *   You and all allies you designate gain **Advantage on attack rolls** and **Resistance to Cold and Necrotic damage**.
    *   Hostile creatures within the sphere suffer **Disadvantage on all attack rolls and saving throws**, and take **3d10 Psychic or Necrotic damage** (your choice) at the start of each of their turns.
*   Once you use this feature, you cannot do so again until you finish a **Long Rest**, unless you expend a **5th-level or higher spell slot** and one use of your Bardic Inspiration to manifest it again.

# **Chapter 6: New Base Class — The Bread Mage (Kenyérmágus)**

## **Class Overview & Philosophy**

> *"First, they buried me under the cold ground, and I survived.*  
> *When I sprouted toward the sun, they cruelly cut me down with sickles, and I survived.*  
> *They threshed me with their iron flails, and I survived.*

The **Bread Mage** (*Kenyérmágus* in the ancient tongue) is a resilient, agrarian primal-arcane spellcaster whose magic is not drawn from dusty tomes or fickle patrons, but from the immortal resilience of the sacred grain. 

Rooted in ancient folklore and the sacred rites of the hearth, a Bread Mage has spiritually—and physically—passed through the **Sixfold Ordeal of the Grain**: burial in the dark earth, the slicing blade of the sickle, the blunt trauma of the flail, the pulverizing pressure of the millstone, the stretching torment of the kneading trough, and the searing agony of the roaring clay oven (*Kemence*). Having endured all six deaths and risen as the golden, consecrated loaf that feeds nations, the Bread Mage commands absolute mastery over earth, dough, steam, yeast fermentation, protective golden crusts, and primordial hearth-fire.

*   **Cantrips:** You know three cantrips of your choice from the **Bread Mage Spell List**. Whenever you gain a Bread Mage level, you can replace one cantrip you know with another from the spell list. You learn additional cantrips at higher levels as shown in the Cantrips Known column.
*   **Spell Slots:** The Bread Mage table shows how many spell slots you have to cast your spells of 1st level and higher. To cast one of these spells, you must expend a slot of the spell’s level or higher. You regain all expended spell slots when you finish a **Long Rest**.
*   **Prepared Spells:** You prepare the list of Bread Mage spells that are available for you to cast, choosing from the Bread Mage spell list. The number of spells you can prepare equals your **Wisdom modifier + your Bread Mage level** (minimum of one spell).
*   **Spellcasting Ability:** **Wisdom** is your spellcasting ability for your Bread Mage spells, reflecting your spiritual harmony with the ancestral rhythm of harvest, nourishment, and hearth.

    *   **Spell Attack Modifier** = your Proficiency Bonus + your Wisdom modifier

### **Level 1: The Sixfold Crucible of Grain (*A Hat Próba*)**
You have survived the six ancient tortures of the wheat, engraving their metaphysical lessons into your very flesh and soul. You possess a pool of **Kovász Points** (Living Leaven Points) equal to **your Bread Mage level + your Constitution modifier** (minimum of 2 points). You regain all expended Kovász Points when you finish a **Long Rest**, and you regain a number of points equal to your **Wisdom modifier** (minimum 1) when you finish a **Short Rest**.

You can expend Kovász Points to activate the following Ordeal Arts:

1.  **The First Ordeal: The Burial (*Elhantolás — Earth Endurance*):** As a **Bonus Action** (1 point), you attune to the cold earth that buried you. Until the end of your current turn, you gain a **Burrow speed of 15 feet** through loose earth, sand, grain, or flour, and you gain **Tremorsense out to 15 feet** for 1 minute.
2.  **The Second Ordeal: The Sickle (*Aratás — Severance Defiance*):** When you take Slashing damage or gain the Grappled or Restrained condition, you can use your **Reaction** (1 point) to slip through the binding blade. You reduce the incoming damage by **1d8 + your Constitution modifier** and immediately end the Grappled or Restrained condition on yourself.
3.  **The Third Ordeal: The Threshing (*Cséplés — Unyielding Stalk*):** When a creature hits you with a melee attack or attempts to push or knock you Prone, you can use your **Reaction** (1 point) to plant your feet like rooted grain. You cannot be moved or knocked Prone against your will, and the attacker takes **Bludgeoning damage equal to your Proficiency Bonus in d6s** as kinetic shockwaves violently rebound.
4.  **The Fourth Ordeal: The Milling (*Őrlés — Flour Particulate Mist*):** As a **Bonus Action** (1 point), you briefly dissolve into a 10-foot-radius cloud of fine, swirling flour dust. You can teleport to an unoccupied space you can see within **20 feet**, and attack rolls made against you have **Disadvantage** until the start of your next turn.
5.  **The Fifth Ordeal: The Kneading (*Dagasztás — Elastic Dough Physique*):** As a **Bonus Action** (1 point), your limbs and joints become as pliable and resilient as masterfully kneaded dough for 1 minute. Your melee attack reach increases by **5 feet**, you have Advantage on Strength (Athletics) checks to Grapple, and you can pull a grappled target up to 10 feet toward you when you hit it.
6.  **The Sixth Ordeal: The Searing Oven (*Kemencesütés — Hardened Golden Crust*):** Whenever you cast a Bread Mage spell of 1st level or higher, you can expend 1 Kovász point as part of the casting to flash-bake your skin into an armor of blazing, golden crust. You gain **Temporary Hit Points equal to twice your Bread Mage level** for 1 hour, and your melee attacks deal an additional **1d6 Fire damage** while these Temporary Hit Points remain.
