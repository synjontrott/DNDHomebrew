## **Course Introduction & Linguistic Overview**

Welcome to the **Ninjonian Linguistic Compendium**, the definitive grammatical, morphological, and lexicographical teaching guide for the languages of the Elsaither galaxy.

The **Ninjonian Cluster language** (*Spaechi Ninjoni*) serves as the primary root language (lingua franca) across much of the known cosmos. From this root language stems an interconnected family of regional dialects, planetary vernaculars, and phonological shifts spanning Ninjonia, Techno, Warrion, and remote frontier settlements. # **Part I: Orthography, Scripts, & Dialect Variations**

## **1.1 The Three Writing Scripts**
Writing in the Elsaither universe is categorized into three primary calligraphic scripts:
1. **Hybrid Script (Standard):** Characterized by balanced strokes with moderate curves and defined serifs. Used by Standard Ninjonian, Ninji, Battacarian, Tek, and Southern Ninjarikan.
2. **Curved Script (Techno):** Characterized by rounded, aerodynamic, and looping glyphs optimized for technical engraving and digital readouts. Used across the Technika territories.
3. **Straight Script (Warrion):** Characterized by sharp, angular, vertical, and linear runes carved into bone, metal, and ceremonial ironwood.

## **3.2 Galactic Slang & Idiomatic Expressions**

The warriors, scavengers, and void-farers of the Elsaither galaxy use specialized slang to express extreme emotion, frustration, or allegiance:

*   **Zag (Singular) / Zagen (Plural):** The amount of emotional energy or "cares" a person has left. Used when completely drained of patience or empathy.
    *   *Usage:* *"Maen piroeken zag!"* or *"Maen oif sok zag!"* $\rightarrow$ *"I don't give a single care!"*
*   **Smiroak:** A derogatory insult used against someone acting like a crying baby, showing physical weakness, or demonstrating a complete lack of combat talent.
*   **Urba:** An expletive expressing sudden disappointment, irritation, or minor confusion.
*   **Yukfa:** A severe, high-tier curse word used to express burning fury, utter disgust, or to condemn someone. (A much stronger version of *Urba* or *Guaro*).
*   **Guaro:** A condemnation uttered in bitter annoyance or irritation toward an obstacle or failure.
*   **Zaraen:** A grave accusation branding someone a traitor, heretic, or turncoat.
*   **Dakvura:** The universal term for the dark, intoxicating bloodlust and aggression awakened by shadow, necrotic, or forbidden elemental arts.
*   **Wemblow:** An insult denoting someone who is completely, irredeemably stupid (far worse than standard *Rikue*).
*   **Compound Curse Example:**
    *   *"Ugh, taen rikue yukfa!"* $\rightarrow$ *"Ugh, you stupid piece of filth!"* (Or *"taen rikue smiroak"* if less severe).

# **Part V: Primordial Hidan (The Shadow Whisper)**

## **5.1 Nature & Syntax of Primordial Hidan**
**Primordial Hidan** (*Hidan Primora*) is the ancient tongue spoken by **Nyxids**, **Shadows**, and **Shadow Tendrils**. 

Unlike the expressive Ninjonian Cluster, Primordial Hidan is a dark, sibilant whisper. It obeys several unique grammatical laws:
1. **Omission of Articles & Filler Copula:** Words such as *"the"*, *"a"*, *"an"*, and *"are"* do not exist.
   * *Example:* The question *"Are you the chosen one?"* becomes *"You chosen?"* in Hidan.
2. **Strict SVO Sentence Economy:** Follows a terse Subject-Verb-Object (SVO) order with minimal embellishment.
3. **Phonetic Delivery:** Whispered through the vocal cords or transmitted as an ethereal psychic resonance.

```

 ├──────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────┼───────────────────────┤
 │ Abandon                  │ OSBEVRU                  │ Oz-bev-roo            │
 │ Bleed                    │ HAETSHOLT                │ Hayt-sholt            │
 │ Chosen / Choose          │ IBAN                     │ Ee-bahn               │
 │ Close / Near             │ SHAELA                   │ Shay-lah              │
```
