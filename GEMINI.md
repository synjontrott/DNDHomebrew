# D&D Homebrew: Legends of the Elsaither (LotE)

## Project Objective
Create a perfectly balanced D&D Homebrew guidebook based on LotE Lore. The result should be fun to play and compliment the standard D&D 2024 classes as if it were a canon rulebook.

## Core Mandates
- **2024 Compatibility:** All content must be built for and balanced against the D&D 2024 rules (PHB, DMG, MM).
- **Balance:** Prioritize mathematical fairness and progression consistency. Ensure homebrew options do not overshadow or underperform compared to official options.
- **Lore Fidelity:** All descriptions, names, and mechanics must align with the "Legends of the Elsaither" campaign setting.
- **Technical Writing:** Use the standard wording and syntax found in official D&D sourcebooks (e.g., proper capitalization of game terms, clear action economy).

## Project Status & Guidance (July 2026)
- **Feats Architecture:** A comprehensive feats section has been established, including Origin Feats, General Feats (Level 4+), Mastery Feats (Level 12+), and Epic Boons (Level 16+). All feats are mathematically scaled to D&D 2024 standards.
- **Mutations Expansion:** The `Mutations_Expansion_Guide.md` has been established, formalizing 21 rare elemental variations (e.g., Blood, Red Death, Witherbone). All flat DCs and static damage have been replaced with dynamic scaling (Spell Save DC, PB, Spellcasting Modifiers).
- **High-Risk/High-Reward System:** Mutations are balanced using a "Power/Danger" framework. Dangers must use D&D 2024 mechanics (Hit Dice, scaled Saving Throws, self-damage) and should be narratively violent or taxing to justify their enhanced power. Action economies for teleportation or status effects are strictly enforced.
- **Damage Profiles:** All mutations must follow a strict 1-primary/3-secondary damage type profile.
- **Comprehensive Audit:** Both the `Mutations_Expansion_Guide.md` and the `Legends_of_the_Elsaither_Campaign_Guide.md` have been fully audited and corrected for D&D 2024 balance, fixing infinite loops and overpowered capstones.
- **Action Economy:** The 'Dual Player' mode for the Shadow Warrior is a "Hero Character" mechanic that replaces two PC slots at the table, thus retaining a double action economy to keep both players engaged.
- **Cantrip Standardization:** All cantrips have been adjusted to provide utility or temporary benefits rather than permanent healing or overpowered debuffs.
- **Currency:** Standardized as "cc" (Crystalarium Currency) across all primary documents.
- **Reference Management:** Legacy homebrew volumes and individual class files are archived in the `ReferenceDocuments/` directory.
