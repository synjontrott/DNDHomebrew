# D&D Homebrew Project Agents

> **Cross-Reference:** This file works alongside [`GEMINI.md`](./GEMINI.md) and [`CLAUDE.md`](./CLAUDE.md). All three files share the same project mandates; refer to them together for full context.

The primary goal of this project is to create a perfectly balanced D&D Homebrew guidebook based on Legends of the Elsaither (LotE) Lore. The content must be fun to play and compliment the standard D&D 2024 classes as if it were a canon rulebook.

</subagent>

## **Recent Audit & Project Progress (August 2026)**

The project has completed a major mechanical audit and introduced structured character progression:

1.  **Feats Architecture:** A robust feats system has been added to Chapter 3, defining Origin Feats, General Feats, Mastery Feats, and Epic Boons tailored to the LotE universe.
2.  **Mutations Expansion:** The `Mutations_Expansion_Guide.md` has been established, formalizing 21 rare elemental variations (e.g., Blood, Red Death, Witherbone).
3.  **High-Risk / High-Reward Balancing:** Each mutation follows a strict "Power vs. Danger" framework. Dangers are designed using D&D 2024 conditions (Hit Dice, scaled saving throws) to ensure they are narratively impactful without breaking early/late game balance.
4.  **Standardized Elemental Profiles:** Every mutation must follow a 1-primary/3-secondary damage type profile to maintain system consistency.
5.  **Action Economy:** The 'Dual Player' mode for the Shadow Warrior remains a "Hero Character" mechanic for two players sharing one body.
6.  **Terminology & Workspace:** Currency is standardized as "cc," and all legacy volumes are archived in `ReferenceDocuments/`.
7.  **White Shadow Mutation Fix (August 2026):** The Power of the White Shadow mutation was revised. "No penalties in bright light" had zero mechanical effect in D&D 2024 and was replaced with a meaningful prismatic distortion ability (Advantage on Sleight of Hand, 1/Short Rest blinding flash).
8.  **Deity Spell Integration (August 2026):** A "Deity Blessing" class feature was added to both the Savaroen and Shadow Warrior, formalizing the mechanical pathway to obtain a Deity's Cantrip and Unique Spells. All shorthand deity spell descriptions in Chapter 6 were expanded to full D&D 2024 block format.
9.  **Technical Rules & RAW Audit (August 2026):** Resolved missing durations across all chapters (including Warrion `Elemental Trance` 1-minute limit), missing action costs (Animal Spirit, Bonded Weapon, Automaton Assistant), and RAW loopholes.
10. **6-Agent 2024 OP Balance Sweep (August 2026):** Conducted a multi-agent balance sweep. Adjusted Techno Railgun (2d10 Loading), capped spell damage multipliers (Fusion Blast, Potent Spellcasting), re-leveled tier-inappropriate features (Mind Break, Rift Maker), and set Epic Boon prerequisites to Level 19+.
11. **Species Traits & Benchmarks (August 2026):** Genempriea updated to 35ft base speed + *Overclocked Actuators* (Bonus Action Dash PB/LR). Plunesako expanded to grant dual skills (Origin Feat removed for balance parity). Krenpowen/Tenebrie rebuilt as the standard elementally charged species (2024 Human equivalent) with dual skill proficiencies and *Resourceful Determination* (Heroic Inspiration on Long Rest) — *Elemental Heritage* Origin Feat removed to hit exact 2024 Human benchmark.
12. **Per-Region Magic Item Compendiums (August 2026):** Expanded Chapter 4 with 150 total balanced magic items organized into 5 regional compendiums (Ninjonia, Techno, Warrion, Nidosis, Shadow/Ancient Elsaither) with exactly 2 items per rarity (Common to Legendary) across Weapons, Armor/Shields, and Wondrous Items.
13. **High-Fantasy Subclass Titles & Disconnect Audit (August 2026):** Renamed all informal parenthetical subclass titles to rich high-fantasy titles (e.g. *The Blademaster Discipline*, *The Arcane Technomancer*, *The Forge Sovereign*) and resolved feature disconnects (e.g. replacing Rogue Evasion on Ninjonian with *Martial Counter*).
14. **Glass Cannon Mutation Audit (August 2026):** Re-tuned all 21 mutations in `Mutations_Expansion_Guide.md` to ensure high offensive/utility potency balanced by active, taxing D&D 2024 penalties (self-burn, speed penalty encasement, cover loss).
15. **Full 28-Deity Spell Compendium (August 2026):** Standardized all 28 Deities in Chapter 6 (starting with Pireous down to Zarta) to 100% compliance. Every deity features a 3-option cantrip `(Utility), (Utility), (Damage)` and 5 unique spells mapped to 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 5th, and a 7th/8th Level Capstone spell (168 total deity spells).
16. **Inherent Magic & 2024 Class Parity Audit (August 2026):** Standardized *Elementally Charged* across all 5 species (Krenpowen, Genempriea, Garanko, Plunesako, Nyxid) to grant Primary Resistance + 1 Elemental Cantrip of choice (Int/Wis/Cha), guaranteeing complete mathematical balance and zero cantrip economy exploits when played with standard D&D 2024 PHB classes.
17. **Campaign Guide Modularization (August 2026):** Split the monolithic `Legends_of_the_Elsaither_Campaign_Guide.md` into 7 chapter files in the `Chapters/` directory for efficient agent-based editing and reduced token overhead per task. The monolith is regenerated by concatenating all chapter files and is kept up to date for PDF/Google Docs export.
18. **Beast Master Class Added & Refactored (August 2026):** A Wisdom-based half-caster class established in `Chapters/Chapter_02c_Beast_Master.md`. Wildshaping removed in favor of a 2-Pillar Engine: **Pillar I (Spirit Companion)** and **Pillar II (Aspect Mutations & Apex Aspect Mutations)**. Standard companion movement and basic commands (Attack, Dash, Disengage, Help, Search) cost **0 PE**. PE expenditure is reserved for 1-PE Enhanced Strikes, 1-PE Aspect Mutations, and 2-PE Apex Aspect Transformations. Features: 1d8 HD, PE pool (`Lvl + Wis mod`), Weapon Mastery (2), Extra Attack (L5), Enhanced Pillars (L11), Primal Arcanum (1/LR 4th-level at L13 & 5th-level at L17), Spirit Sentinel (L18), Epic Boon feat (L19), Avatar of the Mark capstone (L20). Six subclasses: **Mark of the Wolf**, **Mark of the Dragon**, **Mark of the Bear**, **Mark of the Owl**, **Mark of the Eagle**, **Mark of the Sheep**.
19. **8-Chapter Comprehensive 2024 Balance Sweep (August 2026):** Conducted an 8-agent parallel audit of all chapter files and the Mutations Expansion. Resolved 37 critical and 34 minor issues. Key fixes: Elementally Charged stripped to Resistance + Cantrip only; Primal Arcanum corrected to half-caster-appropriate 4th/5th-level spells; all 28 deity damage cantrips gained level scaling at 5th/11th/17th; 7 overpowered deity spells brought to correct tier; Gruhunsh Haste corrected to 1 minute; Railgun corrected to 1d12; Blood/Black Bone mutation Dangers redirected to harm the caster; Warrion Trance spell-lock added; Techno attunement gated to L10/L14; Ink-Web limited to 1/LR.
20. **Apex Aspect Flight Elimination & Land Companion Normalization (August 2026):** Rebalanced all 6 Apex Aspect Transformations (2 PE) to eliminate true flying speeds, replacing flight with ground speed boosts, zero-drag slipstream leaps, tripled jump distance, and fall-damage-free gliding. Normalized land-based spirit companions (Lynx, Fox, Lion, Horse, Serpent, etc.) with explicit 40–50 ft walking speeds and land agility traits (`Silent Prowl`, `Pouncing Stride`) when selected under flying marks.
21. **18-Subclass 2024 PHB Power Budget Audit (August 2026):** Executed a 6-subagent panel audit across all 18 subclasses comparing them against official D&D 2024 PHB baselines (Fighter 26.0, Ranger 27.0, Warlock 28.0, Paladin 29.0, Wizard 30.0). Confirmed complete power budget parity for homebrew classes (Savaroen 28.5, Shadow Warrior 28.0, Beast Master 28.5 out of 40.0) with zero overshadowing of official 2024 PHB classes. Standardized all subclasses to 2024 `Prepared Spells` syntax and title casing.
22. **Vanguard Class Added (August 2026):** A new no-spellslot STR/CON martial class established in `Chapters/Chapter_02d_Vanguard.md`. Core mechanics: **Dakvura (The Shift)** — a Barbarian-analog transformation (1d12 HD, BPS Resistance, +speed, enhanced weapon damage scaling 1d4→1d10) with **Feral Bloodlust** (forced nearest-target aggression, Charisma save to override, resolved at Level 9 via *Controlled Bloodlust*). Magical effects are achieved through **Elemental Arts** (a use-per-rest pool scaling 2→10, recovering half PB on Short Rest) rather than spell slots. Class features: Weapon Mastery (3), Fighting Style (L1), Ruthless Onslaught (L11 cleave on kill in Dakvura), Relentless Shift (L13 free use on Initiative), Vanguard's Protection (L15 Reaction intercept), Perfected Dakvura (L18 expanded Resistance + elemental type), Epic Boon (L19), Abyssal Executioner (L20 unlimited Dakvura + 6d10 Con save/Stun on 2 Art uses). **16 Elemental Disciplines** — one per element in the LotE Elemental System: *The Infernal Discipline* (Fire), *The Tide Sovereign* (Water), *The Glacial Bulwark* (Ice), *The Stonewarden Discipline* (Earth), *The Gale Sovereign* (Air), *The Stormstrike Discipline* (Lightning), *The Radiant Sovereign* (Laser), *The Miasmic Discipline* (Gas), *The Ironclad Discipline* (Metal), *The Ossuary Tyrant* (Bone), *The Umbral Sovereign* (Shadow), *The Reaping Discipline* (Death), *The Venomblood Sovereign* (Poison), *The Razoreige Discipline* (Knife/Cold Laser), *The Void-Iron Sovereign* (Darki), *The Stormcrown Discipline* (Sky). Each subclass provides 4 Elemental Arts at Level 3 (Strike, Burst, Defensive Reaction, and Control Arts) plus features at Levels 3, 7, 10, 14, and 17, culminating in elemental Immunity and a free/reduced-cost Art at Level 17. Estimated power budget: 28.0–29.5/40 (in line with Savaroen and Beast Master).
23. **Resistance & Immunity Progression Audit (August 2026):** Standardized elemental Resistance and Immunity across species, classes, and subclasses. (a) **1st-Level Class Bonds (Savaroen *Spiritual Awakening*, Beast Master *Primal Bond*):** Grant Resistance to the chosen class element if not already possessed (enabling dual resistances for hybrid builds), preventing early Level 1 damage Immunities. (b) **7th-Level Mastery Milestones:** Full elemental **Immunity** is awarded at Tier 2 (Savaroen *Haranoma* and Beast Master Mark of the Dragon *Elemental Scales*). (c) **Mark of the Bear — Dragonkin Apex:** Replaced redundant resistance with *Tenacious Resilience* (Advantage on Con saves + once-per-Short-Rest Reaction to drop to 1 HP instead of 0). (d) **Vanguard Perfected Dakvura (L18):** Replaced redundant Immunity with *Elemental Supremacy* (attacks and Arts ignore Resistance and treat Immunity as Resistance).
24. **Savaroen Etymology Confirmed (August 2026):** *Savaroen* is Ninjonian language for "Warrior." All future lore text, class descriptions, and glossary entries should reference this.
25. **Ninjonian Glossary Corrections & Elemental Specialties Added (August 2026):** Corrected several Ninjonian translations and added two new Elemental Specialty options to the Savaroen at Level 7. Confirmed terms: *Hiarelobi* = "Gifted Hybrid" (updated from "Dual Element" parenthetical); *Saistinum* = "Spirit Blade / Spirit Cut"; *Venokibara* = "Fang Resonance"; *Haranoma* = "Hold Back"; *Hokosha* = "The Book of God." New Specialties written into `Chapters/Chapter_02_Classes.md`: **Venokibara (Fang Resonance)** — once-per-turn 1d10 elemental weapon bonus damage + 1/SR/LR detonation on max roll; **Haranoma (Hold Back)** — upgrades primary elemental Resistance to Immunity + Proficiency Bonus elemental retaliation on melee hits (free Reaction, once per turn).

Introduced the **Mycorites**—a sentient, 4-foot-tall elemental fungal species born from Crystalarium radiation on Ninji. Balanced for D&D 2024 (Plant type, Small, 30ft, Elementally Charged, E-Type Resonant Telepathy, Hyperactive Decomposition & fertilizer creation, and 6 Generational Colony Specializations).
27. **Druid of Wildcrafting Subclass & Variant Added (August 2026):** Added Chapter 2 to `Miscellaneous_Expansions.md` containing the **Circle of Wildcrafting** Druid subclass (D&D 2024 3rd/6th/10th/14th-level progression using Wild Shape to brew 9 primal draughts, Witch Doctor healing boosts, throw mechanics, poison immunities, and botanical omniscience) as well as the **Dedicated Wildcrafter Variant** (replacing Wild Shape with dedicated potion pools and Beast Spells/Archdruid with alchemical spell infusions and unlimited synthesis).
