# **Chapter 1: The Savaroen — Part 1: Mythic/Paragon Ascendance (Levels 21–30)**

*In the ancient scriptures of the Hokosha, it is written that when a Savaroen pushes past the twentieth threshold of awakening, their inner power spirit no longer merely dwells within their chest. Instead, the boundary between mortal flesh and primordial elemental energy dissolves entirely. At Level 21, every Savaroen—regardless of their initial subclass (Ninjonian, Ninjarikan, Warrion, Technika, Tek, Techno, or Ninji)—chooses one of two modular Epic Ascensions (Paragon Paths): the martial-focused **Spirit Blade Sovereign** or the spell-focused **Elemental Archon**. These paths allow any character to tailor their mythic evolution to their preferred style of play while maintaining perfect mathematical balance with D&D 5.5e (2024 Revision).*

---

## **The Mythic/Paragon Tier (Levels 21–30) Overview**

At Tier 5 (Levels 21–30), characters transcend mortal limitations:
*   **Proficiency Bonus:**
    *   **Levels 21–24:** **+7**
    *   **Levels 25–28:** **+8**
    *   **Levels 29–30:** **+9**
*   **Hit Dice:** You continue gaining 1d8 (or 5) + your Constitution modifier in Hit Points per Savaroen level beyond 20th.
*   **Ability Score Limits:** At 21st level, the maximum score for your **Charisma** and **Constitution** increases to **24** (and up to 26 via subsequent epic improvements).
*   **Modular Paragon Path Selection:** At 21st level, choose one of two Paragon Paths:
    *   **Path 1: The Spirit Blade Sovereign (Hokosha Sword-Saint)** — The martial perfection of blade, kinetic speed, and devastating elemental swordplay.
    *   **Path 2: The Elemental Archon (Font of the Primordial Weave)** — The arcane mastery of raw elemental evocation, weave sculpting, and cataclysmic spellcraft.
*   **10th-Level Primordial Magic:** At 26th level, all Savaroen unlock a **10th-level spell slot** to cast ancient Primordial Rites.

## **Core Paragon Class Features**

### **Paragon Surge**
*21st-Level Savaroen Feature*

Your mastery over elemental energy expands into mythic potency:
*   **Enhanced Die:** The bonus damage granted by your *Elemental Surge* increases from 1d8 to **1d10** of your chosen elemental type.
*   **Multi-Strike Application:** Once per turn, you can apply your Elemental Surge damage to **two separate attacks** (weapon or spell attacks) rather than just one.
*   **Expanded Reservoir:** You regain all expended uses of Elemental Surge whenever you complete a **Short Rest** or a **Long Rest**.
*   **Ability Score Expansion:** The maximum for your Charisma and Constitution scores increases from 22 to **24**.

---

### **Spiritual Apotheosis**
*22nd-Level Savaroen Feature*

Your *Spiritual Resonance* evolves into an impenetrable aegis:
*   **Double Resonance Ward:** When you expend a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, you gain Temporary Hit Points equal to **twice the slot's level + your Charisma modifier**.
*   **Retaliatory Detonation:** While you hold Temporary Hit Points granted by Spiritual Resonance, any creature that hits you with a melee attack takes damage equal to your **Proficiency Bonus + your Charisma modifier** of your primary elemental type.
*   **Aegis Flare:** When these Temporary Hit Points are completely depleted by damage, a shockwave detonates in a 10-foot radius centered on you. Each hostile creature in that area must make a Dexterity saving throw against your Spell Save DC or take **3d10 damage** of your primary elemental type and be pushed 10 feet directly away from you.

---

### **Epic Ability Score Improvement / Epic Boon**
*24th- and 28th-Level Savaroen Feature*

At 24th level, and again at 28th level, you can choose one of the following benefits:
*   Increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or two ability scores of your choice by 1 (to a maximum of 26).
*   Select one **Epic Boon Feat** from Chapter 3 of the *Campaign Guide* or the official D&D 2024 rules (such as *Boon of the First Elementals*, *Boon of the Void Weaver*, or *Boon of Dimensional Travel*).

---

### **Primordial Infusion**
*25th-Level Savaroen Feature*

Your strikes and spells pierce supernatural wards:
*   **Elemental Penetration:** Damage dealt by your Savaroen spells, features, and weapon attacks ignores **Resistance** to your primary and secondary elemental damage types. Against targets with **Immunity**, your damage is treated as dealing **half damage** (standard Resistance) rather than zero.
*   **Primordial Precision:** Once per turn when you roll damage for a Savaroen spell or an Elemental Surge strike, you can reroll up to two damage dice. You must use the new rolls.

---

### **10th-Level Primordial Magic**
*26th-Level Savaroen Feature*

You break through the mortal Ninth Seal of the Elsaither Weave, unlocking a single **10th-level spell slot**. This slot can be used to upcast existing spells or cast one of the following ancient Primordial Rites (regained on a Long Rest):

#### **Cataclysmic Elemental Tempest**
*10th-Level Evocation (Primordial) | Casting Time: 1 Action | Range: Self (120-ft radius) | Duration: Concentration, up to 10 minutes*  
A swirling tempest of your primary element surrounds you. The area is difficult terrain for enemies, and ranged attacks passing through automatically miss. When cast and at the start of each of your turns, you can direct elemental wrath down upon up to five creatures within 120 feet. Each target must make a Constitution saving throw against your Spell Save DC, taking **12d10 elemental damage**, being knocked Prone, and Deafened for 1 minute on a failed save, or half damage on a success.

#### **Aegis of the First World**
*10th-Level Abjuration (Primordial) | Casting Time: 1 Action | Range: Touch | Duration: 1 hour*  
You touch a creature, enveloping it in a crystallized barrier. The target is immune to all damage except Force and Psychic damage, cannot suffer conditions, regains **20 Hit Points** at the start of each of its turns, and emits an aura dealing **4d10 elemental damage** to hostile creatures within 10 feet. (Usable once per 7 Long Rests).

#### **Soul-Weave Convergence**
*10th-Level Transmutation (Primordial) | Casting Time: 1 Action | Range: Self | Duration: 1 minute*  
You merge your body with your inner power spirit. Your walking speed increases by 30 feet, you gain a flying speed (hover) equal to your walking speed, you can cast Savaroen cantrips as a **Bonus Action**, spells of 1st through 5th level do not consume spell slots, and hostile creatures have Disadvantage on saving throws against your spells.

---

### **Paragon Specialty Mastery**
*29th-Level Savaroen Feature*

Your 7th-level *Elemental Specialty* ascends into legendary potency:
*   **Hiarelobi (Tri-Elemental Convergence):** Choose a third elemental affinity. You gain Resistance to its damage type. In addition, you can use *Fusion Blast* twice per Long Rest, combining two elemental types into a single blast dealing maximum damage that ignores resistance.
*   **Venokibara (Fang Annihilation):** The bonus weapon damage from Fang Resonance increases to **2d10** of your elemental type. When either die rolls maximum, the detonation expands to a **15-foot radius**, dealing elemental damage equal to your **Charisma score** (minimum 24) to all hostile creatures.
*   **Haranoma (Absolving Bulwark):** Once per round when you take damage from an attack or spell, you can use your Reaction to reduce that damage by **5d10 + your Charisma modifier + your Savaroen level**. The attacker takes the prevented damage as your elemental damage type.

## **Savaroen Modular Paragon Paths (Choose at Level 21)**

Regardless of your subclass, choose one of the following two Epic Ascensions at Level 21:

```
SAVAROEN PARAGON PATHS (LEVELS 21–30)
 ├── PATH 1: THE SPIRIT BLADE SOVEREIGN (Martial & Kinetic Blade Mastery)
 └── PATH 2: THE ELEMENTAL ARCHON (High Evocation & Weave Manipulation)
```

---

### **Path 1: The Spirit Blade Sovereign (Hokosha Sword-Saint)**
*The Spirit Blade Sovereign embodies the martial perfection of the Savaroen. By fusing swordplay with pure elemental flow, the Sword-Saint can slice through spatial dimensions, parry dragon breath, and unleash storms of blade-light.*

#### **Level 21: Sword-Saint’s Stance**
*   **Martial Mastery:** You gain proficiency with all Martial Weapons and Shields (if you do not already have it).
*   **Blade Velocity:** When you take the Attack Action on your turn, you can make **three melee weapon attacks** instead of two.
*   **Elemental Edge:** Your weapon attacks count as magical and deal an additional **1d8 Force damage** on every hit.

#### **Level 23: Thousand-Cut Counter**
*   **Flurry of Blades:** Once per turn, when you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack, you can immediately make one free attack against a different creature within your reach.
*   **Supreme Martial Counter:** When targeted by an attack, you can use your **Reaction** to add your **Proficiency Bonus (+7 to +9)** to your Armor Class. If the attack misses as a result, you can make an immediate melee weapon attack with Advantage against the attacker.

#### **Level 27: Spatial Cleave of the First Horizon**
*   **Critical Threshold:** Your weapon attacks score a Critical Hit on a roll of **18–20**.
*   **Dimensional Cleave:** When you score a critical hit with a melee weapon, you can teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space adjacent to another enemy and make one additional melee attack against that enemy as part of the same strike.
*   **Whirlwind Annihilation:** You can use your Action to make a melee weapon attack against **every hostile creature within 15 feet** of you.

#### **Level 30: Paragon Capstone — Sovereign of the Divine Edge**
At 30th level, you master the ultimate synthesis of blade and spirit:
*   **Permanent Divine Weapon:** Your *Divine Elemental Weapon* (from Level 20 *The Power Within*) can be summoned at will as a free action, lasts for 1 hour, and deals **3d10 + your Charisma modifier** elemental damage.
*   **Unstoppable Blade:** Your weapon attacks ignore all armor, shields, and magical barriers (such as *Shield* or *Wall of Force*).
*   **Wings of the Sword-Saint:** You gain a flying speed of **60 feet (hover)** as razor-sharp elemental wings sprout from your back.
*   **Parrying Aegis:** You have Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage from all sources.

---

### **Path 2: The Elemental Archon (Font of the Primordial Weave)**
*The Elemental Archon treats the Weave of Elsaither as an instrument to be played. By opening their mortal channels completely, the Archon casts spells with devastating volume, sculpts elemental blasts around companions, and weaves two elements simultaneously.*

#### **Level 21: Archon’s Font**
*   **Arcane Capacity:** You learn two additional Savaroen cantrips and can prepare three additional Savaroen spells.
*   **Spell Sculpting:** When you cast a Savaroen spell that affects an area, you can choose a number of creatures equal to **1 + the spell’s level** to be immune to the spell's damage and effects.
*   **Cantrip Quicken:** You can cast any Savaroen cantrip as a **Bonus Action**.

#### **Level 23: Weave-Overchannel**
*   **Potent Primordial Casting:** Whenever you cast a Savaroen spell that deals your primary or secondary elemental damage, you add your **Proficiency Bonus** to the damage roll.
*   **Elemental Transmutation:** When you cast a spell that deals elemental damage, you can freely change its damage type to any of your unlocked affinities (primary, secondary, or tertiary).

#### **Level 27: Dual-Weave Convergence**
*   **Simultaneous Casting:** Once per Short or Long Rest, when you use your Action to cast a Savaroen spell of 1st through 5th level using a spell slot, you can cast a **second Savaroen spell of 1st through 3rd level** as part of the exact same Action without expending a spell slot for the second spell.
*   **Maximized Primordial Blast:** Twice per Long Rest, when you cast a spell that deals elemental damage, you can deal **maximum damage** instead of rolling.

#### **Level 30: Paragon Capstone — Avatar of the Living Weave**
At 30th level, you become the living avatar of Elsaither's magical weave:
*   **Infinite Reservoir:** You can cast Savaroen spells of **1st through 3rd level at will** without expending spell slots.
*   **Uncounterable Magic:** Your spells cannot be interrupted by *Counterspell*, dispelled, or suppressed by an *Antimagic Field*.
*   **Elemental Omnipotence:** You gain **Immunity** to your primary and secondary elemental damage types. Whenever you cast an evocation spell of 5th level or higher, you gain Temporary Hit Points equal to **four times the spell's level**.
