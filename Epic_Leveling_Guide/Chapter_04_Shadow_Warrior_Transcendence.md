# **Chapter 4: The Shadow Warrior — Part 2: Legendary/Godlike Transcendence (Levels 31–40)**

*At the absolute summit of symbiotic ascension, host and shadow cease to be two entities sharing a shell. They become the Void Itself—an ancient, primordial intelligence older than the stars of Silveronia. At Level 31, every Shadow Warrior—regardless of which Paragon Path they walked in Tier 5—chooses one of two modular Godlike Transcendence Paths: the biological terror of the **Cosmic World-Devourer (Avatar of Nex)** or the reality-warping **Nether Architect (Sovereign of the Cosmic Web)**. At Level 40, these paths culminate in divine deification, granting the character an eternal throne in the pantheon of Silveronia alongside primordial deities like Nex.*

---

## **The Legendary/Godlike Tier (Levels 31–40) Overview**

At Tier 6 (Levels 31–40), the Shadow Warrior achieves divine symbiosis:
*   **Proficiency Bonus:**
    *   **Levels 31–32:** **+9**
    *   **Levels 33–36:** **+10**
    *   **Levels 37–40:** **+11**
*   **Hit Dice:** You continue gaining 1d10 (or 6) + your Constitution modifier in Hit Points per Shadow Warrior level beyond 30th.
*   **Universal Ability Cap:** At 31st level, the maximum score for **all ability scores** increases to **30**.
*   **Modular Godlike Transcendence Selection:** At 31st level, choose one of two Godlike Paths:
    *   **Path 1: The Cosmic World-Devourer (Avatar of Nex)** — The physical manifestation of the Void, rending natural weapons, consuming planetary vitality, and becoming an unkillable elder god.
    *   **Path 2: The Nether Architect (Sovereign of the Cosmic Web)** — The cosmic weaver of spacetime, puppeteering armies through shadow tethers, shattering psyches, and commanding black hole singularities.
*   **Apex Half-Caster Magic:** The Shadow Warrior gains an **8th-level spell slot at Level 35** and a **9th-level spell slot at Level 39**.
*   **Godlike Manifestations:** You gain your 7th and 8th Manifestations at Levels 33 and 37 from the [Godlike Shadow Manifestations](#godlike-shadow-manifestations) table.

## **Core Godlike Class Features**

### **Void Immortal**
*31st-Level Shadow Warrior Feature*

Your physiology is completely subsumed by living cosmic shadow:
*   **Eternal Vitality:** You cease to age, cannot die of old age, and do not need to eat, drink, sleep, or breathe.
*   **Shadow Sustenance:** If reduced to 0 Hit Points, you do not fall unconscious. Instead, your symbiote erupts across your body as an ethereal shadow avatar for 1 minute (with 1 HP, immune to all non-radiant damage). If you regain Hit Points before the minute ends, you stabilize normally.
*   **Universal Ability Cap:** The maximum for all your ability scores increases to **30**.

---

### **Epic Ability Score Improvement / Epic Boon**
*32nd-, 36th-, and 40th-Level Shadow Warrior Feature*

At 32nd level, 36th level, and again at 40th level, you can choose one of the following benefits:
*   Increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or two ability scores of your choice by 1 (to a maximum of 30).
*   Select one **Epic Boon Feat** from Chapter 3 or official D&D 2024 rules.

---

### **7th Manifestation & Dual Soul Cleave**
*33rd-Level Shadow Warrior Feature*

You learn a **7th Shadow Manifestation** from the [Godlike Shadow Manifestations](#godlike-shadow-manifestations) table.
In addition:
*   **Single Player:** When you take the Attack Action on your turn, you can make **three attacks** instead of two, with each strike dealing your full weapon/natural weapon damage plus your *Improved Symbiosis* necrotic damage.
*   **Dual Player:** Both Host and Symbiote make three attacks on their respective turns, bringing the shared body's output to **six attacks per round**.

---

### **8th-Level Shadow Slot & Void Separation**
*35th-Level Shadow Warrior Feature*

You gain an **8th-level spell slot**.
In addition, your symbiote can detach from your physical form while retaining full lethality:
*   **Physical Manifestation:** As an Action (once per Long Rest, lasting up to 1 hour), you split into two distinct bodies: the **Host Form** and the **Shadow Husk**.
*   **Independent Entity:** The Shadow Husk occupies an unoccupied space within 30 feet of you. It shares your AC, has Hit Points equal to half your maximum, possesses your speed, and attacks with your Natural Weapons using your Charisma modifier.
*   **Tactical Coordination:** The Husk acts on your initiative count directly after your turn. You can cast your prepared spells through the Husk as if you were in its space.

---

### **8th Manifestation & Absolute Necrotic Mastery**
*37th-Level Shadow Warrior Feature*

You learn an **8th Shadow Manifestation**.
In addition:
*   **Complete Bypass:** Necrotic and Psychic damage dealt by your attacks, spells, and features **completely ignores all damage Resistances and Immunities**. No deity or ward can reduce this damage.
*   **Essence Drain:** Whenever a creature takes necrotic damage from your attacks or spells, its maximum Hit Points are reduced by an amount equal to the damage dealt until it finishes a Long Rest.

---

### **9th-Level Shadow Slot & Godlike Shadow Rites**
*39th-Level Shadow Warrior Feature*

You gain a **9th-level spell slot**, unlocking the most terrifying shadow magics in existence:

#### **Black Hole Horizon**
*9th-Level Evocation / Transmutation | Casting Time: 1 Action | Range: 150 ft | Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute*  
You tear a microscopic black hole into existence. Creatures within 60 feet are pulled 30 feet toward the center. When created and at the start of each of your turns, creatures in the area must make a Strength save against your Spell Save DC, taking **15d10 Force damage plus 15d10 necrotic damage**, being pulled to the center, and **Restrained** on a failed save (half damage on success).

#### **Void Apotheosis**
*9th-Level Transmutation | Casting Time: 1 Action | Range: Self | Duration: 1 hour*  
You become pure cosmic void energy: gain Immunity to all Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage, and Immunity to Radiant, Necrotic, and Psychic damage; gain a flying speed of **120 feet (hover)**; cast any Shadow Warrior spell of 1st through 5th level at will without expending slots; and enemies starting their turn within 30 feet take **4d10 necrotic damage**.

## **Godlike Shadow Manifestations**

Shadow Warriors of 31st level or higher can select from these Godlike Manifestations:

1.  **Chitin of the Void Sovereign:** Your Armor Class gains a permanent **+3 bonus**. Critical hits against you are converted into normal hits.
2.  **Umbral Wormhole:** As a Bonus Action, open two interconnected shadow portals anywhere within 120 feet of each other for 1 minute. Allies can step through to emerge instantly without expending movement.
3.  **Mind-Flayer Synapse:** You can cast *Telekinesis* and *Mind Spike* (at 6th level) at will without expending spell slots.
4.  **Tendril World-Anchor:** Your Tendril Whip reach increases to **30 feet**. As a Reaction when a creature within reach attempts to teleport or use planar travel, you force a Charisma save; on a failure, teleportation fails and the target takes **8d10 psychic damage**.
5.  **Shadow Swarm Clones:** As an Action (once per Short or Long Rest), dissolve into three shadow duplicates for 1 minute. Each clone has your stats and attacks. If an attack targets you, you can use a Reaction to switch positions with a clone.

 ├── PATH 1: THE COSMIC WORLD-DEVOURER (Mantle of Nex / Elder Nyxid God)

### **Path 1: The Cosmic World-Devourer (Mantle of Nex)**
*The Cosmic World-Devourer embodies the elder hunger of Nex, the supreme spirit of carnage and death in the Elsaither. Famous for his red death energy and devastating weapon empowerment during the wars of Ninjonia and Raiben Vangar, Nex channels his fury through this path, turning the warrior into an immortal cosmic predator capable of consuming stars, tearing down planar titans, and drinking the life force of armies.*

#### **Level 31: Titan of the Abyss**
*   **World-Eater Weapons:** Your Shadow Claws deal **2d12 slashing damage**, and your Tendril Whip deals **2d10 piercing damage** with a reach of **25 feet**.
*   **Abyssal Armor:** You gain permanent **Damage Reduction equal to your Constitution modifier** against all incoming damage.
*   **Fourfold Strike:** When you take the Attack Action, you can make **four attacks** instead of three.

#### **Level 34: God of Slaughter & Blood Siphon**
*   **Vampiric Execution:** When you score a critical hit with a melee attack, you regain Hit Points equal to **half the total damage dealt** by the strike.
*   **Undying Rage:** You cannot be reduced below 1 Hit Point while there is a hostile creature within 10 feet of you, unless struck by a 9th-level or higher spell.
*   **Essence Drain:** Hostile creatures that hit you in melee take **4d10 necrotic damage** (no save).

#### **Level 38: Crimson Cosmic Cataclysm**
*   **Planar Blood-Rupture:** As an Action (once per Long Rest), you detonate the blood of all creatures within a 60-foot radius. Each enemy must make a Constitution saving throw against your Spell Save DC, taking **25d10 necrotic damage**, being Blinded for 1 minute, and having their speed reduced to 0 on a failure (half damage on success). You gain Temporary Hit Points equal to the highest damage taken by a single creature.

#### **Level 40: Divine Ascendance — Living God of the Void (Ascendant Nex)**
At 40th level, you achieve ultimate deification:
*   **Pantheon Seat:** You ascend to the divine council of Elsaither as the **Supreme God of Carnage, Predation, and the Red Death (The Living Nex)**.
*   **Permanent Apex Divinity:** Your *Apex Predator* features are **permanently active**. You constantly possess all of its benefits without needing activation.
*   **Immortal Shroud:** You are permanently Immune to Radiant, Necrotic, and Psychic damage, and immune to all conditions.
*   **Swallow the Cosmos:** Once per turn when you hit a creature with your Natural Weapons, you can force it to make a Charisma saving throw. On a failure, it is dragged into an extraplanar singularity in the Shadow Realm, remaining trapped there until you choose to release it.
*   **Divine Immortality:** If slain, your symbiote reconstitutes your body in the heart of the Shadow Realm within 1d10 days.

### **Path 2: The Nether Architect (Mantle of Umbra & Zarta)**
*The Nether Architect weaves the quantum threads of the Elsaither Void into reality, channeling the dual authority of Umbra (Spiritess of Shadow and Darki) and Zarta (Spiritess of the Void and Raiben Vangar). By manipulating shadow tethers across galactic distances, dominating the psyches of gods, and collapsing spacetime into black holes, this path commands reality itself.*

#### **Level 31: Supermassive Void Artillery**
*   **Six-Beam Eldritch Blast:** Your *Eldritch Blast* now fires **six beams**, each dealing **1d10 + your Charisma modifier + your Proficiency Bonus** Force damage.
*   **Gravity Implosion:** Critical hits with Eldritch Blast trigger an implosion dealing an extra **4d10 Force damage** to all creatures within 10 feet.
*   **At-Will Banishment:** You can cast *Banishment* (at 5th level) at will without expending spell slots.

#### **Level 34: Infinite Umbral Mesh & Psionic Subjugation**
*   **Universal Tether:** You can tether up to **five creatures** simultaneously across any distance. Tethered allies gain your AC, and you can redirect any attack targeting a tethered ally to hit a tethered enemy instead.
*   **Psionic Dominance:** Enemies within 60 feet have Disadvantage on all Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saving throws against your spells and abilities.
*   **Cosmic Befuddle:** You can cast *Befuddle* (*Feeblemind*) once per Short or Long Rest without expending a spell slot.

#### **Level 38: Complete Ego Death & Singularity Tear**
*   **Ego Erasure:** As an Action (once per Long Rest), you touch the psyche of one creature within 60 feet. The target must make an Intelligence saving throw against your Spell Save DC. On a failed save, it takes **25d10 psychic damage**, falls Unconscious, and its mind becomes your thrall (as if under *Dominate Monster*) for as long as it remains Unconscious and for up to 1 year thereafter. Once per 24 hours, the thralled creature may repeat the saving throw (DC 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your Charisma modifier), ending the effect on a success.
*   **Rift to Oblivion:** (Once per Long Rest) You rip open a 100-foot cube into the outer dark. Creatures inside take **30d10 necrotic damage** and are banished to Elsaither's outer dark for **1 year** on a failed Constitution save (half damage and no banishment on success). A creature banished this way can be retrieved only by a *Wish* spell or divine intervention.

#### **Level 40: Divine Ascendance — Primordial God of the Shadow Realm (Ascendant Umbra-Zarta)**
At 40th level, you achieve ultimate deification:
*   **Pantheon Seat:** You ascend to the divine pantheon of Elsaither as the **Supreme Deity of the Umbral Weave, Darki, and the Shadow Realm** alongside Umbra and Zarta.
*   **Omnipresent Shadow:** You can teleport anywhere on the Material Plane or Shadow Realm as a Bonus Action.
*   **Untouchable Entity:** You are permanently Incorporeal. All attacks against you have Disadvantage, and you take half damage from all spells.
*   **Divine Immortality:** If destroyed, you dissolve into the Shadow Weave, reforming with full Hit Points at the next eclipse or nightfall.
*   **Cosmic Spell Reservoir:** You gain a second 9th-level spell slot.
