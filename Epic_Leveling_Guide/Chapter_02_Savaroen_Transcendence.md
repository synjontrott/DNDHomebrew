# **Chapter 2: The Savaroen — Part 2: Legendary/Godlike Transcendence (Levels 31–40)**

*Beyond the thirtieth circle of awakening lies a threshold that mortal minds were never engineered to touch. At Level 31, the Savaroen steps into the domain of living godhood. No matter which Paragon Path was chosen in Tier 5, any Savaroen can now choose one of two modular Godlike Transcendence Paths: the martial **Avatar of the Martial Godhead (Raeko’s Mantle)** or the arcane **Avatar of the Primordial Sovereign (Pireous’s Mantle)**. At Level 40, these paths culminate in true deification, granting the character an eternal seat in the pantheon of Silveronia alongside ancient legends such as Pireous, Zarta, and Raeko.*

---

## **The Legendary/Godlike Tier (Levels 31–40) Overview**

At Tier 6 (Levels 31–40), characters reach divine transcendence:
*   **Proficiency Bonus:**
    *   **Levels 31–32:** **+9**
    *   **Levels 33–36:** **+10**
    *   **Levels 37–40:** **+11**
*   **Hit Dice:** You continue gaining 1d8 (or 5) + your Constitution modifier in Hit Points per Savaroen level beyond 30th.
*   **Universal Ability Cap:** At 31st level, the maximum score for **all ability scores** increases to **30**.
*   **Modular Godlike Transcendence Selection:** At 31st level, choose one of two Godlike Paths:
    *   **Path 1: The Martial Godhead (Raeko’s Mantle)** — The divine perfection of physical destruction, world-severing swordplay, and unyielding celestial armor.
    *   **Path 2: The Primordial Sovereign (Pireous/Zarta’s Mantle)** — The divine dominion over the cosmos, boundless spellcasting, and planetary terraforming.
*   **11th-Level Primordial Arcanum:** At 39th level, all Savaroen unlock an **11th-level cosmic rite** capable of rewriting the reality of Silveronia.

## **Core Godlike Class Features**

### **Transcendent Surge**
*31st-Level Savaroen Feature*

Your connection to elemental creation ceases to require conscious invocation:
*   **Passive Empowerment:** Whenever you roll initiative, your *Elemental Surge* activates automatically and remains active for the duration of combat without requiring a Bonus Action or expending uses.
*   **Die Escalation:** The bonus damage increases to **2d8** of your primary elemental type.
*   **Total Saturation:** This extra 2d8 elemental damage applies to **all weapon attacks and damaging spell attacks** you make on each of your turns.
*   **Universal Ability Cap:** The maximum for all your ability scores increases to **30**.

---

### **Epic Ability Score Improvement / Epic Boon**
*32nd-, 36th-, and 40th-Level Savaroen Feature*

At 32nd level, 36th level, and again at 40th level, you can choose one of the following benefits:
*   Increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or two ability scores of your choice by 1 (to a maximum of 30).
*   Select one **Epic Boon Feat** from Chapter 3 of the *Campaign Guide* or the official D&D 2024 rules.

---

### **Omni-Resonance & Absolute Elemental Ward**
*33rd-Level Savaroen Feature*

Your spirit weave protects not just your own vessel, but your entire warband:
*   **Projected Ward:** When you gain Temporary Hit Points from *Spiritual Resonance*, all friendly creatures within **30 feet** of you also gain Temporary Hit Points equal to your **Charisma modifier + half your Savaroen level**.
*   **Absolute Immunity:** Your Immunity to your primary elemental damage type becomes absolute. No feature, divine artifact, or planar effect can bypass, pierce, downgrade, or treat your Immunity as Resistance.
*   **Elemental Siphon:** When targeted by an attack, spell, or effect of your primary elemental type, you absorb the raw energy, regaining an expended spell slot of 1st through 5th level (or regaining Hit Points equal to the prevented damage).

---

### **10th-Level Mastery**
*35th-Level Savaroen Feature*

You gain a **second 10th-level spell slot**. In addition, you can prepare and cast three additional legendary 10th-level Godlike Spells:

#### **Chrono-Elemental Warp**
*10th-Level Transmutation (Primordial) | Casting Time: 1 Action | Range: Self (60-ft radius) | Duration: 1 round*  
You rupture the local time stream with raw elemental acceleration. You immediately take **two additional turns** back-to-back following this turn. All other creatures and objects in the 60-foot radius remain frozen in stasis and are Incapacitated until your extra turns conclude. After these turns end, you suffer one level of Exhaustion.

#### **Primordial Rebirth**
*10th-Level Necromancy / Transmutation (Primordial) | Casting Time: 1 Reaction | Range: 60 feet | Duration: Instantaneous*  
When you or a creature within 60 feet drops to 0 Hit Points or is slain, a pillar of cosmic fire and crystal radiance descends. The target is instantly restored to life, cleansed of all negative conditions and exhaustion, and regains **all of its maximum Hit Points**. Hostile creatures within 30 feet take **10d10 radiant or elemental damage** (Constitution save halves).

#### **Planar Cleave**
*10th-Level Evocation / Conjuration (Primordial) | Casting Time: 1 Action | Range: Self (150-ft line, 10 ft wide) | Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute*  
You slash your weapon across the cosmos, opening a line of elemental oblivion. Each creature in the line must make a Dexterity saving throw against your Spell Save DC, taking **14d10 Force damage plus 14d10 elemental damage** and being Banished to an elemental demiplane on a failed save (half damage and no banishment on success). The rift deals **6d10 damage** to any creature entering it.

---

### **Absolute Elemental Dominion**
*37th-Level Savaroen Feature*

Your elemental command is absolute:
*   **Complete Bypass:** Whenever you deal your primary or secondary elemental damage through any spell, weapon attack, or feature, you **ignore all damage Resistances and Immunities**. The damage is dealt as full, unmitigated damage to every creature, god, or construct.
*   **Elemental Annihilation:** Whenever a hostile creature is reduced to 0 Hit Points by your elemental damage, it is instantly disintegrated into ash or pure crystal particulate.

---

### **11th-Level Primordial Arcanum**
*39th-Level Savaroen Feature*

You shatter the final cosmic barrier, unlocking an **11th-level Primordial Arcanum** usable **once per seven Long Rests**:

#### **The Elsaither Genesis Event**
*11th-Level Evocation / Transmutation (Primordial) | Casting Time: 10 minutes (or 1 Action in combat with 3 levels of Exhaustion) | Range: Self (5-mile radius) | Duration: Permanent / 24 hours*  
You summon the primordial forge-fires that birthed Silveronia, choosing one of the following world-altering outcomes:
1.  **Planar Terraforming:** Reshape an area within a 5-mile radius (raise mountains, solidify lava into crystal plains, cleanse all Blight, radiation, and planar corruption permanently).
2.  **Cosmic Annihilation:** Call down an orbital meteor storm striking up to 10 points. Each impact creates a 100-foot radius burst dealing **20d10 Bludgeoning damage plus 20d10 elemental damage** (Dexterity save halves).
3.  **Divine Resurgence:** Restore up to twenty deceased creatures within 1 mile to life with full Hit Points (*True Resurrection* with no material cost), granting all of them Immunity to non-magical damage for 24 hours.

## **Savaroen Modular Godlike Paths (Choose at Level 31)**

Regardless of which subclass or Paragon Path you chose earlier, select one of the following two Godlike Paths at Level 31:

```
SAVAROEN GODLIKE TRANSCENDENCE PATHS (LEVELS 31–40)
 ├── PATH 1: THE MARTIAL GODHEAD (Avatar of Raeko / Kren-Pireous)
 └── PATH 2: THE PRIMORDIAL SOVEREIGN (Avatar of Pireous / Zarta)
```

---

### **Path 1: The Martial Godhead (Raeko’s Mantle)**
*The Martial Godhead ascends as an unstoppable deity of warfare and blade-craft. Channeling the mantle of Raeko, the legendary warrior god of the Elsaither, this path transforms the Savaroen into a living divine weapon.*

#### **Level 31: God-King’s Armament**
*   **Fourfold Strike:** When you take the Attack Action on your turn, you can make **four melee weapon attacks** instead of three.
*   **God-Forged Reach:** Your melee weapon attacks gain a reach of **15 feet** as blades of raw compressed elemental plasma extend from your weapon.
*   **Armor of the War God:** While wearing armor or using Unarmored Defense, your AC gains a permanent **+3 bonus**.

#### **Level 34: Reality-Severing Strike**
*   **Critical Supremacy:** Your weapon attacks score a Critical Hit on a roll of **18–20**.
*   **Infinite Cut:** When you hit with a melee weapon attack, you can rip open space. The target takes an additional **6d10 Force damage**, and cannot take Reactions or cast spells with somatic components until the end of its next turn.
*   **Martial Invulnerability:** You are immune to the Blinded, Charmed, Frightened, Paralyzed, and Stunned conditions.

#### **Level 38: Sovereign God-Shatter**
*   **World-Cleaving Whirlwind:** (Once per Short or Long Rest) You make a melee weapon attack against **every hostile creature within 30 feet** of you. Each creature hit takes your weapon's full damage plus **15d10 Force damage**, is knocked Prone, and must succeed on a Constitution save against your Spell Save DC or be **Stunned** for 1 minute.
*   **Armor-Shatter:** Non-magical weapons that hit you are instantly destroyed upon contact, and magical weapons deal half damage.

#### **Level 40: Divine Ascendance — Living God of the Sacred Blade (Ascendant Raeko)**
At 40th level, you achieve ultimate deification:
*   **Pantheon Seat:** You ascend to the divine pantheon of Elsaither as a **Supreme Deity of War and the Blade**.
*   **Permanent Divine Weapon:** Your *Divine Elemental Weapon* is permanently active. It deals **4d10 + your Charisma modifier** elemental damage, cannot be disarmed, and ignores all magical shields or force effects.
*   **Aura of Absolute Command:** You emanate a 60-foot aura of divine supremacy. Enemies within the aura have Disadvantage on all attack rolls and saving throws. Friendly creatures have Advantage on all rolls and add your Charisma modifier to their weapon damage.
*   **Divine Immortality:** If you are destroyed, your spirit reconstitutes a new body at the sacred shrine of your chosen element within 1d10 days, accompanied by a cosmic planetary storm.
*   **Third 10th-Level Slot:** You gain a third 10th-level spell slot.

---

### **Path 2: The Primordial Sovereign (Pireous/Zarta’s Mantle)**
*The Primordial Sovereign ascends as the supreme arbiter of cosmic magic and elemental law. Channeling the divine thrones of Pireous and Zarta, this path commands the elemental foundations of Silveronia.*

#### **Level 31: Font of Cosmic Creation**
*   **Free Leveled Casting:** Whenever you cast a Savaroen spell of **1st through 4th level**, it does not consume a spell slot.
*   **Divine Spell DC:** The Spell Save DC for all your Savaroen spells and features increases by **+2**.
*   **Overwhelming Magic:** Hostile creatures cannot benefit from *Magic Resistance* (Advantage on saves against spells) when making saving throws against your spells.

#### **Level 34: Chrono-Weave Mastery**
*   **Dual Concentration:** You can maintain **Concentration on two Savaroen spells simultaneously**. When you take damage, you make a single Constitution saving throw to maintain both.
*   **Planar Counterspell:** You can cast *Counterspell* (at 6th level) at will without expending a spell slot.
*   **Weave Teleport:** You can teleport up to 60 feet as a Bonus Action on every turn.

#### **Level 38: Supernova Creation Cataclysm**
*   **Planar Overchannel:** Once per Long Rest, when you cast a Savaroen evocation spell of 1st through 8th level that deals elemental damage, the spell’s area of effect is **doubled**, all damage dice are **automatically maximized**, and any creature that fails its save suffers **Vulnerability** to that damage type for 1 minute.
*   **Font of Immortality:** You regenerate **20 Hit Points** at the start of each of your turns as long as you have at least 1 Hit Point.

#### **Level 40: Divine Ascendance — Primordial God of the Elsaither (Ascendant Pireous / Zarta)**
At 40th level, you achieve supreme deification:
*   **Pantheon Seat:** You ascend to the divine council of Elsaither as a **Supreme Deity of Magic and Elemental Creation**.
*   **Master of the Universe:** You gain permanent Truesight out to **300 feet**, and you can cast *Wish* once per Long Rest without expending a spell slot or suffering stress.
*   **Divine Biosphere Dominion:** You can alter the weather, climate, and geography of an entire planet over 24 hours of meditation.
*   **Divine Immortality:** You do not age and cannot die of natural causes. If slain, you reform at the core of the planet’s Crystalarium with full Hit Points at the next sunrise.
*   **Third 10th-Level Slot:** You gain a third 10th-level spell slot.
