# **Chapter 3: The Shadow Warrior — Part 1: Mythic/Paragon Ascendance (Levels 21–30)**

*To share one’s flesh with a Nyxid symbiote or to master the primordial shadow within is to walk a knife's edge between symbiosis and obliteration. Beyond the twentieth circle, that boundary ceases to be an uneasy truce. At Level 21, every Shadow Warrior—regardless of their initial archetype (Ravager, Weaver, Void-Arachnid, Mind-Eater, or Harbinger)—chooses one of two modular Epic Ascensions (Paragon Paths): the ferocious melee **Abyssal Devourer** or the eldritch, spell-weaving **Nether Conduit**. These paths give characters total freedom to specialize their mythic symbiosis while ensuring complete balance within the D&D 5.5e (2024 Revision) rules.*

---

## **The Mythic/Paragon Tier (Levels 21–30) Overview**

At Tier 5 (Levels 21–30), the Shadow Warrior’s symbiotic bond expands into mythic proportions:
*   **Proficiency Bonus:**
    *   **Levels 21–24:** **+7**
    *   **Levels 25–28:** **+8**
    *   **Levels 29–30:** **+9**
*   **Hit Dice:** You continue gaining 1d10 (or 6) + your Constitution modifier in Hit Points per Shadow Warrior level beyond 20th.
*   **Ability Score Limits:** At 21st level, the maximum score for your **Charisma**, **Dexterity**, and **Constitution** increases to **24** (and up to 26 via subsequent epic improvements).
*   **Modular Paragon Path Selection:** At 21st level, choose one of two Paragon Paths:
    *   **Path 1: The Abyssal Devourer (Apex Carnivore)** — Predatory melee brutality, rending natural weapons, blood-draining ferocity, and physical slaughter.
    *   **Path 2: The Nether Conduit (Umbral Void-Weaver)** — Eldritch void artillery, spatial tethers, shadow webbing, psionic mind-shattering, and planar rifts.
*   **Epic Half-Caster Magic:** The Shadow Warrior gains **6th-level spell slots at Level 23** and **7th-level spell slots at Level 27**.
*   **Dual Player Parity:** In games utilizing the **Dual Player Hero Character mode**, the synchronicity between Host and Symbiote evolves seamlessly, providing tactical parity and heightened dual action economy without disrupting 5.5e balance.

## **Core Paragon Class Features**

### **Paragon Symbiosis**
*21st-Level Shadow Warrior Feature*

Your physical anatomy fuses with the symbiote at a cellular and metaphysical level:
*   **Upgraded Natural Weapons:** The damage dice of your natural weapons increase:
    *   **Shadow Claw:** Deals **1d10 slashing damage** (Finesse).
    *   **Tendril Whip:** Deals **1d8 piercing damage** (Reach 15 feet).
*   **Paragon Necrotic Bonus:** The extra damage granted by *Improved Symbiosis* increases from 1d8 to **2d8 necrotic damage** whenever you hit with a melee weapon or natural weapon attack.
*   **Ability Score Expansion:** The maximum for your Charisma, Dexterity, and Constitution scores increases from 20 to **24**.

---

### **Dual Mind Overdrive & 5th Manifestation**
*22nd-Level Shadow Warrior Feature*

You learn a **5th Shadow Manifestation** chosen from the standard list in Chapter 2 or the [Mythic Shadow Manifestations](#mythic-shadow-manifestations) list below.
In addition:
*   **The Unbonded (Single Player):** You gain a **second Reaction** per round. This extra reaction can only be used for Shadow Warrior class features, manifestations, or Opportunity Attacks with your Natural Weapons.
*   **The Bonded (Dual Player):** When either Host or Symbiote succeeds on a saving throw against an effect targeting mental attributes (Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma), both players succeed. Furthermore, the Host and Symbiote can each concentrate on a separate spell simultaneously, provided their total spell levels do not exceed 8.

---

### **Epic Ability Score Improvement / Epic Boon**
*24th- and 28th-Level Shadow Warrior Feature*

At 24th level, and again at 28th level, you can choose one of the following benefits:
*   Increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or two ability scores of your choice by 1 (to a maximum of 26).
*   Select one **Epic Boon Feat** from Chapter 3 or official D&D 2024 rules (such as *Boon of the Perfect Host*, *Boon of the Void Weaver*, or *Boon of Dimensional Travel*).

---

### **Incorporeal Predator & Necrotic Piercing**
*25th-Level Shadow Warrior Feature*

Your shadow anatomy can phase through physical matter:
*   **Ghost-Walk:** You can move through other creatures and solid objects up to 10 feet thick as if they were difficult terrain. If you end your turn inside an object, you are shunted to the nearest unoccupied space and take 1d10 Force damage.
*   **Necrotic Piercing:** Necrotic damage dealt by your spells, features, and natural weapons ignores **Resistance**. Against creatures with **Immunity**, your attacks treat that immunity as **Resistance** instead of ignoring it completely.

---

### **Symbiotic Flurry**
*26th-Level Shadow Warrior Feature*

Your symbiote attacks independently with lethal speed:
*   Whenever you take the Attack Action on your turn, you can make **one additional attack** with your Shadow Claw or Tendril Whip as a **Bonus Action**.
*   Whenever you score a **Critical Hit** with a melee weapon or natural weapon, your symbiote erupts into a frenzied latch: the target takes an extra **3d8 necrotic damage**, and you can pull yourself up to 15 feet toward the target without provoking Opportunity Attacks.

---

### **6th Manifestation & Improved Symbiosis III**
*28th- and 29th-Level Shadow Warrior Feature*

*   At 28th level, you learn a **6th Shadow Manifestation**.
*   At 29th level, your *Improved Symbiosis* necrotic damage increases to **2d10 necrotic damage**.
*   **Vampiric Leech:** Whenever you deal necrotic damage with a melee attack or Shadow Warrior spell, you regain Hit Points equal to your **Proficiency Bonus**.

## **Mythic Shadow Manifestations**

Shadow Warriors of 21st level or higher can select from these Mythic Manifestations:

1.  **Abyssal Tendril Kraken:** As an Action, you unleash six shadowy tendrils that lash out at all enemies within 20 feet. Each hostile creature must make a Dexterity saving throw against your Spell Save DC, taking **6d8 necrotic damage**, being pulled 15 feet toward you, and being **Restrained** until the start of your next turn on a failed save (half damage and no pull/restrain on success). Usable a number of times equal to your Charisma modifier per Long Rest.
2.  **Void Phase Step:** When you take the Disengage or Dash action, you can instantly teleport up to **60 feet** to an unoccupied space in dim light or darkness, gaining Advantage on your next attack roll before the end of your turn.
3.  **Nether Carapace:** While not wearing armor, your AC equals **12 + your Dexterity modifier + your Charisma modifier**. You also gain Resistance to Cold, Acid, and Poison damage.
4.  **Parasitic Hive-Mind:** You gain telepathy out to **1 mile** with any creature you have touched within the last 24 hours, and can perceive through their senses as if using *Clairvoyance*.
5.  **Psychic Dread-Gaze:** As a Bonus Action, you fix your shadowy gaze on one creature within 60 feet. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw against your Spell Save DC or take **4d10 psychic damage** and be **Frightened** of you for 1 minute.
6.  **Symbiotic Regeneration:** At the start of each of your turns in combat, if you have at least 1 Hit Point and are below half your maximum Hit Points, you regain Hit Points equal to **5 + your Constitution modifier**.

## **Shadow Warrior Modular Paragon Paths (Choose at Level 21)**

Regardless of your subclass, choose one of the following two Epic Ascensions at Level 21:

```
SHADOW WARRIOR PARAGON PATHS (LEVELS 21–30)
 ├── PATH 1: THE ABYSSAL DEVOURER (Apex Melee Carnivore)
 └── PATH 2: THE NETHER CONDUIT (Umbral Void-Weaver)
```

---

### **Path 1: The Abyssal Devourer (Apex Carnivore)**
*The Abyssal Devourer turns the body into a terrifying apex predator. Symbiotic tissue hardens into razor-sharp chitin, tendrils rip through flesh, and every kill feeds the insatiable hunger of the host and entity.*

#### **Level 21: Claws of the Devourer**
*   **Predatory Weapons:** Your Shadow Claws deal **1d12 slashing damage**, and your Tendril Whip deals **1d10 piercing damage** with a reach of **20 feet**.
*   **Pouncing Stride:** Your walking speed increases by **15 feet**. When you move at least 20 feet straight toward a creature and hit it with a melee attack, it must succeed on a Strength saving throw against your Spell Save DC or be knocked Prone.
*   **Apex Rend:** Once per turn, when you hit a prone creature, your attack deals an extra **2d10 slashing damage**.

#### **Level 23: Blood Frenzy Apex**
*   **Savage Surge II:** Once per Short or Long Rest, you can make **two additional attacks** as a Bonus Action on your turn.
*   **Vampiric Devour:** When you reduce a hostile creature to 0 Hit Points, you gain Temporary Hit Points equal to your **Charisma score + twice your Shadow Warrior level**, and you can immediately use your Reaction to move up to your speed and make one melee attack with Advantage.

#### **Level 27: Ruin-Bringer Execution**
*   **Execution Strike:** When you score a critical hit with a melee weapon or natural weapon against a creature with fewer than 100 Hit Points, you can force it to make a Constitution saving throw against your Spell Save DC. On a failed save, it is instantly executed as its life essence is consumed. On a success, it takes an extra **10d10 necrotic damage**. (Usable twice per Long Rest).
*   **Chitin Armor:** You gain Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage from all sources while shifted or below half HP.

#### **Level 30: Paragon Capstone — Sovereign of the Abyssal Hunt**
At 30th level, you become the ultimate biological nightmare:
*   **Apex Form Expansion:** Your *Apex Predator* lasts for **10 minutes** and can be used **twice per Long Rest**.
*   **Wings of Chitin:** While in Apex Predator form, you gain a **flying speed of 60 feet (hover)**.
*   **Critical Hunger:** Your natural weapons score a Critical Hit on a roll of **18–20**.
*   **Terror-Rend:** Whenever you score a critical hit during Apex Predator, the target is **Blinded and Frightened** of you until the end of your next turn (no save).

---

### **Path 2: The Nether Conduit (Umbral Void-Weaver)**
*The Nether Conduit turns the shadow within into a planar gateway to the outer dark. By weaving void threads and firing beams of singularity energy, this path commands the battlefield from distance and manipulates spacetime.*

#### **Level 21: Conduit of the Void**
*   **Eldritch Void Beam:** You learn *Eldritch Blast*. It counts as a Shadow Warrior cantrip for you, fires 4 beams, and you add your **Charisma modifier** to each beam's damage roll.
*   **At-Will Webbing:** You can cast *Web* and *Misty Step* at will without expending spell slots.
*   **Void Sight:** You can see normally through magical darkness out to **120 feet**.

#### **Level 23: Web of Shared Agony & Synaptic Overload**
*   **Tether Matrix:** You can maintain up to **three active shadow tethers** simultaneously. When you take damage, you can use your Reaction to split the damage, reflecting half of it onto a tethered enemy as Force damage.
*   **Psionic Synapse:** When you hit with a ranged spell or weapon attack, you can deal an extra **3d8 psychic damage** and prevent the target from taking Reactions until the start of its next turn.

#### **Level 27: Dimensional Apocalypse & Soul Rewind**
*   **Shadow Apocalypse:** As an Action (twice per Long Rest), you unleash a 60-foot cone of void energy. Enemies must make a Constitution save, taking **16d6 necrotic damage plus 8d6 Force damage**, being knocked Prone, and Blinded for 1 minute on a failure (half damage on success).
*   **Fate-Weaver’s Rewind:** Once per Long Rest, when an ally within 30 feet of you drops to 0 Hit Points, you can use your Reaction to snap a void thread: the ally drops to 1 Hit Point instead, teleports to your side, and gains **50 Temporary Hit Points**.

#### **Level 30: Paragon Capstone — Sovereign of the Void Singularity**
At 30th level, you become a living gate to the outer dark:
*   **Apex Conduit:** While in *Apex Predator* form, hostile creatures within 60 feet have **Disadvantage on saving throws** against your spells and abilities.
*   **Singularity Step:** You can teleport up to 60 feet as a Bonus Action on every turn.
*   **Void Banishment:** Once per turn when you hit a creature with an Eldritch Blast beam or Tendril Whip, you can force it to make a Charisma saving throw against your Spell Save DC. On a failed save, it is **Banished to the Shadow Realm** until the end of your next turn.
