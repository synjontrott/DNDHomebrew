# **Chapter 6: The Beast Master — Part 2: Legendary/Godlike Transcendence (Levels 31–40)**

*At the final zenith of primal power, the Beast Master is no longer a warden of nature—they are Nature Incarnate. The ancient forests, continental fault lines, sky streams, and volcanic rifts of Silveronia answer their call. At Level 31, every Beast Master—regardless of which Mark or Paragon Path they walked in Tier 5—chooses one of two modular Godlike Transcendence Paths: the titanic **World-Beast Sovereign (Lord of the Kaiju)** or the omnipresent **Primal Deity (Avatar of Nature Incarnate)**. At Level 40, these paths culminate in true deification, granting the character an eternal seat in the pantheon of Silveronia alongside ancient nature gods.*

---

## **The Legendary/Godlike Tier (Levels 31–40) Overview**

At Tier 6 (Levels 31–40), the Beast Master achieves divine ascension:
*   **Proficiency Bonus:**
    *   **Levels 31–32:** **+9**
    *   **Levels 33–36:** **+10**
    *   **Levels 37–40:** **+11**
*   **Hit Dice:** You continue gaining 1d8 (or 5) + your Constitution modifier in Hit Points per Beast Master level beyond 30th.
*   **Universal Ability Cap:** At 31st level, the maximum score for **all ability scores** increases to **30**.
*   **Modular Godlike Transcendence Selection:** At 31st level, choose one of two Godlike Paths:
    *   **Path 1: The World-Beast Sovereign (Lord of the Kaiju)** — Pillar I transcendence; your Spirit Companion becomes an immortal Gargantuan World-Beast with legendary actions, crushing fortresses, and wrestling ancient dragons.
    *   **Path 2: The Primal Deity (Avatar of Nature Incarnate)** — Pillar II transcendence; the master permanently fuses with the primal cradle itself, gaining passive multi-aspect omniscience, biosphere command, and divine shapeshifting.
*   **Primal Energy (PE) Reservoir:** Your PE pool expands to **55+ points**, allowing virtually unlimited deployment of enhanced strikes and primal arts.
*   **Primal Arcanum Culmination:** The Beast Master gains access to an **8th-level Primal Arcanum at Level 35** and a **9th-level Primal Arcanum at Level 39** (each usable once per Long Rest).

## **Core Godlike Class Features**

### **Epic Ability Score Improvement / Epic Boon**
*32nd-, 36th-, and 40th-Level Beast Master Feature*

At 32nd level, 36th level, and again at 40th level, you can choose one of the following benefits:
*   Increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or two ability scores of your choice by 1 (to a maximum of 30).
*   Select one **Epic Boon Feat** from Chapter 3 or official D&D 2024 rules.

---

### **Eternal Aspect (Omni-Aspect Passive)**
*33rd-Level Beast Master Feature*

Your physical body permanently integrates the primal genome:
*   **All Six Active:** You no longer need to spend PE or take an action to activate Aspect Mutations. **All six Aspect Mutations of your Mark of the Wild are now permanently and simultaneously active** on your body at all times.
*   **No Concentration or Duration:** These mutations never expire, cannot be dispelled, and function passively. You gain all of their sensory enhancements, movement modes, damage bonuses, and special reactions continuously.

---

### **8th-Level Primal Arcanum & Twin Apex Synthesis**
*35th-Level Beast Master Feature*

You gain access to an **8th-level Primal Arcanum** chosen from the Savaroen or Druid spell list (such as *Earthquake*, *Animal Shapes*, *Sunburst*, or *Control Weather*). You can cast this spell once per Long Rest without expending a spell slot.
In addition:
*   **Shared God-Form:** When you enter an *Apex Aspect Transformation*, your companion also enters the Apex transformation, gaining your fly/leap speeds, damage resistances, attack bonuses, and critical threat range for the duration.

---

### **Primal Sovereign Dominion**
*37th-Level Beast Master Feature*

All mortal beasts and elemental spirits recognize you as their divine sovereign:
*   **Planar Telepathy:** You can communicate telepathically with any beast, monstrosity, or plant creature within **5 miles** of you.
*   **Command the Wild:** As an Action, you can force any beast or monstrosity within 120 feet to make a Wisdom saving throw against your Spell Save DC. On a failed save, the creature is charmed by you for 24 hours and follows your verbal or telepathic commands to the letter.
*   **Companion Chitin:** Your spirit companion gains permanent Immunity to non-magical Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage, and cannot be Charmed, Frightened, Paralyzed, Poisoned, Petrified, or Stunned.

---

### **9th-Level Primal Arcanum**
*39th-Level Beast Master Feature*

You unlock the apex tier of primal magic, gaining a **9th-level Primal Arcanum** chosen from the Savaroen or Druid spell list (such as *Shapechange*, *Storm of Vengeance*, *Foresight*, or *True Resurrection*). You can cast this spell once per Long Rest without expending a spell slot.

---

## **Beast Master Modular Godlike Paths (Choose at Level 31)**

Regardless of which Mark or Paragon Path you chose earlier, select one of the following two Godlike Paths at Level 31:

```
BEAST MASTER GODLIKE TRANSCENDENCE PATHS (LEVELS 31–40)
 ├── PATH 1: THE WORLD-BEAST SOVEREIGN (Mantle of Terra & Mundus / Lord of Titans)
 └── PATH 2: THE PRIMAL DEITY (Mantle of Anastasia / Avatar of Nature Incarnate)
```

---

### **Path 1: The World-Beast Sovereign (Mantle of Terra & Mundus)**
*The World-Beast Sovereign elevates the Spirit Companion to the status of a cosmic earth-titan, channeling the mantle of Terra (Spiritess of Earth and Living Crust) and Mundus (Spirit of Geological Harmony). The companion grows to gargantuan size, commands three legendary actions per round, and battles divine avatars on equal footing.*

#### **Level 31: Gargantuan Kaiju Evolution**
*   **Kaiju Size:** Your companion can manifest as **Gargantuan** (occupying a 20-by-20-foot space or larger) with a melee reach of **15 feet**.
*   **Siege Monster:** Your companion deals double damage to objects, buildings, and siege structures.
*   **3 Legendary Actions:** Your companion gains **3 Legendary Actions** per round (regained at the start of its turn):
    *   **Primal Strike (Costs 1 Action):** The companion makes one melee attack.
    *   **Trampling Stride (Costs 1 Action):** The companion moves up to half its walking speed. Creatures in its path must make a Strength save or take **3d10 Bludgeoning damage** and fall Prone.
    *   **Cataclysmic Roar (Costs 2 Actions):** An elemental roar in a 30-foot cone deals **8d10 elemental damage** (Constitution save halves).

#### **Level 34: Kaiju Armor & Tectonic Resistance**
*   **Titan Chitin:** Your companion's Armor Class increases by **+3**.
*   **Universal Resistance:** Your companion gains Resistance to **all damage types** except Force and Psychic damage.
*   **Titan HP Pool:** Your companion's maximum Hit Points increase by **100**.

#### **Level 38: Continental Fracture Slam**
*   **World-Quake:** As an Action (once per Long Rest), your companion slams the ground, causing a tectonic rupture in a **120-foot radius**. The ground shatters into abyssal chasms. Hostile creatures must make a Dexterity save against your Spell Save DC, taking **15d10 Bludgeoning plus 5d10 elemental damage** on a failed save (half on success), falling Prone, and being Restrained by collapsing stone until the end of their next turn.

#### **Level 40: Divine Ascendance — Living God of the World-Beasts (Ascendant Terra-Mundus)**
At 40th level, you and your companion achieve ultimate deification:
*   **Pantheon Seat:** You and your companion ascend to the divine pantheon of Elsaither as the **Supreme Dual Deities of Beasts, Titans, and Monsters** alongside Terra and Mundus.
*   **Triple Legendary Resistance:** You and your companion each gain **3 uses of Legendary Resistance** per Long Rest.
*   **Immortal World-Spirit:** If your companion is slain, its physical form dissolves into pure elemental earth, reforming at full Hit Points at the next sunrise.
*   **Aura of the Apex Titan:** You emanate a 60-foot aura of primal majesty. All allies within the aura gain **30 Temporary Hit Points** each turn and add your Wisdom modifier to their attack and damage rolls.

---

### **Path 2: The Primal Deity (Mantle of Anastasia)**
*The Primal Deity fuses the Beast Master's mortal essence directly into the living biosphere of Silveronia, channeling the sacred vitality of Anastasia (Spiritess of Life and Wild Resurrection). With total mastery over shapeshifting, climate, and primal rejuvenation, this path becomes an undying god of the wilderness.*

#### **Level 31: Divine Primal Physiology**
*   **Permanent Apex Divinity:** Your *Apex Aspect Transformation* is **permanently active** at 0 PE cost. You never need to activate it; you constantly embody the supreme archetype of your Mark.
*   **Chimeric Armor:** While not wearing heavy armor, your AC gains a permanent **+3 bonus**.
*   **Immunity to Conditions:** You are permanently Immune to the Poisoned, Petrified, Paralyzed, Charmed, and Frightened conditions.

#### **Level 34: Sovereign of the Living Biosphere**
*   **Universal Shapeshifting:** You can cast *Shapechange* once per Short or Long Rest without expending a spell slot, retaining all your class features while in any beast or dragon form.
*   **Primal Strike Piercing:** All your weapon and natural attacks completely ignore damage Resistances and Immunities, dealing full unmitigated elemental damage.
*   **Climate Authority:** You can alter the weather and climate within a 10-mile radius at will.

#### **Level 38: Supernova Primal Cataclysm**
*   **Flock of the First Dawn:** As an Action (once per Long Rest), you summon a celestial wave of primal spirits across a 150-foot radius. Hostile creatures must make a Charisma save against your Spell Save DC, taking **20d10 radiant or elemental damage** and being rendered docile (unable to attack for 1 minute) on a failed save (half damage and no condition on success). Simultaneously, all allies in the area regain **60 Hit Points**, are cured of all conditions, and gain **30 Temporary Hit Points**.

#### **Level 40: Divine Ascendance — Primordial God of the Great Wilds**
At 40th level, you achieve ultimate deification:
*   **Pantheon Seat:** You ascend to the divine council of Elsaither as the **Supreme Deity of the Primal Wilds and Natural Creation**.
*   **Divine Immortality:** You do not age and cannot die of natural causes. If slain, your spirit returns to the heart of Elsaither’s primal wild, reforming completely at the dawn of the next day with full Hit Points at any natural spring or sacred grove.
*   **Master of Life:** You can cast *True Resurrection* and *Power Word Heal* each once per Long Rest without expending spell slots or requiring material components.
*   **Triple Legendary Resistance:** You gain **3 uses of Legendary Resistance** per Long Rest.
