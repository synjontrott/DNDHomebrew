# **Chapter 5: The Beast Master — Part 1: Mythic/Paragon Ascendance (Levels 21–30)**

*In the untamed primal cradles of Elsaither—from the bioluminescent canopies of the Plunesako deep-forests to the volcanic roosts of the Dragonkin mountains—the Beast Master does not merely lead wild beasts. They are the apex heartbeat of the ecosystem itself. Beyond the twentieth circle of primal mastery, every Beast Master—regardless of their initial Mark of the Wild (Wolf, Dragon, Bear, Owl, Eagle, or Sheep)—chooses one of two modular Epic Ascensions (Paragon Paths): the companion-focused **Behemoth Master (Pack Sovereign)** or the shapeshifting, mutation-focused **Primordial Chimera (Aspect Sovereign)**. These paths allow characters to tailor their mythic evolution while preserving flawless balance within the D&D 5.5e (2024 Revision) rules.*

---

## **The Mythic/Paragon Tier (Levels 21–30) Overview**

At Tier 5 (Levels 21–30), the Beast Master ascends to mythic authority:
*   **Proficiency Bonus:**
    *   **Levels 21–24:** **+7**
    *   **Levels 25–28:** **+8**
    *   **Levels 29–30:** **+9**
*   **Hit Dice:** You continue gaining 1d8 (or 5) + your Constitution modifier in Hit Points per Beast Master level beyond 20th.
*   **Ability Score Limits:** At 21st level, the maximum score for your **Wisdom**, **Dexterity**, and **Constitution** increases to **24** (and up to 26 via subsequent epic improvements).
*   **Modular Paragon Path Selection:** At 21st level, choose one of two Paragon Paths:
    *   **Path 1: The Behemoth Master (Pack Sovereign)** — Pillar I specialization; elevating your Spirit Companion into a Huge mythic juggernaut with legendary reactions, shared pack stride, and devastating multiattacks.
    *   **Path 2: The Primordial Chimera (Aspect Sovereign)** — Pillar II specialization; physical primal mutations, simultaneous multi-aspect blending, effortless apex transformations, and personal melee dominance.
*   **Primal Energy (PE) Scaling:** Your Primal Engine pool scales to **Level + Wisdom modifier + Proficiency Bonus**, giving Paragon Beast Masters an expansive reservoir of 40+ PE.
*   **Primal Arcanum Expansion:** Breaking the mortal 5th-level ceiling, the Beast Master unlocks a **6th-level Primal Arcanum at Level 23** and a **7th-level Primal Arcanum at Level 27** (each usable once per Long Rest).

## **Core Paragon Class Features**

### **Dual Aspect Synthesis**
*22nd-Level Beast Master Feature*

Your bodily mastery over primal mutations deepens:
*   **Aspect Harmonization:** You can have **two different Aspect Mutations** active on your body simultaneously. Activating the second mutation while one is already active costs only **0 PE**, allowing you to combine two animal traits for a single 1 PE investment.
*   **Extended Mutation:** The duration of your Aspect Mutations increases from 10 minutes to **1 hour**.
*   **Primal Energy Reservoir:** Your PE pool calculation updates to **Level + Wisdom modifier + Proficiency Bonus**.

---

### **6th-Level Primal Arcanum**
*23rd-Level Beast Master Feature*

You gain access to a **6th-level Primal Arcanum** chosen from the Savaroen or Druid spell list (such as *Heal*, *Sunbeam*, *Wall of Thorns*, or *Primordial Ward*). You can cast this spell once per Long Rest without expending a spell slot.

---

### **Epic Ability Score Improvement / Epic Boon**
*24th- and 28th-Level Beast Master Feature*

At 24th level, and again at 28th level, you can choose one of the following benefits:
*   Increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or two ability scores of your choice by 1 (to a maximum of 26).
*   Select one **Epic Boon Feat** from Chapter 3 or official D&D 2024 rules (such as *Epic Boon of the Wild*, *Boon of the First Elementals*, or *Boon of Combat Prowess*).

---

### **Apex Fusion & Elemental Resilience**
*25th-Level Beast Master Feature*

While in an **Apex Aspect Transformation**:
*   Your weapon and unarmed attacks score a critical hit on a roll of **19–20**.
*   You gain Resistance to all damage types channeled by your Mark's allied spirits.
*   Your movement speed increases by an additional **15 feet**.

---

### **Twin Pack Resonance**
*26th-Level Beast Master Feature*

The life force connecting you and your companion is unbreakable:
*   **Damage Sharing:** Whenever you or your companion takes damage, the other can use a **Reaction** to take half of that damage instead, reducing the original target's damage by half. This shared damage cannot be reduced further.
*   **Pack Stride:** Both you and your companion gain a **+10-foot bonus** to your walking, climbing, and swimming speeds.
*   **Unified Strike:** Once per turn, when you hit a creature with an attack, your companion gains Advantage on its next attack roll against that target before the end of the round.

---

### **7th-Level Primal Arcanum**
*27th-Level Beast Master Feature*

You gain access to a **7th-level Primal Arcanum** chosen from the Savaroen or Druid spell list (such as *Regenerate*, *Fire Storm*, *Draconic Transformation*, or *Whirlwind*). You can cast this spell once per Long Rest without expending a spell slot.

---

### **Colossus Aspect & Legendary Resilience**
*29th-Level Beast Master Feature*

*   Your companion’s maximum Hit Points permanently increase by **50**, and it gains a **second use of Legendary Resistance** per Long Rest.
*   **Colossus Apex:** The duration of your *Apex Aspect Transformation* increases from 1 minute to **1 hour**.

---

## **Beast Master Modular Paragon Paths (Choose at Level 21)**

Regardless of your Mark of the Wild, choose one of the following two Epic Ascensions at Level 21:

```
BEAST MASTER PARAGON PATHS (LEVELS 21–30)
 ├── PATH 1: THE BEHEMOTH MASTER (Pack Sovereign / Companion Focus)
 └── PATH 2: THE PRIMORDIAL CHIMERA (Aspect Sovereign / Mutation Focus)
```

---

### **Path 1: The Behemoth Master (Pack Sovereign)**
*The Behemoth Master focuses on Pillar I, forging their Spirit Companion into a mythic titan. The companion grows to massive scale, gains independent tactical reactions, and fights as an unstoppable partner.*

#### **Level 21: Huge Mythic Evolution**
*   **Colossal Scale:** Whenever you summon your companion or finish a rest, you can set its size to Medium, Large, or **Huge**.
*   **Titanic Reach:** Your companion's melee reach increases to **10 feet**.
*   **Mythic Damage Die:** All attack dice of your companion gain one additional die (e.g. 1d8 becomes 2d8, 2d6 becomes 3d6).
*   **Legendary Resistance (1/LR):** If your companion fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

#### **Level 23: Coordinated Pack Slaughter**
*   **Coordinated Flank:** When you take the Attack Action, your companion can use its Reaction to make a melee attack with Advantage.
*   **Trampling Charge:** If your companion moves at least 20 feet straight toward a target and hits with an attack, the target takes an extra **3d10 Bludgeoning damage** and is knocked **Prone** (Strength save negates prone).
*   **Companion Vitality:** Your companion gains Temporary Hit Points equal to **three times your Beast Master level** on every Short or Long Rest.

#### **Level 27: Primal Behemoth Roar**
*   **Cataclysmic Roar:** (Once per Short or Long Rest) Your companion releases an elemental roar in a 60-foot cone. Enemies must make a Constitution save against your Spell Save DC, taking **10d10 damage** of your Primal Bond element, being knocked Prone, and **Stunned** until the end of your next turn (half damage on success).
*   **Unstoppable Pack:** You and your companion cannot be moved, knocked Prone, or restrained against your will.

#### **Level 30: Paragon Capstone — Sovereign of the Mythic Pack**
At 30th level, your companion achieves mythic perfection:
*   **Legendary Companion Actions:** Your companion gains **2 Legendary Actions** per round (costs 1 action to make a strike, 1 action to move half speed without provoking opportunity attacks).
*   **Instant Resurgence:** If your companion is reduced to 0 Hit Points, you can use your **Reaction** to restore it to life at half its maximum Hit Points within 30 feet (once per Short or Long Rest).
*   **Primal Truesight:** You and your companion both gain **Truesight out to 60 feet** and Advantage on all saving throws against magical effects.

---

### **Path 2: The Primordial Chimera (Aspect Sovereign)**
*The Primordial Chimera focuses on Pillar II, turning the Beast Master's own body into an avatar of wild mutations. Chimeras seamlessly weave multiple animal traits simultaneously, dominating combat with enhanced claws, flight, and primal armor.*

#### **Level 21: Chimeric Synthesis**
*   **Triple Aspect Harmonization:** You can have **three different Aspect Mutations** active simultaneously on your body at 0 extra PE cost.
*   **Primal Claws & Fangs:** Your unarmed strikes deal **1d10 slashing or piercing damage**, count as magical, and use your Wisdom modifier for attack and damage rolls.
*   **Apex Agility:** You gain a climbing and swimming speed equal to your walking speed, and your jump distance is tripled.

#### **Level 23: Multi-Aspect Overdrive**
*   **Critical Predator:** Your weapon and natural attacks score a Critical Hit on a roll of **19–20**.
*   **Elemental Infusion:** Whenever you hit with an attack while in an Aspect Mutation, your strike deals an extra **2d8 damage** of your Primal Bond elemental type.
*   **Predatory Reflexes:** You can take the Dash or Disengage action as a Bonus Action on each of your turns.

#### **Level 27: Effortless Apex Transformation**
*   **Free Apex Form:** Entering your *Apex Aspect Transformation* costs **0 PE**, lasts for **1 hour**, and can be used at will.
*   **Chimera Breath Weapon:** While in Apex form, you can exhale a 60-foot cone of elemental breath as an Action (twice per Short or Long Rest), dealing **10d10 elemental damage** (Dexterity save halves).
*   **Primal Armor:** While not wearing heavy armor, your AC gains a **+2 bonus**, and you have Resistance to all non-magical physical damage.

#### **Level 30: Paragon Capstone — Sovereign of the Chimera Throne**
At 30th level, you become the living avatar of Elsaither's primal beasts:
*   **Omni-Aspect Presence:** **All six Aspect Mutations of your Mark are permanently active** on your body at all times without expending PE or requiring concentration.
*   **Apex God-Form:** While in Apex Transformation, your strikes critically hit on a roll of **18–20**, and you gain **Immunity** to your Primal Bond damage type.
*   **Primal Regeneration:** You regain Hit Points equal to **10 + your Wisdom modifier** at the start of each of your turns as long as you have at least 1 Hit Point.
