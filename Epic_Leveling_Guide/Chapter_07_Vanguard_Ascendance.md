# **Chapter 7: The Vanguard — Part 1: Mythic/Paragon Ascendance (Levels 21–30)**

*Where other champions invoke spells or beseech deities, the Vanguard tears open the elemental core of their own flesh. In the crucible of Dakvura (The Shift), their bones harden into titanium alloys, their blood ignites into roaring magma, and their muscles weave with pure elemental plasma. Beyond the twentieth circle, every Vanguard—regardless of which of the 16 Elemental Disciplines they practice—chooses one of two modular Epic Ascensions (Paragon Paths): the immovable **Unstoppable Juggernaut (Living Bastion)** or the devastating **Cataclysmic Ravager (Primordial Breaker)**. These paths allow characters to tailor their martial supremacy while remaining strictly balanced within the D&D 5.5e (2024 Revision) rules.*

---

## **The Mythic/Paragon Tier (Levels 21–30) Overview**

At Tier 5 (Levels 21–30), the Vanguard’s martial dominance achieves mythic status:
*   **Proficiency Bonus:**
    *   **Levels 21–24:** **+7**
    *   **Levels 25–28:** **+8**
    *   **Levels 29–30:** **+9**
*   **Hit Dice:** You continue gaining 1d12 (or 7) + your Constitution modifier in Hit Points per Vanguard level beyond 20th.
*   **Ability Score Limits:** At 21st level, the maximum score for your **Strength** and **Constitution** increases to **24** (and up to 26 via subsequent epic improvements).
*   **Modular Paragon Path Selection:** At 21st level, choose one of two Paragon Paths:
    *   **Path 1: The Unstoppable Juggernaut (Living Bastion)** — Immovable defense, intercepting lethal blows for allies, kinetic reflection, and condition invulnerability.
    *   **Path 2: The Cataclysmic Ravager (Primordial Breaker)** — World-shattering offensive brutality, expanded critical hits, seismic shockwaves, and siege destruction.
*   **Dakvura Availability:** Having unlocked *Abyssal Executioner* at 20th level, your Dakvura uses remain **Unlimited**.
*   **Martial Attack Scaling:** At 21st level, the Vanguard gains **Extra Attack (2)**, allowing them to attack three times when taking the Attack action.
*   **Elemental Arts Scaling:** Your pool of Elemental Arts scales from 10 to **16 uses**, recovering an amount equal to your **Proficiency Bonus** on a Short Rest, and all uses on a Long Rest.

## **Core Paragon Class Features**

### **Extra Attack (2) & Paragon Dakvura**
*21st-Level Vanguard Feature*

Your martial speed and weapon strikes match the greatest fighters in history:
*   **Three Attacks:** You can attack **three times**, instead of twice, whenever you take the Attack Action on your turn.
*   **Paragon Damage Die:** The enhanced weapon damage bonus granted while in Dakvura increases from 1d10 to **1d12** of your attack's damage type.
*   **Ability Score Expansion:** The maximum for your Strength and Constitution scores increases from 20 to **24**.

---

### **Mythic Elemental Arts (Tier II Arts)**
*22nd-Level Vanguard Feature*

You unlock advanced mastery over your internal elemental reservoir, gaining access to **Tier II Mythic Arts** (each costing **2 Elemental Art uses**):

*   **Cataclysmic Shockwave (Costs 2 Arts):** As an Action while in Dakvura, you strike the ground. A shockwave erupts in a 30-foot cone. Each creature must make a Dexterity save against your Art Save DC (`8 + PB + CON mod`), taking **8d10 damage** of your Discipline's elemental type, being pushed 20 feet away, and knocked Prone on a failure (half damage on success).
*   **Primordial Counter-Burst (Costs 2 Arts):** When hit by a melee attack while in Dakvura, you use your Reaction to detonate elemental armor. The attacker takes **6d10 elemental damage** and must succeed on a Constitution save or be **Stunned** until the start of your next turn.
*   **Planar Rend Strike (Costs 2 Arts):** When you hit with a melee attack while in Dakvura, the strike deals an extra **6d10 elemental damage**, and the target cannot regain Hit Points or teleport until the end of your next turn.

---

### **Epic Ability Score Improvement / Epic Boon**
*24th- and 28th-Level Vanguard Feature*

At 24th level, and again at 28th level, you can choose one of the following benefits:
*   Increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or two ability scores of your choice by 1 (to a maximum of 26).
*   Select one **Epic Boon Feat** from Chapter 3 or official D&D 2024 rules (such as *Boon of Irresistible Offense*, *Boon of Fortitude*, or *Boon of the First Elementals*).

---

### **Indomitable Juggernaut**
*25th-Level Vanguard Feature*

While shifted in Dakvura:
*   You cannot be moved, pushed, knocked Prone, Grappled, Restrained, or Paralyzed against your will.
*   You gain **Advantage on all saving throws** while in Dakvura.
*   Difficult terrain does not impede your movement, and you can charge through solid non-magical structures up to 5 feet thick, shattering them upon impact.

---

### **Unstoppable Cleave**
*26th-Level Vanguard Feature*

*   *Ruthless Onslaught* now triggers whenever you score a **Critical Hit**, in addition to when you reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points.
*   When Ruthless Onslaught triggers, you can make the extra melee weapon attack against any target within your reach, dealing additional elemental damage equal to your **Constitution modifier + your Proficiency Bonus**.

---

### **Primordial Surge & Recovery**
*27th-Level Vanguard Feature*

*   The enhanced weapon damage bonus granted while in Dakvura increases to **2d8**.
*   When you finish a Short Rest, you now regain a number of expended Elemental Arts equal to your **full Proficiency Bonus** (instead of half).

---

### **Titan's Aegis (Reflective Intercept)**
*29th-Level Vanguard Feature*

*   **Extended Intercept:** When an ally within **30 feet** of you is targeted by an attack, spell, or breath weapon, you can use your **Reaction** to move up to your speed to an unoccupied space adjacent to that ally and take the effect in their place.
*   **Reflective Deflection:** You have Resistance to the incoming damage from that intercepted attack. Furthermore, you reflect **half of the total incoming damage** directly back at the attacker as damage of your Discipline's elemental type (no save required).

## **Vanguard Modular Paragon Paths (Choose at Level 21)**

Regardless of which of the 16 Elemental Disciplines you practice, choose one of the following two Epic Ascensions at Level 21:

```
VANGUARD PARAGON PATHS (LEVELS 21–30)
 ├── PATH 1: THE UNSTOPPABLE JUGGERNAUT (Living Bastion / Defensive Intercept)
 └── PATH 2: THE CATACLYSMIC RAVAGER (Primordial Breaker / Offensive Slaughter)
```

---

### **Path 1: The Unstoppable Juggernaut (Living Bastion)**
*The Unstoppable Juggernaut turns the Vanguard into an immovable mountain of living element. Specialized in absorbing punishment, protecting companions, and deflecting dragon strikes, this path anchors the frontline against divine armies.*

#### **Level 21: Bastion’s Iron Stance**
*   **Reinforced Chassis:** Your Armor Class gains a permanent **+2 bonus**.
*   **Kinetic Anchor:** You cannot be moved or displaced by any means, and you can reduce any incoming physical damage by your **Constitution modifier** (applied before resistance).
*   **Taunting Presence:** When you hit a creature with a melee attack, it has Disadvantage on attack rolls against any target other than you until the start of your next turn.

#### **Level 23: Continental Resilience**
*   **Expanded Resistance:** While in Dakvura, you gain Resistance to **all damage types** except Force and Psychic damage.
*   **Retaliatory Spikes:** Whenever a creature within 5 feet hits you with a melee attack, it takes damage equal to your **Constitution modifier + your Proficiency Bonus** of your Discipline’s elemental type (no save required).

#### **Level 27: Bulwark of the First World**
*   **Aegis Wall:** As a Bonus Action, you can project a 15-foot aura of protective elemental force. Friendly creatures within the aura gain **Resistance to all damage** and cannot be knocked Prone.
*   **Unyielding Stance:** You can use your *Titan’s Aegis* reaction twice per round instead of once.

#### **Level 30: Paragon Capstone — Colossus of the Living Bastion**
At 30th level, you become the living bedrock of Elsaither:
*   **Huge Bastion Shift:** Whenever you enter Dakvura, you can choose to grow up to **Huge** size. While Huge, your reach increases by **10 feet**, and your weapon attacks deal an extra **1d12 Bludgeoning damage**.
*   **Titan Temporary HP:** When you roll initiative, you instantly gain **60 Temporary Hit Points**.
*   **Absolute Anchor:** While you have at least 1 HP, you are completely Immune to all conditions except Incapacitated.

---

### **Path 2: The Cataclysmic Ravager (Primordial Breaker)**
*The Cataclysmic Ravager embodies the explosive, offensive wrath of Dakvura. Designed to cleave through battalions, shatter castles, and execute titan-sized monsters, this path turns every weapon swing into a natural disaster.*

#### **Level 21: Breaker’s Onslaught**
*   **Siege Striker:** Your melee weapon attacks deal double damage to objects, buildings, and constructs.
*   **Critical Threshold:** Your melee weapon attacks score a Critical Hit on a roll of **19–20**.
*   **Kinetic Impact:** Whenever you hit with a weapon attack, the target is pushed 10 feet directly away from you.

#### **Level 23: World-Ender Cleave**
*   **Rupture Strike:** When you score a critical hit, all enemies within 10 feet of the target take damage equal to your **full weapon damage roll** as your elemental damage type.
*   **Feral Overdrive:** When you reduce a creature to 0 HP, you regain **1 expended Elemental Art** and your walking speed increases by 20 feet until the end of your turn.

#### **Level 27: Primordial Calamity Arts**
*   **Empowered Arts:** The damage of your Tier I and Tier II Elemental Arts increases by an additional **4d10 elemental damage**.
*   **Relentless Ravager:** When you take the Attack Action, you can choose to make one additional attack (totaling 4 attacks per Action) by expending 1 Elemental Art.

#### **Level 30: Paragon Capstone — Colossus of Cataclysm**
At 30th level, your strikes register on seismic scales across Silveronia:
*   **Huge Calamity Shift:** Entering Dakvura can grant **Huge** size, increasing weapon reach by **10 feet** and adding **1d12 elemental damage** to all strikes.
*   **Armor-Sundering Cleave:** Your weapon attacks completely ignore all armor, shields, and damage resistances.
*   **Maximized Criticals:** Whenever you score a critical hit, **all damage dice for the strike are automatically maximized**.
