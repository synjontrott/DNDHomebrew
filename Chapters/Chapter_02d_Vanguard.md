# **Chapter 2d: The Vanguard**

*Warriors who have shattered the boundary between body and element, Vanguards do not cast spells—they become them. Through the terrifying, all-consuming power of Dakvura, they transform their own flesh into a weapon of elemental destruction. Revered for their battlefield dominance and feared for their volatile, barely-contained fury, Vanguards occupy a unique position in the Elsaither: raw power incarnate, walking the knife's edge between warrior and force of nature. Where the Savaroen channels power through spiritual discipline and the Shadow Warrior through symbiotic bond, the Vanguard simply tears that power from within—at great personal cost.*

**Primary Ability:** Strength and Constitution
**Hit Die:** 1d12 per Vanguard level
**Hit Points at 1st Level:** 12 + your Constitution modifier
**Hit Points at Higher Levels:** 1d12 (or 7) + your Constitution modifier per level after 1st

**Proficiencies**
**Armor:** Light Armor, Medium Armor, Heavy Armor, Shields
**Weapons:** Simple Weapons, Martial Weapons
**Tools:** None
**Saving Throws:** Constitution, Charisma
**Skills:** Choose two from Athletics, Intimidation, Perception, Survival, Animal Handling, and Nature.

**Equipment**
You start with the following equipment, in addition to the equipment granted by your background:
* (a) a regional Martial Weapon or (b) two Simple Weapons
* (a) Heavy Armor or (b) Medium Armor and a Shield
* (a) Explorer's Pack or (b) Dungeoneer's Pack
* A pouch with 15cc (or 40cc starting gold)

**Multiclassing Prerequisites:** To multiclass into the Vanguard, you must have a Strength score of 13 and a Constitution score of 13.

## **Core Class Features**

### **Weapon Mastery**
*1st-Level Vanguard Feature*

Your training with weapons allows you to use the Mastery properties of three simple or martial weapons you are proficient with. Whenever you finish a Long Rest, you can change any of those weapon choices to another eligible weapon you are proficient with.

### **Fighting Style**
*1st-Level Vanguard Feature*

You adopt a style of fighting as your specialty. Choose one of the following Fighting Style feats: *Defense, Dueling, Great Weapon Fighting, Protection, Thrown Weapon Fighting*, or *Two-Weapon Fighting*.

---

### **Dakvura (The Shift)**
*2nd-Level Vanguard Feature*

You can use a Bonus Action to enter a state of Dakvura for up to 1 minute. While in Dakvura, you gain the following benefits:

* **Enhanced Weapons:** Your melee weapon attacks deal an additional **1d4 damage** of the attack's normal damage type. This bonus increases to **1d6 at 5th level**, **1d8 at 11th level**, and **1d10 at 17th level**.
* **Resilience:** You gain Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.
* **Speed:** Your walking speed increases by 10 feet (this increases to +20 feet at 18th level via *Perfected Dakvura*).

Dakvura ends early if you are Knocked Unconscious, or if you use a Bonus Action to end it voluntarily. You cannot enter Dakvura again without expending a use. You have a number of uses as shown in the Dakvura Uses column of the Vanguard table, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest. You also regain 1 expended use when you finish a Short Rest.

---

### **Feral Bloodlust**
*2nd-Level Vanguard Feature*

The raw power of Dakvura overwhelms your senses. While in Dakvura, you are subject to Feral Bloodlust:

* **Compulsive Aggression:** At the start of each of your turns while in Dakvura, you must move toward the nearest creature you can see or hear (friend or foe). If you can reach that creature using your movement, you must use your Action to make a weapon attack against it.
* **Fighting the Urge:** If you wish to target a different creature, you must succeed on a **Charisma saving throw** (DC = 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your Constitution modifier). On a success, you regain control for that turn and can act normally. On a failure, you must attack the closest creature. If no creature is within reach, you must use your Action to Dash toward the nearest perceived creature.
* **Clarity at 9th Level:** This drawback is eliminated entirely when you gain the *Controlled Bloodlust* feature at 9th level.

---

### **Elemental Discipline**
*3rd-Level Vanguard Feature*

You bind your volatile power to a specific element, choosing from the full elemental list of the Elsaither: **Fire, Water, Ice, Earth, Air, Lightning, Laser, Gas, Metal, Bone, Shadow, Death, Poison, Knife, Darki**, or **Sky**. This choice is permanent and grants you your Elemental Arts and subclass features at 3rd level and again at 7th, 10th, 14th, and 17th level.

**Elemental Arts.** Your subclass unlocks a pool of powerful Elemental Arts—abilities that harness your chosen element without expending spell slots. You have a number of uses as shown in the Elemental Arts column of the Vanguard table. You regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest. You regain a number of expended uses equal to half your Proficiency Bonus (rounded up) when you finish a Short Rest.

**Art Save DC** = 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your Constitution modifier
**Art Attack Modifier** = your Proficiency Bonus + your Strength modifier

---

### **Extra Attack**
*5th-Level Vanguard Feature*

You can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn.

### **Fast Movement**
*5th-Level Vanguard Feature*

Your walking speed increases by 10 feet permanently (this bonus is separate from and stacks with the speed bonus granted by Dakvura).

---

### **Elemental Strike**
*6th-Level Vanguard Feature*

The dark elemental power permanently bleeds into your armaments. Your weapon attacks count as Magical for the purpose of overcoming Resistance and Immunity to non-magical attacks. Additionally, whenever you deal damage with a weapon attack (including any bonus damage from Dakvura), you can choose to have all of that damage deal your Elemental Discipline's primary damage type instead of its normal type.

---

### **Controlled Bloodlust**
*9th-Level Vanguard Feature*

You have mastered the mental toll of the Shift. While in Dakvura, you are no longer subject to Feral Bloodlust. You no longer need to make Charisma saving throws to choose your targets, and you can flawlessly distinguish between friend and foe in the heat of battle.

---

### **Ruthless Onslaught**
*11th-Level Vanguard Feature*

Your precision while shifted reaches lethal levels. While in Dakvura, whenever you reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points with a melee weapon attack, you can immediately make one additional melee weapon attack against a different creature within your reach as part of the same Action.

---

### **Relentless Shift**
*13th-Level Vanguard Feature*

If you roll Initiative and have no uses of Dakvura remaining, you immediately regain 1 use.

---

### **Vanguard's Protection**
*15th-Level Vanguard Feature*

Your bloodlust evolves into a ruthless protective instinct. While in Dakvura, when an ally within your walking speed (including your Dakvura speed bonus) is targeted by an attack roll or fails a saving throw against an effect you can see, you can use your **Reaction** to instantly move to an unoccupied space adjacent to that ally. If you do, you become the target of the triggering attack or effect instead. If the triggering creature is within your reach after you move, you can make one melee weapon attack against it as part of this Reaction. This movement does not provoke Opportunity Attacks.

---

### **Perfected Dakvura**
*18th-Level Vanguard Feature*

Your physical form is perfectly synchronized with the dark power. The Resistance granted by Dakvura expands to include **Necrotic damage**. You achieve **Elemental Supremacy**: your weapon attacks and Elemental Arts that deal your Elemental Discipline's primary damage type ignore Resistance and treat Immunity as Resistance. The speed bonus granted by Dakvura increases to **+20 feet** (replacing the previous +10 feet).

---

### **Epic Boon**
*19th-Level Vanguard Feature*

You achieve an epic milestone. You gain one Epic Boon feat of your choice from the *Player's Handbook (2024)* for which you meet the prerequisites.

---

### **Abyssal Executioner**
*20th-Level Vanguard Feature*

Your uses of Dakvura become **unlimited**—you can enter Dakvura as many times as you wish without expending uses.

Additionally, while in Dakvura, when you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack, you can expend **2 Elemental Arts uses** to unleash a devastating surge of power. The creature must make a **Constitution saving throw** against your Art Save DC. On a failure, the creature takes an additional **6d10 damage** of your Elemental Discipline's primary damage type and is **Stunned** until the end of your next turn. On a success, it takes half the additional damage and is not Stunned.

## **Elemental Disciplines**

*Each Elemental Discipline represents a Vanguard who has bound their Dakvura to a specific element of the Elsaither. All Disciplines share the same framework: 4 Elemental Arts defined at 3rd level, a passive 3rd-level feature, and subclass features at 7th, 10th, 14th, and 17th level.*

*Unless otherwise noted, each Elemental Art costs 1 Elemental Art use to activate, and the Art Save DC equals 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your Constitution modifier.*

---

### **The Infernal Discipline (Fire)**
*Elemental Profile: Primary — Fire | Secondary — Radiant, Force, Piercing*

*Vanguards of the Infernal Discipline do not simply wield fire—they are consumed by it. Every blow they land sets the world alight, every step they take leaves scorched earth, and every moment they hold the Shift brings them closer to a volcanic eruption. They are the most feared Vanguards at close range, pouring on offensive pressure until nothing remains but ash.*

#### Elemental Arts (Infernal)
At 3rd level, you gain the following Elemental Arts.

**Blazing Strike.** *Triggered when you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack.* The target takes an additional **2d6 Fire damage** and must make a **Constitution saving throw** or begin Burning (taking **1d4 Fire damage** at the start of each of its turns). A creature or a creature within 5 feet of it can use an Action to extinguish the flames, ending the Burning early.

**Flame Surge.** *Bonus Action.* You erupt in superheated air. Each creature in a **15-foot cone** must make a **Dexterity saving throw**, taking **3d6 Fire damage** on a failure (half on a success). Creatures that fail the save also begin Burning (1d4 Fire per turn, as above).

**Ember Shield.** *Reaction, triggered when you take damage.* You sheathe yourself in volatile embers, gaining **Temporary Hit Points** equal to **2d8 + your Constitution modifier**. If the attacker is within 5 feet of you, it takes **1d6 Fire damage**.

**Infernal Dash.** *Bonus Action.* You ignite your legs and surge forward, moving up to **20 feet** in a straight line without provoking Opportunity Attacks. Each creature within 5 feet of your path must succeed on a **Dexterity saving throw** or take **1d10 Fire damage**.

#### Infernal Aura
*3rd-Level Infernal Discipline Feature*
While in Dakvura, you radiate intense heat. Any creature that starts its turn within **5 feet** of you takes **Fire damage equal to your Proficiency Bonus**.

#### Detonation
*7th-Level Infernal Discipline Feature*
Your Flame Surge Art is supercharged. When you use Flame Surge, you may also apply a shockwave: creatures that fail the saving throw are pushed **10 feet** away from you and knocked **Prone**, and the cone increases to **20 feet**.

#### Phoenix Rush
*10th-Level Infernal Discipline Feature*
As a Bonus Action while in Dakvura (no Elemental Art cost), you charge up to **30 feet** in a straight line without provoking Opportunity Attacks. If you moved at least 10 feet, your next melee weapon attack this turn deals an additional **3d6 Fire damage** and the target begins Burning (1d6 Fire per turn). You can use this feature a number of times equal to your **Proficiency Bonus** per Long Rest.

#### Living Flame
*14th-Level Infernal Discipline Feature*
You gain **Immunity to Fire damage**. While in Dakvura, you shed bright light in a 10-foot radius and dim light for an additional 10 feet. Any creature that hits you with a melee attack takes **2d6 Fire damage**.

#### Volcanic Eruption
*17th-Level Infernal Discipline Feature*
Once per Long Rest, as an Action, you trigger a volcanic eruption centered on yourself. Each creature within **30 feet** must make a **Dexterity saving throw** against your Art Save DC, taking **8d8 Fire damage** on a failure (half on a success). Creatures that fail also begin Burning (2d6 Fire per turn). The ground within the eruption radius becomes Difficult Terrain for 1 minute.

### **The Tide Sovereign (Water)**
*Elemental Profile: Primary — Cold | Secondary — Bludgeoning, Piercing, Acid*

*Tide Sovereigns are masters of momentum and pressure. Their strikes carry the weight of an ocean wave, and their battlefield control is unmatched among the Vanguards. Where others overwhelm with fury, Tide Sovereigns overwhelm with inevitability—pulling enemies in, slowing their movements, and freezing them solid.*

#### Elemental Arts (Tidal)

**Crushing Current.** *Triggered when you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack.* The target takes an additional **2d6 Cold damage** and must make a **Strength saving throw** or be pushed **10 feet** away from you and knocked **Prone**.

**Tidal Surge.** *Bonus Action.* You release a burst of pressurized water in a **15-foot cone**. Each creature must make a **Strength saving throw**, taking **3d6 Cold damage** on a failure (half on a success). Creatures that fail are pushed **10 feet** away and have their Speed reduced by 10 feet until the start of your next turn.

**Frost Barrier.** *Reaction, triggered when you are targeted by an attack or effect.* You crystallize moisture into a defensive shell, gaining **Temporary Hit Points** equal to **2d10 + your Constitution modifier** that last until the start of your next turn or until depleted.

**Undertow Pull.** *Bonus Action.* You reach out with freezing water. One creature you can see within **30 feet** must make a **Strength saving throw** or be pulled **15 feet** directly toward you and have its Speed reduced to **0** until the start of your next turn.

#### Ebb and Flow
*3rd-Level Tide Sovereign Feature*
Whenever you push or pull a creature using one of your Elemental Arts, you can also move up to 10 feet in the opposite direction as part of the same Bonus Action or Reaction, without provoking Opportunity Attacks.

#### Freezing Strike
*7th-Level Tide Sovereign Feature*
When you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack, you can expend **1 Elemental Art use** (in addition to any other cost for that action) to force it to make a **Constitution saving throw**. On a failure, the target is **Restrained** by a coating of ice until the end of its next turn.

#### Undertow Nova
*10th-Level Tide Sovereign Feature*
Once per Long Rest, as an Action (no Elemental Art cost), you unleash a massive tidal surge. Each creature within **20 feet** must make a **Strength saving throw**. On a failure, a creature takes **6d8 Cold damage**, is pulled to a space within 5 feet of you, and is **Restrained** (iced over) until the end of your next turn. On a success, it takes half damage.

#### Glacial Dominion
*14th-Level Tide Sovereign Feature*
Creatures Restrained by your ice have their Speed reduced to 0 for the duration, cannot take Reactions, and take **1d6 Cold damage** at the start of each of their turns until freed. Additionally, you ignore Difficult Terrain.

#### Ocean's Wrath
*17th-Level Tide Sovereign Feature*
You gain **Immunity to Cold damage**. While in Dakvura, whenever you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack, you can choose to deal **Cold damage** instead of the attack's normal type (no Elemental Art cost; stacks with *Elemental Strike*). Your Crushing Current Art costs no Elemental Art uses while in Dakvura.

### **The Glacial Bulwark (Ice)**
*Elemental Profile: Primary — Cold | Secondary — Piercing, Slashing, Bludgeoning*

*Where the Tide Sovereign commands the flow of battle, the Glacial Bulwark simply becomes immovable. Ice Vanguards crystallize their Dakvura into a second skin of jagged ice, turning every attempted blow into a punishing experience for the attacker. They are the hardest Vanguards to kill.*

#### Elemental Arts (Glacial)

**Frost Strike.** *Triggered when you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack.* The target takes an additional **2d6 Cold damage** and its Speed is reduced by **15 feet** until the start of your next turn.

**Ice Spike Burst.** *Bonus Action.* Spikes of ice erupt in a **10-foot radius** centered on a point you can see within 30 feet. Each creature in the area must make a **Dexterity saving throw**, taking **3d8 Cold damage** on a failure (half on a success). Creatures that fail have their Speed reduced by 10 feet until the start of your next turn.

**Crystal Carapace.** *Bonus Action.* You crystallize a shell of ice around your body, gaining **Temporary Hit Points** equal to **3d6 + your Constitution modifier**. Until these Temporary Hit Points are depleted, any creature that hits you with a melee attack takes **1d4 Cold damage** from the icy shards.

**Freezing Grip.** *Triggered when you successfully Grapple a creature (1 use expended at the moment of grappling).* At the start of each of the Grappled creature's turns, it must make a **Constitution saving throw** or take **2d6 Cold damage** and be unable to use verbal components until the start of your next turn.

#### Permafrost Body
*3rd-Level Glacial Bulwark Feature*
While in Dakvura, your body is encased in crackling ice. Creatures that hit you with melee attacks take **Cold damage equal to your Proficiency Bonus**. Additionally, the ground within 10 feet of you is Difficult Terrain for other creatures.

#### Crystal Armor
*7th-Level Glacial Bulwark Feature*
While in Dakvura, your AC increases by **+2** from the crystallized ice overlaying your armor. If you are not wearing armor while in Dakvura, your AC equals **12 + your Dexterity modifier + your Constitution modifier** instead.

#### Absolute Zero
*10th-Level Glacial Bulwark Feature*
Once per Long Rest, as an Action (no Elemental Art cost), you drop the ambient temperature to lethal levels. Each creature within **20 feet** must make a **Constitution saving throw**. On a failure, a creature takes **8d6 Cold damage** and is **Frozen Solid** (Incapacitated, Speed 0) until it takes Fire or Thunder damage, or until a creature within 5 feet uses an Action to break the ice (Strength check against your Art Save DC). On a success, it takes half damage.

#### Glacial Immortal
*14th-Level Glacial Bulwark Feature*
When you are reduced to 0 Hit Points while in Dakvura, you can choose not to fall Unconscious. Instead, you drop to 1 Hit Point and gain Resistance to all damage until the start of your next turn, at which point the ice shell shatters. Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you finish a Long Rest.

#### Glacial Apotheosis
*17th-Level Glacial Bulwark Feature*
You gain **Immunity to Cold damage**. While in Dakvura, whenever you score a Critical Hit with a melee weapon attack, the target is **Frozen Solid** (as per *Absolute Zero*) until the end of your next turn, with no saving throw.

### **The Stonewarden Discipline (Earth)**
*Elemental Profile: Primary — Bludgeoning | Secondary — Force, Piercing, Thunder*

*The Stonewarden is the immovable wall of Elsaither's battlefield. Their connection to the earth runs deeper than most—they become one with the stone beneath their feet. As they shift, they become nigh-impossible to topple, and their strikes carry the force of avalanche and earthquake.*

#### Elemental Arts (Stone)

**Seismic Slam.** *Triggered when you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack.* The target takes an additional **2d8 Bludgeoning damage** and must make a **Strength saving throw** or be knocked **Prone**. If the target is already Prone, it takes an extra **1d8 Bludgeoning damage** instead.

**Tremor Pulse.** *Bonus Action.* You stomp the ground, sending a shockwave outward. Each creature within **15 feet** must make a **Strength saving throw**, taking **3d6 Bludgeoning damage** on a failure (half on a success) and being knocked **Prone** on a failure. This feature has no effect on flying creatures or creatures of Huge size or larger.

**Stone Skin.** *Bonus Action.* You harden your skin into living stone, gaining **Temporary Hit Points** equal to **2d8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your Constitution modifier** that last for 1 minute or until depleted.

**Ground Shatter.** *Bonus Action.* You destroy the earth beneath your enemies. Each creature within a **10-foot radius** of a point on the ground within 30 feet must make a **Dexterity saving throw**, taking **2d8 Bludgeoning damage** on a failure (half on a success). The affected area becomes Difficult Terrain for 1 minute.

#### Earthen Immovability
*3rd-Level Stonewarden Feature*
You have Advantage on ability checks and saving throws made to avoid being knocked Prone or forcibly moved. While in Dakvura, you ignore Difficult Terrain.

#### Tremor Step
*7th-Level Stonewarden Feature*
While in Dakvura, your walking speed cannot be reduced below 20 feet by any means. Additionally, when you move at least 10 feet before making a melee weapon attack, the target of that attack has Disadvantage on its next saving throw against one of your Elemental Arts before the end of your next turn.

#### Seismic Dominance
*10th-Level Stonewarden Feature*
Once per Long Rest, as an Action (no Elemental Art cost), you trigger a localized earthquake within **30 feet** of you. The area becomes Difficult Terrain. Each creature in the area must make a **Dexterity saving throw**. On a failure, a creature takes **6d8 Bludgeoning damage** and is Restrained (buried to the knees in rubble) until it uses an Action to free itself (Strength check against your Art Save DC). On a success, it takes half damage.

#### Titan's Grip
*14th-Level Stonewarden Feature*
You can Grapple creatures up to two sizes larger than you. A creature Grappled by you is also **Restrained**. Your weapon attacks against a creature you are Grappling deal an additional **1d8 Bludgeoning damage**.

#### Living Mountain
*17th-Level Stonewarden Feature*
You cannot be involuntarily pushed, pulled, knocked Prone, or relocated by any means while standing on solid ground. While in Dakvura, you gain **Immunity to Bludgeoning damage from non-magical sources**, and your Tremor Pulse Art costs no Elemental Art uses.

### **The Gale Sovereign (Air)**
*Elemental Profile: Primary — Force | Secondary — Thunder, Cold, Bludgeoning*

*Gale Sovereigns redefine what it means to be a melee combatant. They are never in one place long enough to be hit. Their Dakvura manifests as a perpetual storm that carries them across the battlefield in an instant, making them the most mobile and evasive Vanguards in the Elsaither.*

#### Elemental Arts (Gale)

**Gale Strike.** *Triggered when you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack.* The target takes an additional **2d6 Force damage** and must make a **Strength saving throw** or be pushed **10 feet** away from you. If it fails and collides with an object or another creature, it also takes **1d6 Bludgeoning damage**.

**Cyclone Burst.** *Bonus Action.* You unleash a localized cyclone. Each creature within **15 feet** must make a **Strength saving throw**, taking **3d6 Force damage** on a failure (half on a success). Creatures that fail are pushed to the outer edge of the burst. The area becomes Difficult Terrain (swirling winds) until the start of your next turn.

**Wind Shroud.** *Reaction, triggered when targeted by a ranged attack.* Swirling winds deflect the blow. You gain **+5 to your AC** against the triggering attack. If the attack misses, you can move up to 10 feet as part of this Reaction without provoking Opportunity Attacks.

**Vortex Pull.** *Bonus Action.* You create a localized vortex at a point within **20 feet**. Each creature within 10 feet of that point must make a **Strength saving throw** or be pulled **10 feet** toward the point and Restrained (wind-bound) until the start of your next turn.

#### Wind Walker
*3rd-Level Gale Sovereign Feature*
Opportunity Attacks against you are made with **Disadvantage**. While in Dakvura, you can move through the space of any creature (you must end your turn in an unoccupied space).

#### Tailwind
*7th-Level Gale Sovereign Feature*
As a Bonus Action (no Elemental Art cost) while in Dakvura, you can take the **Dash** action. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your **Proficiency Bonus** per Long Rest.

#### Cyclone Form
*10th-Level Gale Sovereign Feature*
Once per Long Rest, as a Bonus Action, you transform into a whirling cyclone for 1 minute. While active, your walking speed increases by **30 feet** (stacks with Dakvura), you gain Resistance to Force and Thunder damage, and each creature that starts its turn within 10 feet of you must succeed on a **Strength saving throw** (DC = your Art Save DC) or be pushed 5 feet away and knocked Prone.

#### Windborne Retaliation
*14th-Level Gale Sovereign Feature*
When a creature misses you with a melee attack, you can use your **Reaction** (no Elemental Art cost) to move up to 15 feet without provoking Opportunity Attacks and make one melee weapon attack against that creature.

#### Eye of the Storm
*17th-Level Gale Sovereign Feature*
You gain **Immunity to Force damage**. While in Dakvura, at the start of each of your turns, each hostile creature within 15 feet must succeed on a **Strength saving throw** or be pushed **5 feet** directly away from you. Your Gale Strike Art costs no Elemental Art uses while in Dakvura.

### **The Stormstrike Discipline (Lightning)**
*Elemental Profile: Primary — Lightning | Secondary — Fire, Piercing, Radiant*

*A Stormstrike Vanguard moves like a bolt of lightning and hits like one too. Their power chains from target to target, paralyzing entire enemy formations before a single enemy has registered what hit them. They are offensive specialists who destroy single targets while threatening the entire formation around them.*

#### Elemental Arts (Stormstrike)

**Chain Strike.** *Triggered when you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack.* The target takes an additional **2d6 Lightning damage**, and one other creature of your choice within **10 feet** of the original target must make a **Dexterity saving throw** or take **1d8 Lightning damage**.

**Static Burst.** *Bonus Action.* You release a built-up electrical charge in a **15-foot cone**. Each creature must make a **Dexterity saving throw**, taking **3d6 Lightning damage** on a failure (half on a success). Creatures that fail cannot take Reactions until the start of your next turn.

**Galvanic Shell.** *Reaction, triggered when you take damage.* You discharge built-up static energy, taking **half the triggering damage** (rounded down). The attacker must make a **Dexterity saving throw** or take **1d10 Lightning damage**.

**Thunder Dash.** *Bonus Action.* You move with the speed of lightning, teleporting to an unoccupied space you can see within **30 feet**. Creatures within 5 feet of where you depart and where you arrive must make a **Dexterity saving throw** or take **1d6 Lightning damage** and lose their Reaction until the start of your next turn.

#### Voltage
*3rd-Level Stormstrike Feature*
Each time you hit the same creature with a melee weapon attack on consecutive turns, it takes an extra **1d4 Lightning damage** per consecutive hit (maximum **4d4**). The charge resets if you miss the creature, it moves more than 30 feet away, or you attack a different creature.

#### Thunder Leap
*7th-Level Stormstrike Feature*
Your Thunder Dash Art improves: the range increases to **60 feet**, and creatures within 10 feet of your departure and arrival points must make the saving throw. Additionally, if you teleport at least 30 feet, your next melee weapon attack before the end of your turn deals an extra **2d8 Lightning damage**.

#### Chain Lightning
*10th-Level Stormstrike Feature*
Once per Long Rest, as an Action (no Elemental Art cost), you unleash a full lightning barrage. Choose one creature you can see within 30 feet. It must make a **Dexterity saving throw**. On a failure, it takes **8d6 Lightning damage** and the bolt jumps to up to **4** other creatures of your choice within 30 feet of the original target (each making its own save, taking **4d6 Lightning** on a failure or half on a success). On a success, the original creature takes half damage with no jump.

#### Static Field
*14th-Level Stormstrike Feature*
As a Bonus Action while in Dakvura (1 Elemental Art use), you activate a Static Field that lasts 1 minute. While active, hostile creatures within 15 feet of you have their Speed halved, ranged weapon attacks against creatures within the field are made with Disadvantage, and you have Advantage on melee attack rolls against creatures within the field.

#### Storm Body
*17th-Level Stormstrike Feature*
You gain **Immunity to Lightning damage**. While in Dakvura, your Chain Strike Art costs no Elemental Art uses. Additionally, whenever you score a Critical Hit with a melee weapon attack, lightning arcs to all hostile creatures within 15 feet of the target: each must succeed on a **Dexterity saving throw** or take **2d10 Lightning damage**.

### **The Radiant Sovereign (Laser)**
*Elemental Profile: Primary — Radiant | Secondary — Fire, Piercing, Necrotic*

*Radiant Sovereigns bring the precision of a laser to the brutality of a Vanguard chassis. *7th-Level Void-Iron Feature*
When you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack, you can expend **1 Elemental Art use** (no additional action required) to intensify the gravity field around it. The creature's Speed is reduced by **30 feet** (minimum 0) and it has **Disadvantage on Strength and Dexterity saving throws** until the start of your next turn.

#### Void Explosion
*10th-Level Void-Iron Feature*
Once per Long Rest, as an Action (no Elemental Art cost), you release a catastrophic void detonation. Each creature within **30 feet** must make a **Strength saving throw**. On a failure, a creature takes **8d6 Necrotic damage** and **4d6 Force damage**, is knocked **Prone**, and is pushed **20 feet** away from you. On a success, it takes half Necrotic damage and no Force damage.

#### Singularity
*14th-Level Void-Iron Feature*
You can expend **2 Elemental Art uses** as an Action to create a gravitational singularity at a point within 60 feet. Each creature within **30 feet** of that point must make a **Strength saving throw**. On a failure, it is pulled to a space within 5 feet of the singularity, takes **6d8 Necrotic damage**, and is **Restrained** (gravitationally pinned) for 1 minute (repeating the Strength save at the end of each of its turns to break free). On a success, it takes half damage and is not Restrained.

#### Void Apotheosis
*17th-Level Void-Iron Feature*
You gain **Immunity to Necrotic damage**. While in Dakvura, whenever you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack, gravitational pulses radiate outward: each hostile creature within 10 feet of the target must succeed on a **Strength saving throw** or take **2d6 Force damage** and be pushed **5 feet** away from the target. Your Void Fist Art costs no Elemental Art uses while in Dakvura.

### **The Stormcrown Discipline (Sky)**
*Elemental Profile: Primary — Lightning or Force (choose one permanently at Level 3) | Secondary — Force or Lightning (whichever was not chosen as Primary), Thunder, Cold*

*Stormcrown Vanguards are the sovereigns of the open sky and everything beneath it. They combine the lethal precision of lightning with the overwhelming force of hurricane winds, creating a class that dominates both close-range and wide-area combat. They are among the most spectacular Vanguards to witness in battle—and among the most deadly to face.*

#### Elemental Arts (Stormcrown)
When you select this Discipline at 3rd level, choose whether your primary damage type is **Lightning** or **Force**. This choice is permanent and applies to all features that reference your primary elemental damage type.

**Thunderclap Strike.** *Triggered when you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack.* The blow detonates with combined storm energy. The target takes **1d8 Lightning damage** and **1d8 Thunder damage** and must make a **Constitution saving throw** or be **Deafened** until the start of your next turn and pushed **5 feet** away from you.

**Maelstrom Burst.** *Bonus Action.* You release a rotating column of storm energy. Each creature within **20 feet** must make a **Strength saving throw**, taking **2d6 Lightning damage** and **2d6 Thunder damage** on a failure (half Lightning damage, no Thunder damage on a success). Creatures that fail are pushed **10 feet** away from you.

**Storm Ward.** *Reaction, triggered when you take Lightning, Thunder, or Force damage.* You align with the storm, taking **half the triggering damage** (rounded down) and gaining **Advantage on your next attack roll** before the start of your next turn.

**Heaven's Descent.** *Bonus Action.* You hurl yourself downward in a thunderous leap, moving up to **30 feet** toward a point on the ground. Each creature within 10 feet of your landing point must make a **Dexterity saving throw** or take **3d6 Thunder damage** and be knocked **Prone** (half damage, not Prone on a success).

#### Lord of the Open Sky
*3rd-Level Stormcrown Feature*
You gain Resistance to **Lightning and Thunder damage**. Your jump distance is tripled, and you take no fall damage from falls of 60 feet or fewer.

#### Skyward Leap
*7th-Level Stormcrown Feature*
As a Bonus Action (no Elemental Art cost) while in Dakvura, you can leap up to **60 feet** in any direction, taking no fall damage. If you land on or adjacent to a hostile creature, it must succeed on a **Dexterity saving throw** or take **3d6 Thunder damage** and be knocked **Prone**. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your **Proficiency Bonus** per Long Rest.

#### Thunderstorm
*10th-Level Stormcrown Feature*
Once per Long Rest, as an Action (no Elemental Art cost), you call down a concentrated storm for 1 minute. At the start of each of your turns while the storm is active, choose up to **3 creatures** you can see within **60 feet**. Each must succeed on a **Dexterity saving throw** or take **3d6 Lightning damage** and **3d6 Thunder damage** (half on a success). This feature does not require Concentration.

#### Storm's Eye
*14th-Level Stormcrown Feature*
While in Dakvura, you create a **20-foot radius** aura of storm winds. Hostile creatures entering or starting their turn in the aura have their Speed halved. Ranged weapon attacks against any creature within the aura are made with **Disadvantage**. Friendly creatures within the aura have **Advantage on Dexterity saving throws**.

#### Lord of Storms
*17th-Level Stormcrown Feature*
You gain **Immunity to Lightning and Thunder damage**. While in Dakvura, at the start of each of your turns, you can direct a **Storm Bolt** at one creature you can see within **60 feet** (no Elemental Art cost): that creature must succeed on a **Dexterity saving throw** or take **3d10 Lightning damage** (half on a success). Your Thunderclap Strike Art costs no Elemental Art uses while in Dakvura.
