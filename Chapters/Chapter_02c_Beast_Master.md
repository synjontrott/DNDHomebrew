# **Beast Master**

*Those who walk between worlds — speaking the language of wind and root, claw and scale — the Beast Masters of Elsaither are primal conduits between civilization and the untamed wild. Bound to the spirit of a living archetype, they channel elemental animal mutations into their own bodies while commanding a loyal spirit companion. Revered among the Garanko for their ferocity and among the Plunesako for their wisdom, Beast Masters can be found in every corner of Elsaither, from the shadowed Ninjonian forests to the elemental plateaus of the Warrion highlands.*

**Primary Ability:** Wisdom
**Hit Die:** 1d8 per Beast Master level
**Hit Points at 1st Level:** 8 + your Constitution modifier
**Hit Points at Higher Levels:** 1d8 (or 5) + your Constitution modifier per level after 1st.

**Proficiencies**
**Armor:** Light Armor, Medium Armor, Shields
**Weapons:** Simple Weapons, Martial Weapons
**Tools:** Herbalism Kit
**Saving Throws:** Wisdom, Constitution
**Skills:** Choose two from Animal Handling, Athletics, Insight, Nature, Perception, Stealth, or Survival.

**Equipment**
(a) a regional Martial Weapon or (b) two Simple Weapons
(a) regional Light or Medium Armor or (b) Leather Armor and a Shield
(a) Explorer's Pack or (b) Dungeoneer's Pack
A Herbalism Kit and 20cc (or 50cc starting gold)

## **Core Class Features**

### **Spellcasting**
*1st-Level Beast Master Feature*

You have learned to draw upon the magic of the natural world.

**Primal Bond.** Choose one elemental affinity from the following list: Fire, Water, Air, Lightning, Earth, Ice, Laser, Bone, Gas, Metal, Knife (Cold Laser), Poison, Death, Shadow, or Darki. This choice reflects the primal spirit that animates your Mark. You gain Resistance to that element's primary damage type (as listed in Chapter 1: The Elemental System), if you do not already have it.

**Deity Blessing (Optional).** At 1st level, you may dedicate yourself to one of the Deities of the Elsaither (see Chapter 6). Choosing a Deity is optional, but doing so grants you access to sacred magic tied to that god's domain.
*   **Requirement:** Your element must match the element listed for your chosen Deity's cantrip, or your DM must approve the connection as lore-appropriate.
*   **Deity Cantrip:** You learn your Deity's **Deity Cantrip** (listed at the start of each deity's section in Chapter 6). This cantrip does not count against your Cantrips Known total. You use Wisdom as your spellcasting ability for it.
*   **Deity's Unique Spells:** Each Deity has unique spells. These spells are always prepared for you and do not count against your Prepared Spells total. You can cast them using your Beast Master spell slots of the appropriate level. You gain access to each Unique Spell only when you can cast spells of that level.
*   **Changing Deities:** With your DM's permission and a significant story moment (such as a pilgrimage, ordeal, or act of devotion), you may switch to a different Deity. You lose the previous Deity's benefits and gain the new Deity's.

**Cantrips.** You learn two cantrips of your choice from the Savaroen spell list. You learn additional cantrips at higher levels, as shown in the Cantrips Known column of the Beast Master table.

**Prepared Spells.** You prepare two 1st-level spells of your choice from the Savaroen spell list. The Prepared Spells column of the Beast Master table shows when you can prepare more spells. Each of these spells must be of a level for which you have spell slots. 

**Preparing and Casting Spells.** The Beast Master table shows how many spell slots you have to cast your spells of 1st level and higher. You regain all expended spell slots when you finish a Long Rest. 

**Spellcasting Ability.** Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for your Beast Master spells.
* **Spell Save DC** = 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your Wisdom modifier
* **Spell Attack modifier** = your Proficiency Bonus + your Wisdom modifier

### **Weapon Mastery**
*1st-Level Beast Master Feature*

Your training with weapons allows you to use their special properties. You gain the Weapon Mastery property of two weapons you are proficient with. Whenever you finish a Long Rest, you can change one of those weapon choices to another weapon you are proficient with.

### **Primal Engine**
*2nd-Level Beast Master Feature*

You tap into a deep reservoir of natural energy called a Primal Essence Pool (PE). Your pool has a maximum number of points equal to your Beast Master level + your Wisdom modifier. You regain all expended PE when you finish a Long Rest. Once per Long Rest, when you finish a Short Rest, you can recover a number of expended PE equal to your Wisdom modifier.

You can expend these points to fuel two primal pillars:

**PILLAR I — SPIRIT COMPANION**
You are permanently accompanied by a beast companion. Until you choose a Mark of the Wild at 3rd level, this takes the form of a generic primal beast.
* **Statistics:** Its Hit Point maximum equals 5 times your Beast Master level + your Wisdom modifier. Its Armor Class is 12 + your Proficiency Bonus. Its Attack bonus equals your spell attack modifier, and its damage is 1d6 + your Wisdom modifier. Its Speed is 40 feet (unless defined by your Companion Form). Its saving throws use your Spell Save DC.
* **Movement & Positioning (0 PE):** You can direct your companion where to move on your turn without expending an action or Primal Essence.
* **Revival:** If reduced to 0 Hit Points, your companion vanishes and returns at the end of your next Long Rest.
* **Actions:** The companion acts on your turn. If you give it no command, it takes the Dodge action automatically.
* **Commanding an Action (0 PE):** As a Bonus Action (or by replacing one of your own attacks when you take the Attack action), you can command your companion to take the Attack, Dash, Disengage, Help, or Search action. Commanding your companion to move or make a standard attack costs **0 PE**.
* **Enhanced Strike (1 PE):** When your companion hits with an attack, you can spend 1 PE (no action required) to apply your Mark's Enhanced Strike effect (such as *Hamstring Bite*, *Dragonfear Strike*, *Crushing Maul*, *Owl's Hex*, *Talon Mark*, or *Shepherd's Blessing*).

**PILLAR II — ASPECT MUTATIONS**
As a Bonus Action, you can spend 1 PE to channel an animal trait into your own body for 1 minute. You can have a maximum of 1 mutation active at a time (this increases to 2 at 11th level). An Aspect Mutation ends early if you are Incapacitated or Unconscious. Until you gain a subclass at 3rd level, you know two basic mutations:
* **Primal Senses (1 PE):** You gain Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell or hearing, and you gain Darkvision out to a range of 60 feet. *Duration: 1 hour.*
* **Beast Hide (1 PE):** If you are not wearing armor, your AC equals 13 + your Dexterity modifier. *Duration: 10 minutes.*

At 3rd level, when you choose your Mark of the Wild, you unlock your Mark's 6 specific Aspect Mutations (1 PE) as well as your Mark's powerful **Apex Aspect Mutation** (2 PE).

### **Mark of the Wild**
*3rd-Level Beast Master Feature*

In the world of Elsaither, everyone is marked by an animal spirit, whether they possess primal magic or not. Each mark represents the core personality of a character, shaping their outlook toward assigned tasks and their demeanor when facing daunting challenges. There are six primary animal marks — **Wolf**, **Dragon**, **Bear**, **Owl**, **Eagle**, and **Sheep** — though each mark encompasses a wide spectrum of allied animal spirits that share similar temperaments and primal drives.

You choose a Mark of the Wild that defines your spirit companion, your aspect mutations, and your subclass features at 3rd level and again at 6th, 7th, 10th, and 14th level.

### **Ability Score Improvement**
*4th-Level Beast Master Feature*

When you reach 4th, 8th, 12th, and 16th level, you can increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or you can increase two ability scores of your choice by 1. As normal, you can't increase an ability score above 20 using this feature. Alternatively, you can take a Feat from the Player's Handbook for which you qualify.

### **Extra Attack**
*5th-Level Beast Master Feature*

You can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn.

### **Enhanced Pillars**
*11th-Level Beast Master Feature*

Your mastery over the primal engine reaches new heights across both pillars:
* **Pillar I (Spirit Companion):** When your Spirit Companion scores a Critical Hit, the Enhanced Strike triggered by that hit costs 0 PE. Additionally, your companion's damage increases to 1d8 + your Wisdom modifier, and its Armor Class increases by 2.
* **Pillar II (Aspect Mutations):** You can now maintain up to 2 Aspect Mutations simultaneously. The duration of all your 1-PE Aspect Mutations with a 1-minute base duration increases to 10 minutes (Apex Aspect Mutations increase to 5 minutes). Whenever you manifest an Aspect Mutation, you immediately gain Temporary Hit Points equal to 2 × your Beast Master level.

### **Primal Arcanum**
*13th-Level Beast Master Feature*

Your primal mastery reaches beyond normal limits. Choose one 4th-level spell from the Savaroen spell list that is thematically appropriate to your element and Mark. You can cast that spell once per Long Rest without expending a spell slot. Alternatively, you can expend 4 Primal Essence points to cast it again. This spell is always prepared for you and does not count against your Prepared Spells total.

**Primal Arcanum (2nd)** 
*17th-Level Beast Master Feature*

Choose a second spell of 5th level from the Savaroen spell list that is thematically appropriate to your element and Mark. You can cast that spell once per Long Rest without expending a spell slot. Alternatively, you can expend 5 Primal Essence points to cast it again. It is always prepared for you and does not count against your Prepared Spells total.

### **Spirit Sentinel**
*18th-Level Beast Master Feature*

Your bond with your companion allows it to shield your allies from harm. While your Spirit Companion is within 10 feet of an allied creature, that ally has Half Cover (+2 bonus to AC and Dexterity saving throws). Additionally, when a hostile creature makes an attack roll against an ally within 10 feet of your companion, your companion can use its Reaction to interpose itself, taking the damage from the attack instead of the ally.

### **Epic Boon of the Wild**
*19th-Level Beast Master Feature*

You achieve an epic milestone in your primal connection:
* **Epic Boon Feat.** You gain one Epic Boon feat of your choice from the Player's Handbook (2024) for which you meet the prerequisites.
* **Primal Mastery:** Your Primal Essence pool maximum increases by 4, and you can recover expended PE via a Short Rest twice per Long Rest instead of once.

### **Avatar of the Mark**
*20th-Level Beast Master Feature*

As a Bonus Action, you can embody the absolute pinnacle of your Mark for 1 minute. You can use this feature once per Long Rest. While active, you gain the following benefits:
* **Primal Surge:** At the start of each of your turns, you regain 3 Primal Essence points.
* **Transcendent Companion:** Your Spirit Companion's Hit Point maximum is doubled, its AC increases by 2, and it acts on your initiative each round without requiring commands.
* **Aspect Apotheosis:** You gain 50 Temporary Hit Points upon activation and gain the benefits of all 6 of your Mark's Aspect Mutations simultaneously without expending PE.
* **Primal Emanation:** When you activate this form, manifest an Aspect Mutation, or trigger an Enhanced Strike, all enemies within 15 feet of you must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw against your Spell Save DC or be Frightened until the end of their next turn.
* **Elemental Apotheosis:** Your attacks and spells that deal your primary elemental damage ignore Resistance, and treat Immunity as Resistance.

## **Marks of the Wild**

In Elsaither, everyone is marked by an animal spirit, whether they possess primal magic or not. Each mark represents the core personality of a character, shaping their outlook toward assigned tasks and their demeanor when facing daunting challenges. 

There are six primary animal marks — **Wolf**, **Dragon**, **Bear**, **Owl**, **Eagle**, and **Sheep** — though each mark covers a multitude of animal spirits with shared temperaments and primal drives. 

**Allied Spirit Aspect Blending.** In Elsaither, animal marks are not rigid silos. A character marked by one of the six main spirits often manifests overlapping physical, vocal, or sensory traits from their Mark's **allied animal spirits**. For example, a Beast Master with the Mark of the Eagle might channel the razor-sharp target-lock vision and friction-free air movement of an Eagle alongside the terrifying, power-suppressing subsonic voice of an allied **Lion** spirit (as exhibited by Blaze in LotE lore). Players and DMs are encouraged to flavor their aspect mutations, companion forms, and primal features using any combination of their Mark's allied animal spirits.

A Beast Master's choice of Mark determines their spirit companion's form, their aspect mutations, their apex aspect mutation, and their subclass features at 3rd, 6th, 7th, 10th, and 14th level.

Each Mark provides at Level 3:
* **Mark Spells:** Bonus spells always prepared, 1 per spell level as you unlock them (1st at level 3, 2nd at level 5, 3rd at level 9, 4th at level 13, 5th at level 17). These do not count against your Prepared Spells total.
* **Companion Form:** Defines the visual form, passive traits, and movement of your Spirit Companion.
* **Enhanced Strike:** The 1 PE effect applied when your companion hits.
* **Apex Aspect Mutation (2 PE):** A powerful 1-minute elemental animal transformation applied to your own body that embodies your primary Mark beast.
* **Aspect Mutations (1 PE):** The 6 specific 1-PE utility and combat mutations available to you, channeling your Mark's allied animal spirits.

### **Mark of the Wolf**
*Role: Battlefield Control & Pack Tactics (Disciplined Coordination, Pinning Strikes, Area Lockdown)*

The Mark of the Wolf is a mark of focus, drive, and the unyielding will to finish the mission no matter the cost. Wolves are the supreme tacticians and battlefield controllers of the wild: they do not fight as chaotic individuals, but as an orchestrated pack that dictates enemy movement, flanks relentlessly, trips dangerous foes, and pins targets so allies can execute them with ruthless efficiency. Characters like Arcaeus, Ignite, and Fusion are considered wolves. They are given an objective, and they will control the flow of combat until that mission is fulfilled.

*Allied Animal Spirits:* Other examples of animal spirits that fit under the Wolf mark include the Hawk, Falcon, Vortzhe (8-foot-tall, 90-mph hollow-boned mega-wolves), Meite (14-foot armored pangolin-like apex beasts), Hound/Dog, Tiger, and other similar creatures with an intense hunting and pack drive. Your Spirit Companion, Aspect Mutations, and Apex Mutation may aesthetically manifest as any of these hunting predators.

On a hit, the target must make a Strength saving throw against your Spell Save DC or have its Speed reduced by 15 feet until the end of its next turn and be knocked Prone.

#### Apex Aspect Mutation: Dire Hunter (2 PE)
*Level 3 Mark of the Wolf Feature*
As a Bonus Action, you can spend 2 PE to channel the supreme hunting spirit of the wolf for 1 minute:
* **Temp HP:** You gain Temporary Hit Points equal to 3 × your Beast Master level.
* **Speed:** Your walking speed increases to 50 feet.
* **Relentless Pursuit:** Opportunity attacks against you have Disadvantage, and you can take the Dash or Disengage action as a Bonus Action on each of your turns.
* **Predatory Jaws:** Your melee weapon attacks and unarmed strikes deal an extra 1d8 damage of your primary elemental type. In addition, when you hit a creature with a melee attack on your turn, you can force the target to make a Strength saving throw against your Spell Save DC or be knocked Prone.
* **Pack Pin:** While maintaining this mutation, whenever you hit a Prone creature with a melee attack, that creature's Speed becomes 0 until the start of your next turn, pinning it for your allies.

#### Aspect Mutations
*Level 3 Mark of the Wolf Feature*
As a Bonus Action, you can expend 1 Primal Essence to manifest one of these mutations, channeling your Mark's allied hunting spirits for battlefield control. These mutations last for 1 minute unless otherwise noted:
1. **Hawk's Spotter Lock (Hawk):** You channel the high-altitude surveillance of the hawk. You gain Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks. As a Bonus Action, you can designate an enemy within 60 feet as a spotted target: attacks made by you and your allies against that target ignore Half and Three-Quarters Cover, and allies gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls against it while within 30 feet of you. *Duration: 10 minutes.*
2. **Falcon's Harassment (Falcon):** You channel the diving harrier. When you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you can force it to make a Constitution saving throw against your Spell Save DC. On a failed save, the target is Blinded by swirling astral talons until the end of its next turn, and Opportunity Attacks against it are made with Advantage. *Duration: 1 minute (ends when triggered).*
3. **Tiger's Drag (Tiger):** You channel the explosive grappling power of the tiger. You gain a climbing speed equal to your walking speed. When you hit a creature with a melee attack while having Advantage, you can shove or drag the target up to 15 feet in any direction and knock it Prone (no saving throw). *Duration: 1 minute.*
4. **Hound's Cornering Hold (Hound):** You channel the quarry-pinning hound. You gain Advantage on Wisdom (Survival) checks made to track creatures. While you are within 5 feet of an enemy, that creature cannot take the Disengage action, and its Speed is reduced by 10 feet. If an allied creature is also within 5 feet of it, the enemy's Speed becomes 0. *Duration: 10 minutes.*
5. **Meite's Plated Carapace & Claws (Meite):** You channel the 14-foot armored meite titan. Your skin hardens into thick pangolin plates and your hands form massive razor claws. You gain a +2 bonus to your Armor Class, and your unarmed strikes deal 1d8 + your Wisdom modifier Slashing damage plus 1d6 damage of your primary elemental type (using Wisdom for attack and damage rolls). When you hit a creature with these claws, you can grapple it as a free action or shove it 10 feet away. *Duration: 10 minutes.*
6. **Vortzhe's 90-MPH Sprint (Vortzhe):** You channel the 8-foot-tall, hollow-boned mega-wolf whose muscular and skeletal structure achieves explosive 90-mph speeds. Your walking speed increases by 30 feet, and you can take the Dash action as a Bonus Action. When you Dash, your movement does not provoke Opportunity Attacks, and the first time you move through an enemy's space on your turn, that enemy must succeed on a Strength saving throw against your Spell Save DC or be knocked Prone. *Duration: 1 minute.*

#### Pack Leader
*6th-Level Mark of the Wolf Feature*
You and your Spirit Companion coordinate with lethal discipline. You gain the Pack Tactics trait (Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of your allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't Incapacitated). Your Spirit Companion, who already possesses this trait, now extends it to your hunting pack: when you or your companion damages a creature, allies within 10 feet of that creature can use their Reaction to move up to 10 feet toward it without provoking opportunity attacks.

#### Cull the Weak & Vengeful Retaliation
*7th-Level Mark of the Wolf Feature*
When you or your Spirit Companion reduces a creature to 0 Hit Points, you can move up to 15 feet without expending movement or using an action. This movement does not provoke opportunity attacks. 

Additionally, your unyielding drive turns with terrifying fury on those who strike your pack: whenever a creature damages you or an ally within 30 feet of you, your attacks and your companion's attacks against that creature deal an extra 1d6 damage of your primary elemental type until the end of your next turn.

#### Alpha's Command
*10th-Level Mark of the Wolf Feature*
As a Bonus Action, you can let out a commanding howl. All friendly creatures of your choice within 30 feet can immediately move up to their Speed as a Reaction. This movement does not provoke opportunity attacks. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus per Long Rest.

#### Feral Coordination
*14th-Level Mark of the Wolf Feature*
You and your Spirit Companion share a single devastating rhythm. When you hit a creature with a weapon attack or a spell attack, your Spirit Companion has Advantage on all attack rolls against that creature until the start of your next turn (and its next attack requires no PE cost). When attacking a target marked by *Pack Harrier* or designated as your primary mission objective, your companion's attacks also ignore Resistance.

Additionally, when your Spirit Companion is reduced to 0 Hit Points, you can expend 2 Primal Essence as a Reaction to prevent it from vanishing. It returns at the start of your next turn with Hit Points equal to your Wisdom modifier × 2. You can use this Reaction once per Long Rest.

### **Mark of the Dragon**
*Role: DPS / Direct Damage & Frightening Presence (Devastating Damage Spikes, Dragonfear, Area Conflagrations)*

The Mark of the Dragon is a mark of raw passion, explosive energy, and terrifying destructive power. Dragons are the premier damage dealers of the wild: they do not negotiate, and they do not retreat. Channelling draconic fury, characters of this mark tear through enemy lines with overwhelming elemental devastation while paralyzing foes in place through sheer, unadulterated terror. Characters like Drako, Inferno, and Bobbi are considered dragons. An unleashed dragon aimed at an enemy is an unstoppable weapon of mass destruction.

*Allied Animal Spirits:* Other examples of animal spirits that fit under the Dragon mark include the Snake, Xelis (25-foot eagle-dragon hybrids with razor claws and breath weapons), Lizard, and other reptilian or draconic creatures that have an unquenchable hunger for destruction. Your Spirit Companion, Aspect Mutations, and Apex Mutation may aesthetically manifest as any of these fiery or reptilian beasts.

### **Mark of the Sheep**
*Role: Support & Healing (Restoration, Damage Interception, Condition Cleansing, Steadfast Flock Wards)*

The Mark of the Sheep embodies loyalty, unity, and a gentle demeanor. Sheep are team players, fostering harmony within the group while prioritizing the collective goal over individual pursuits. Assigned a task, they execute it diligently, following instructions without hesitation. Characters like common soldiers, Mary, and townspeople are considered sheep. Though not inclined to question authority unnecessarily, they possess a strong moral compass; if commanded to act against their principles, they will stand firm, plant their hooves, and defend their values.

Beast Masters of the Sheep are the paladins of the wild — stalwart defenders and compassionate healers who command restorative magic, intercept incoming harm, and weather the harshest storms to keep their allies alive. Do not mistake their gentle bearing for weakness; a Ram's charge has shattered armies, and an iron shepherd will give their very blood to ensure the entire flock endures.

*Allied Animal Spirits:* Other animal spirits akin to the Sheep include the Mountain Goat, Alpaca, Koi, Floop, Krenishee, Dove, and other gentle creatures known for their cooperative, protective, or restorative nature. Your Spirit Companion, Aspect Mutations, and Apex Mutation may aesthetically manifest as any of these loyal, defensive beasts.
