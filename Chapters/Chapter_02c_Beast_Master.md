# **Beast Master**

*Those who walk between worlds — speaking the language of wind and root, claw and scale — the Beast Masters of Elsaither are primal conduits between civilization and the untamed wild. Bound to the spirit of a living archetype, they channel elemental animal mutations into their own bodies while commanding a loyal spirit companion. Revered among the Garanko for their ferocity and among the Plunesako for their wisdom, Beast Masters can be found in every corner of Elsaither, from the shadowed Ninjonian forests to the elemental plateaus of the Warrion highlands.*

**Primary Ability:** Wisdom
**Hit Die:** 1d8 per Beast Master level
**Hit Points at 1st Level:** 8 + your Constitution modifier
**Hit Points at Higher Levels:** 1d8 (or 5) + your Constitution modifier per level after 1st.

**Proficiencies**
**Armor:** Light Armor, Medium Armor, Shields
**Weapons:** Simple Weapons, Martial Weapons
**Tools:** Herbalism Kit
**Saving Throws:** Wisdom, Constitution
**Skills:** Choose two from Animal Handling, Athletics, Insight, Nature, Perception, Stealth, or Survival.

**Equipment**
(a) a regional Martial Weapon or (b) two Simple Weapons
(a) regional Light or Medium Armor or (b) Leather Armor and a Shield
(a) Explorer's Pack or (b) Dungeoneer's Pack
A Herbalism Kit and 20cc (or 50cc starting gold)

## **Core Class Features**

### **Spellcasting**
*1st-Level Beast Master Feature*

You have learned to draw upon the magic of the natural world.

**Primal Bond.** Choose one elemental affinity from the following list: Fire, Water, Air, Lightning, Earth, Ice, Laser, Bone, Gas, Metal, Knife (Cold Laser), Poison, Death, Shadow, or Darki. This choice reflects the primal spirit that animates your Mark. You gain Resistance to that element's primary damage type (as listed in Chapter 1: The Elemental System).

**Deity Blessing (Optional).** At 1st level, you may dedicate yourself to one of the Deities of the Elsaither (see Chapter 6). Choosing a Deity is optional, but doing so grants you access to sacred magic tied to that god's domain.
*   **Requirement:** Your element must match the element listed for your chosen Deity's cantrip, or your DM must approve the connection as lore-appropriate.
*   **Deity Cantrip:** You learn your Deity's **Deity Cantrip** (listed at the start of each deity's section in Chapter 6). This cantrip does not count against your Cantrips Known total. You use Wisdom as your spellcasting ability for it.
*   **Deity's Unique Spells:** Each Deity has unique spells. These spells are always prepared for you and do not count against your Prepared Spells total. You can cast them using your Beast Master spell slots of the appropriate level. You gain access to each Unique Spell only when you can cast spells of that level.
*   **Changing Deities:** With your DM's permission and a significant story moment (such as a pilgrimage, ordeal, or act of devotion), you may switch to a different Deity. You lose the previous Deity's benefits and gain the new Deity's.

**Cantrips.** You learn two cantrips of your choice from the Savaroen spell list. You learn additional cantrips at higher levels, as shown in the Cantrips Known column of the Beast Master table.

**Prepared Spells.** You prepare two 1st-level spells of your choice from the Savaroen spell list. The Prepared Spells column of the Beast Master table shows when you can prepare more spells. Each of these spells must be of a level for which you have spell slots. 

**Preparing and Casting Spells.** The Beast Master table shows how many spell slots you have to cast your spells of 1st level and higher. You regain all expended spell slots when you finish a Long Rest. 

**Spellcasting Ability.** Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for your Beast Master spells.
* **Spell Save DC** = 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your Wisdom modifier
* **Spell Attack modifier** = your Proficiency Bonus + your Wisdom modifier

### **Weapon Mastery**
*1st-Level Beast Master Feature*

Your training with weapons allows you to use their special properties. You gain the Weapon Mastery property of two weapons you are proficient with. Whenever you finish a Long Rest, you can change one of those weapon choices to another weapon you are proficient with.

### **Primal Engine**
*2nd-Level Beast Master Feature*

You tap into a deep reservoir of natural energy called a Primal Essence Pool (PE). Your pool has a maximum number of points equal to your Beast Master level + your Wisdom modifier. You regain all expended PE when you finish a Long Rest. Once per Long Rest, when you finish a Short Rest, you can recover a number of expended PE equal to your Wisdom modifier.

You can expend these points to fuel two primal pillars:

**PILLAR I — SPIRIT COMPANION**
You are permanently accompanied by a beast companion. Until you choose a Mark of the Wild at 3rd level, this takes the form of a generic primal beast.
* **Statistics:** Its Hit Point maximum equals 5 times your Beast Master level + your Wisdom modifier. Its Armor Class is 12 + your Proficiency Bonus. Its Attack bonus equals your spell attack modifier, and its damage is 1d6 + your Wisdom modifier. Its Speed is 40 feet (unless defined by your Companion Form). Its saving throws use your Spell Save DC.
* **Movement & Positioning (0 PE):** You can direct your companion where to move on your turn without expending an action or Primal Essence.
* **Revival:** If reduced to 0 Hit Points, your companion vanishes and returns at the end of your next Long Rest.
* **Actions:** The companion acts on your turn. If you give it no command, it takes the Dodge action automatically.
* **Commanding an Action (0 PE):** As a Bonus Action (or by replacing one of your own attacks when you take the Attack action), you can command your companion to take the Attack, Dash, Disengage, Help, or Search action. Commanding your companion to move or make a standard attack costs **0 PE**.
* **Enhanced Strike (1 PE):** When your companion hits with an attack, you can spend 1 PE (no action required) to apply your Mark's Enhanced Strike effect (such as *Hamstring Bite*, *Dragonfear Strike*, *Crushing Maul*, *Owl's Hex*, *Talon Mark*, or *Shepherd's Blessing*).

**PILLAR II — ASPECT MUTATIONS**
As a Bonus Action, you can spend 1 PE to channel an animal trait into your own body for 1 minute. You can have a maximum of 1 mutation active at a time (this increases to 2 at 11th level). An Aspect Mutation ends early if you are Incapacitated or Unconscious. Until you gain a subclass at 3rd level, you know two basic mutations:
* **Primal Senses (1 PE):** You gain Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell or hearing, and you gain Darkvision out to a range of 60 feet. *Duration: 1 hour.*
* **Beast Hide (1 PE):** If you are not wearing armor, your AC equals 13 + your Dexterity modifier. *Duration: 10 minutes.*

At 3rd level, when you choose your Mark of the Wild, you unlock your Mark's 4 specific Aspect Mutations (1 PE) as well as your Mark's powerful **Apex Aspect Mutation** (2 PE).

### **Mark of the Wild**
*3rd-Level Beast Master Feature*

In the world of Elsaither, everyone is marked by an animal spirit, whether they possess primal magic or not. Each mark represents the core personality of a character, shaping their outlook toward assigned tasks and their demeanor when facing daunting challenges. There are six primary animal marks — **Wolf**, **Dragon**, **Bear**, **Owl**, **Eagle**, and **Sheep** — though each mark encompasses a wide spectrum of allied animal spirits that share similar temperaments and primal drives.

You choose a Mark of the Wild that defines your spirit companion, your aspect mutations, and your subclass features at 3rd level and again at 6th, 7th, 10th, and 14th level.

### **Ability Score Improvement**
*4th-Level Beast Master Feature*

When you reach 4th, 8th, 12th, and 16th level, you can increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or you can increase two ability scores of your choice by 1. As normal, you can't increase an ability score above 20 using this feature. Alternatively, you can take a Feat from the Player's Handbook for which you qualify.

### **Extra Attack**
*5th-Level Beast Master Feature*

You can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn.

### **Enhanced Pillars**
*11th-Level Beast Master Feature*

Your mastery over the primal engine reaches new heights across both pillars:
* **Pillar I (Spirit Companion):** When your Spirit Companion scores a Critical Hit, the Enhanced Strike triggered by that hit costs 0 PE. Additionally, your companion's damage increases to 1d8 + your Wisdom modifier, and its Armor Class increases by 2.
* **Pillar II (Aspect Mutations):** You can now maintain up to 2 Aspect Mutations simultaneously. The duration of all your 1-PE Aspect Mutations with a 1-minute base duration increases to 10 minutes (Apex Aspect Mutations increase to 5 minutes). Whenever you manifest an Aspect Mutation, you immediately gain Temporary Hit Points equal to 2 × your Beast Master level.

### **Primal Arcanum**
*13th-Level Beast Master Feature*

Your primal mastery reaches beyond normal limits. Choose one 4th-level spell from the Savaroen spell list that is thematically appropriate to your element and Mark. You can cast that spell once per Long Rest without expending a spell slot. Alternatively, you can expend 4 Primal Essence points to cast it again. This spell is always prepared for you and does not count against your Prepared Spells total.

**Primal Arcanum (2nd)** 
*17th-Level Beast Master Feature*

Choose a second spell of 5th level from the Savaroen spell list that is thematically appropriate to your element and Mark. You can cast that spell once per Long Rest without expending a spell slot. Alternatively, you can expend 5 Primal Essence points to cast it again. It is always prepared for you and does not count against your Prepared Spells total.

### **Spirit Sentinel**
*18th-Level Beast Master Feature*

Your bond with your companion allows it to shield your allies from harm. While your Spirit Companion is within 10 feet of an allied creature, that ally has Half Cover (+2 bonus to AC and Dexterity saving throws). Additionally, when a hostile creature makes an attack roll against an ally within 10 feet of your companion, your companion can use its Reaction to interpose itself, taking the damage from the attack instead of the ally.

### **Epic Boon of the Wild**
*19th-Level Beast Master Feature*

You achieve an epic milestone in your primal connection:
* **Arcanum Ascendant.** You gain one Epic Boon feat of your choice from the Player's Handbook (2024) for which you meet the prerequisites.
* **Primal Mastery:** Your PE pool maximum increases by 6. Additionally, when you activate your Avatar of the Mark feature, its duration is 2 minutes instead of 1.

### **Avatar of the Mark**
*20th-Level Beast Master Feature*

As a Bonus Action, you can embody the absolute pinnacle of your Mark for 2 minutes (duration enhanced by Epic Boon of the Wild). You can use this feature once per Long Rest. While active, you gain the following benefits:
* **Primal Surge:** At the start of each of your turns, you regain 3 Primal Essence points.
* **Transcendent Companion:** Your Spirit Companion's Hit Point maximum is doubled, its AC increases by 2, and it acts on your initiative each round without requiring commands.
* **Aspect Apotheosis:** You gain 50 Temporary Hit Points upon activation and gain the benefits of all 4 of your Mark's Aspect Mutations simultaneously without expending PE.
* **Primal Emanation:** When you activate this form, manifest an Aspect Mutation, or trigger an Enhanced Strike, all enemies within 15 feet of you must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw against your Spell Save DC or be Frightened until the end of their next turn.
* **Elemental Apotheosis:** Your attacks and spells that deal your primary elemental damage ignore Resistance, and treat Immunity as Resistance.

## **Marks of the Wild**

In Elsaither, everyone is marked by an animal spirit, whether they possess primal magic or not. Each mark represents the core personality of a character, shaping their outlook toward assigned tasks and their demeanor when facing daunting challenges. 

There are six primary animal marks — **Wolf**, **Dragon**, **Bear**, **Owl**, **Eagle**, and **Sheep** — though each mark covers a multitude of animal spirits with shared temperaments and primal drives. 

**Allied Spirit Aspect Blending.** In Elsaither, animal marks are not rigid silos. A character marked by one of the six main spirits often manifests overlapping physical, vocal, or sensory traits from their Mark's **allied animal spirits**. For example, a Beast Master with the Mark of the Eagle might channel the razor-sharp target-lock vision and friction-free air movement of an Eagle alongside the terrifying, power-suppressing subsonic voice of an allied **Lion** spirit (as exhibited by Blaze in LotE lore). Players and DMs are encouraged to flavor their aspect mutations, companion forms, and primal features using any combination of their Mark's allied animal spirits.

A Beast Master's choice of Mark determines their spirit companion's form, their aspect mutations, their apex aspect mutation, and their subclass features at 3rd, 6th, 7th, 10th, and 14th level.

Each Mark provides at Level 3:
* **Mark Spells:** Bonus spells always prepared, 1 per spell level as you unlock them (1st at level 3, 2nd at level 5, 3rd at level 9, 4th at level 13, 5th at level 17). These do not count against your Prepared Spells total.
* **Companion Form:** Defines the visual form, passive traits, and movement of your Spirit Companion.
* **Enhanced Strike:** The 1 PE effect applied when your companion hits.
* **Apex Aspect Mutation (2 PE):** A powerful 1-minute elemental animal transformation applied to your own body.
* **Aspect Mutations (1 PE):** The 4 specific 1-PE utility and combat mutations available to you.

### **Mark of the Wolf**
*Focus, drive, discipline, relentless pursuit*

The Mark of the Wolf is a mark of focus, drive, and the unyielding will to finish the mission no matter the cost. The wolf is a creature that brings the team together, but in a disciplined way that focuses on objective success rather than sentimental bonds. Characters like Arcaeus, Ignite, and Fusion are considered wolves. They are given a mission, and they will carry that mission out no matter the cost. If they are told to stop, they will stop; if they are told to go, they will go. Wolves don’t ask too many questions unless a command directly breaches their core morals — in which case, they will turn on their former commander with terrifying fury and make sure they never see the light of day again.

*Allied Animal Spirits:* Other examples of animal spirits that fit under the Wolf mark include the Hawk, Falcon, Vortzhe, Meite, Dog, Tiger, and other similar creatures with an intense hunting drive. Your Spirit Companion, Aspect Mutations, and Apex Mutation may aesthetically manifest as any of these hunting predators.

You gain the following spells at the Beast Master levels listed. These spells are always prepared for you, and they do not count against your Prepared Spells total.

|:---:|:---|

| 5 | *Pass Without Trace* |
| 9 | *Fear* |
| 13 | *Death Ward* |
| 17 | *Hold Monster* |

#### Companion Form: Wolf
* **Speed:** 40 ft.
* **Passive Traits:** Pack Tactics (Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the wolf's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated), Keen Hearing and Smell (Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or smell).

#### Enhanced Strike: Hamstring Bite (1 PE)
On a hit, the target must make a Strength saving throw against your Spell Save DC or have its Speed reduced by 15 feet until the end of its next turn and be knocked Prone.

#### Apex Aspect Mutation: Dire Hunter (2 PE)
*Level 3 Mark of the Wolf Feature*
As a Bonus Action, you can spend 2 PE to channel the supreme hunting spirit of the wolf for 1 minute:
* **Temp HP:** You gain Temporary Hit Points equal to 3 × your Beast Master level.
* **Speed:** Your walking speed increases to 50 feet.
* **Pack Leader's Instinct:** You gain Pack Tactics, granting Advantage on your attack rolls against a creature if at least one of your allies is within 5 feet of the creature and isn't incapacitated.
* **Ferocious Charge:** Once per transformation, if you move at least 20 feet straight toward a target before hitting it with a melee attack, the target must make a Strength saving throw against your Spell Save DC or be knocked Prone.

#### Aspect Mutations
These mutations cost 1 PE and last for 1 minute unless otherwise noted:
1. **Predator's Nose:** You gain Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks using smell, and you detect the location of any creature within 30 feet that is hidden or invisible (you know the space, not the creature's exact details). *Duration: 1 hour.*
2. **Hunter's Howl:** All allies within 30 feet gain Advantage on their next attack roll before the end of your next turn. *Instant effect.*
3. **Fleet Paws:** Your Speed increases by 15 feet and you ignore difficult terrain. *Duration: 1 minute.*
4. **Shadow Runner:** You become Invisible while in dim light or darkness. The invisibility ends if you attack or cast a spell. *Duration: 1 minute.*

#### Pack Leader
*6th-Level Mark of the Wolf Feature*
You and your Spirit Companion both have the Pack Tactics trait. Additionally, when you or your companion damages a creature, allies within 10 feet of that creature can use their Reaction to move up to 10 feet toward it without provoking opportunity attacks.

#### Cull the Weak & Vengeful Retaliation
*7th-Level Mark of the Wolf Feature*
When you or your Spirit Companion reduces a creature to 0 Hit Points, you can move up to 15 feet without expending movement or using an action. This movement does not provoke opportunity attacks. 

Additionally, your unyielding drive turns with terrifying fury on those who strike your pack: whenever a creature damages you or an ally within 30 feet of you, your attacks and your companion's attacks against that creature deal an extra 1d6 damage of your primary elemental type until the end of your next turn.

#### Alpha's Command
*10th-Level Mark of the Wolf Feature*
As a Bonus Action, you can let out a commanding howl. All friendly creatures of your choice within 30 feet can immediately move up to their Speed as a Reaction. This movement does not provoke opportunity attacks. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus per Long Rest.

#### Feral Coordination
*14th-Level Mark of the Wolf Feature*
You and your Spirit Companion share a single devastating rhythm. When you hit a creature with a weapon attack or a spell attack, your Spirit Companion has Advantage on all attack rolls against that creature until the start of your next turn (and its next attack requires no PE cost). When attacking a target marked by *Hunter's Mark* or designated as your primary mission objective, your companion's attacks also ignore Resistance.

Additionally, when your Spirit Companion is reduced to 0 Hit Points, you can expend 2 Primal Essence as a Reaction to prevent it from vanishing. It returns at the start of your next turn with Hit Points equal to your Wisdom modifier × 2. You can use this Reaction once per Long Rest.

### **Mark of the Dragon**
*Passion, energy, wild rebellion, destructive power*

The Mark of the Dragon is a mark of passion, energy, and wild rebellion. The dragon is a creature that primarily works alone and focuses on completing the mission the way they deem it should be finished. Characters like Drako, Inferno, and Bobbi would be considered dragons. They are given a mission, and they will complete that mission, but they are violent and reckless in doing so. However, when a group of dragons is brought together and disciplined, they become a dangerous force of destructive power that should never be underestimated. A dragon aimed at the proper target is an unstoppable weapon of mass destruction.

*Allied Animal Spirits:* Other examples of animal spirits that fit under the Dragon mark include the Snake, Xelis, Lizard, and other reptilian or flying creatures that have a hunger for destruction. Your Spirit Companion, Aspect Mutations, and Apex Mutation may aesthetically manifest as any of these fiery or reptilian beasts.

#### Mark Spells
You gain the following spells at the Beast Master levels listed. These spells are always prepared for you, and they do not count against your Prepared Spells total.

|:---:|:---|

| 5 | *Dragon's Breath* |
| 9 | *Fear* |
| 13 | *Fire Shield* |
| 17 | *Destructive Wave* |

#### Companion Form: Wyrmling
* **Speed:** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft. (If choosing a land-based reptilian spirit such as a Serpent or Lizard, its Speed is **Walking 40 ft**, Climb 30 ft, Swim 30 ft, and it gains Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks).
* **Passive Traits:** Elemental Breath (once per Short Rest, as a Bonus Action on your command, the wyrmling exhales a 15-foot cone of your primary element. Each creature in that area must make a Dexterity saving throw against your Spell Save DC, taking 2d6 damage of your primary elemental type on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one).

#### Enhanced Strike: Dragonfear Strike (1 PE)
On a hit, the target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw against your Spell Save DC or be Frightened of you and your companion until the end of your next turn.

#### Apex Aspect Mutation: Dragonkin Aspect (2 PE)
*Level 3 Mark of the Dragon Feature*
As a Bonus Action, you can spend 2 PE to channel the terrifying draconic essence for 1 minute:
* **Temp HP:** You gain Temporary Hit Points equal to 4 × your Beast Master level.
* **Draconic Vanguard & Armor:** Your walking speed increases by 15 feet, your jump distance is tripled (assisted by spectral draconic wings that buffet the air), and your AC cannot be lower than 15 while maintaining this mutation. When falling, you can use your Reaction to glide downward up to 60 feet horizontally without taking fall damage.
* **Elemental Resistance:** You gain Resistance to your primary elemental damage type. If you already have Resistance from another source, your AC increases by 2 instead.
* **Elemental Aura:** Creatures that start their turn within 5 feet of you take 1d6 damage of your primary elemental type.
* **Reckless Rebellion:** Once per turn when you make a weapon attack or spell attack roll while maintaining this mutation, you can fight with violent, reckless passion—gaining Advantage on the attack roll, but attack rolls against you have Advantage until the start of your next turn.

#### Aspect Mutations
These mutations cost 1 PE and last for 1 minute unless otherwise noted:
1. **Draconic Scales:** If you are not wearing armor, your AC equals 13 + your Wisdom modifier. If you are already wearing armor, you add a +2 bonus to your AC. *Duration: 10 minutes.*
2. **Dragon's Breath:** You exhale a 15-foot cone of your primary element. Each creature in the cone must make a Dexterity saving throw against your Spell Save DC, taking 3d6 damage of your primary elemental type on a failure or half as much on a success. *Instant effect.*
3. **Dragon Sight:** You gain Truesight out to a range of 30 feet and Advantage on Charisma (Intimidation) checks. *Duration: 1 minute.*
4. **Wyrm's Wings:** You gain a Fly speed of 30 feet. *Duration: 1 minute.*

#### Draconic Presence
*6th-Level Mark of the Dragon Feature*
When you manifest your Dragonkin Aspect mutation or when you use Dragonfear Strike, each creature of your choice within 15 feet of you must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw against your Spell Save DC or be Frightened of you until the end of its next turn.

#### Elemental Scales
*7th-Level Mark of the Dragon Feature*
While you are not maintaining your Dragonkin Aspect mutation, you gain Resistance to your primary elemental damage type. While maintaining your Dragonkin Aspect mutation, this Resistance is already granted, so instead you gain Resistance to one additional damage type of your choice: Bludgeoning, Piercing, or Slashing.

#### Elder's Breath & Group Cataclysm
*10th-Level Mark of the Dragon Feature*
Your Dragon's Breath Aspect Mutation and your companion's Elemental Breath both increase to a 30-foot cone, and their damage increases to 5d6. Additionally, creatures that fail the saving throw against your companion's Elemental Breath are also Frightened until the end of their next turn.

Furthermore, reflecting how dragons brought together under discipline form a terrifying force of mass destruction, whenever you or your companion use a breath weapon, up to two willing allies within 15 feet of you can add 1d6 elemental damage of your primary element to their next weapon attack roll before the end of their next turn.

#### Ancient's Dominance
*14th-Level Mark of the Dragon Feature*
Your draconic aura becomes overwhelming. Creatures Frightened by any of your features or spells have Disadvantage on all saving throws they make against your abilities. Additionally, once per Long Rest, when a creature fails a saving throw against one of your spells or Mark features, you can choose to have it be Stunned until the end of its next turn instead of suffering the normal effect.

### **Mark of the Bear**
*Outer toughness, inner softness, resilience, guardian role*

The Mark of the Bear is a mark of outer toughness, inner softness, and unmatched resilience. The bear is a creature that defends its home and loved ones with intense passion, fighting hard until it can’t fight any more with a resilience unmatched by most other creatures. Characters like Glacier, Furnace, and city guardsmen would be considered bears. They are defenders of their friends, resilient and strong, nigh impossible to kill, and fight like their own lives depend on it. A bear whose cubs or loved ones are endangered is a force of nature to be reckoned with.

*Allied Animal Spirits:* Other examples of animal spirits that fit under the Bear mark include the Elephant, Whale, and other creatures that are large, resilient, and protective of their young. Your Spirit Companion, Aspect Mutations, and Apex Mutation may aesthetically manifest as any of these colossal guardian beasts.

*Level 3 Mark of the Bear Feature*

* **Mobility:** You gain a Climb speed of 30 feet.
* **Enduring Strikes:** Your melee attacks deal an extra 1d6 force damage while maintaining this mutation.
* **Thick Fur Deflection:** When you take Bludgeoning, Piercing, or Slashing damage while maintaining this mutation, you can use your Reaction to reduce that damage by your Proficiency Bonus. You can use this Reaction a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus per Long Rest.

#### Aspect Mutations
These mutations cost 1 PE and last for 1 minute unless otherwise noted:

4. **Hibernation Surge:** You immediately regain Hit Points equal to 2d10 + your Wisdom modifier. Alternatively, expressing the bear's inner softness and protective care, you can choose to grant all or part of this healing to an allied creature within 10 feet of you. You can use this mutation a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier per Long Rest (this mutation tracks specific uses, not PE). This mutation still costs 1 Primal Essence to activate, in addition to expending one of its uses.

#### Ursine Endurance
*6th-Level Mark of the Bear Feature*
When you are reduced to 0 Hit Points, you can make a Constitution saving throw with a DC of 10 + the damage taken. On a success, you drop to 1 Hit Point instead. You can use this feature once per Long Rest.

#### Guardian's Presence & Cub's Defense
*7th-Level Mark of the Bear Feature*
While you are conscious, allied creatures within 10 feet of you have Resistance to damage from effects that require a saving throw and affect multiple creatures or an area. 

Additionally, reflecting how a bear whose "cubs" or loved ones are endangered becomes a terrifying force to be reckoned with, whenever an allied creature within 30 feet of you takes damage from a hostile attack, your Spirit Companion can use its Reaction to move up to half its Speed toward the attacker without provoking opportunity attacks, and your next attack roll against that attacker before the end of your next turn is made with Advantage. 

#### Immovable
*10th-Level Mark of the Bear Feature*
You cannot be moved against your will. No forced movement, shoves, or spells that physically relocate you affect you unless you choose to allow them. Additionally, you have Advantage on Strength saving throws. Your spirit companion also gains this benefit while it is within 10 feet of you.

#### Last Stand
*14th-Level Mark of the Bear Feature*
While you are at half your maximum Hit Points or fewer (excluding Temporary Hit Points), your melee attacks deal an additional 1d8 damage of your primary elemental type, and you have Advantage on Constitution saving throws. Additionally, the aura of your Guardian's Presence feature increases to 15 feet and now applies to all damage, not just area effects (allies within 15 feet have Resistance to the first source of damage that hits them each round).

### **Mark of the Owl**

They are healers who use wisdom to bring health to others, as well as deadly assassins and helpful allies. Mysterious and elusive, the owl can be a healer to some and a death mark for others.

Your Spirit Companion, Aspect Mutations, and Apex Mutation may aesthetically manifest as any of these secretive night predators.

The Mark of the Eagle is a mark of hope, discipline, and honor. The eagle is the creature that symbolizes freedom, which inspires hope and pride in those who see it. Eagles are natural leaders who bring their team together in a way that is useful and powerful. They are honorable knights in that they fight for a cause that they believe in, knowing their own limits and the limits of their team. An eagle whose eye is trained on success will almost always see it to fulfillment.

*Allied Animal Spirits:* Other examples of animal spirits that fit under the Eagle mark include the Lion, Griffin, Peacock, Horse, and other majestic creatures that are symbols of hope or honor. ### **Mark of the Sheep**

The Mark of the Sheep embodies loyalty, unity, and a gentle demeanor. Sheep are team players, fostering harmony within the group while prioritizing the collective goal over individual pursuits. Assigned a task, they execute it diligently, following instructions without hesitation. Characters like common soldiers, Mary, and townspeople are considered sheep. Beast Masters of the Sheep are the paladins of the wild — stalwart defenders who command restorative magic and weather the harshest storms to keep their allies safe. Do not mistake their gentle bearing for weakness; a Ram's charge has shattered armies.

*Allied Animal Spirits:* Other animal spirits akin to the Sheep include the Goat, Alpaca, Koi, Floop, Krenishee, and other gentle creatures known for their cooperative nature.
