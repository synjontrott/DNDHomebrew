# **Beast Master**

*Those who walk between worlds — speaking the language of wind and root, claw and scale — the Beast Masters of Elsaither are primal conduits between civilization and the untamed wild. Bound to the spirit of a living archetype, they do not merely fight alongside beasts; they become them. Revered among the Garanko for their ferocity and among the Plunesako for their wisdom, Beast Masters can be found in every corner of Elsaither, from the shadowed Ninjonian forests to the elemental plateaus of the Warrion highlands.*

**Primary Ability:** Wisdom
**Hit Die:** 1d8 per Beast Master level
**Hit Points at 1st Level:** 8 + your Constitution modifier
**Hit Points at Higher Levels:** 1d8 (or 5) + your Constitution modifier per level after 1st.

**Proficiencies**
**Armor:** Light Armor, Medium Armor, Shields
**Weapons:** Simple Weapons, Martial Weapons
**Tools:** Herbalism Kit
**Saving Throws:** Wisdom, Constitution
**Skills:** Choose two from Animal Handling, Athletics, Insight, Nature, Perception, Stealth, or Survival.

**Equipment**
(a) a regional Martial Weapon or (b) two Simple Weapons
(a) regional Light or Medium Armor or (b) Leather Armor and a Shield
(a) Explorer's Pack or (b) Dungeoneer's Pack
A Herbalism Kit and 20cc (or 50cc starting gold)

## **Core Class Features**

### **Spellcasting**
*1st-Level Beast Master Feature*

You have learned to draw upon the magic of the natural world.

**Primal Bond.** Choose one elemental affinity from the following list: Fire, Water, Air, Lightning, Earth, Ice, Light (Laser), Bone, Gas, Metal, Blade, Poison, Death, Shadow, Cosmic Shadow, or Void. This choice reflects the primal spirit that animates your Mark. You gain Resistance to that element's primary damage type (as listed in Chapter 1: The Elemental System).

**Deity Blessing (Optional).** At 1st level, you may dedicate yourself to one of the Deities of the Elsaither (see Chapter 6). Choosing a Deity is optional, but doing so grants you access to sacred magic tied to that god's domain.
*   **Requirement:** Your element must match the element listed for your chosen Deity's cantrip, or your DM must approve the connection as lore-appropriate.
*   **Deity Cantrip:** You learn your Deity's **Deity Cantrip** (listed at the start of each deity's section in Chapter 6). This cantrip does not count against your Cantrips Known total. You use Wisdom as your spellcasting ability for it.
*   **Deity's Unique Spells:** Each Deity has unique spells. These spells are always prepared for you and do not count against your Prepared Spells total. You can cast them using your Beast Master spell slots of the appropriate level. You gain access to each Unique Spell only when you can cast spells of that level.
*   **Changing Deities:** With your DM's permission and a significant story moment (such as a pilgrimage, ordeal, or act of devotion), you may switch to a different Deity. You lose the previous Deity's benefits and gain the new Deity's.

**Cantrips.** You learn two cantrips of your choice from the Savaroen spell list. You learn additional cantrips at higher levels, as shown in the Cantrips Known column of the Beast Master table.

**Spells Known.** You know two 1st-level spells of your choice from the Savaroen spell list. The Spells Known column of the Beast Master table shows when you learn more spells. Each of these spells must be of a level for which you have spell slots. 

**Preparing and Casting Spells.** The Beast Master table shows how many spell slots you have to cast your spells of 1st level and higher. You regain all expended spell slots when you finish a Long Rest. 

**Spellcasting Ability.** Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for your Beast Master spells.
* **Spell Save DC** = 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your Wisdom modifier
* **Spell Attack modifier** = your Proficiency Bonus + your Wisdom modifier

### **Weapon Mastery**
*1st-Level Beast Master Feature*

Your training with weapons allows you to use their special properties. You gain the Weapon Mastery property of two weapons you are proficient with. Whenever you finish a Long Rest, you can change one of those weapon choices to another weapon you are proficient with.

### **Primal Engine**
*2nd-Level Beast Master Feature*

You tap into a deep reservoir of natural energy called a Primal Essence Pool (PE). Your pool has a maximum number of points equal to your Beast Master level + your Wisdom modifier. You regain all expended PE when you finish a Long Rest. Once per Long Rest, when you finish a Short Rest, you can recover a number of expended PE equal to your Wisdom modifier.

You can expend these points to fuel three primal pillars:

**PILLAR I — SPIRIT COMPANION**
You are permanently accompanied by a beast companion. Until you choose a Mark of the Wild at 3rd level, this takes the form of a generic primal beast.
* **Statistics:** Its Hit Point maximum equals 5 times your Beast Master level + your Wisdom modifier. Its Armor Class is 12 + your Proficiency Bonus. Its Attack bonus equals your spell attack modifier, and its damage is 1d6 + your Wisdom modifier. Its Speed is 40 feet (unless defined by your Companion Form). Its saving throws use your Spell Save DC.
* **Revival:** If reduced to 0 Hit Points, your companion vanishes and returns at the end of your next Long Rest.
* **Actions:** The companion acts on your turn. It takes the Dodge or Help action automatically (no action required by you).
* **Command (1 PE):** As a Bonus Action, you can spend 1 PE to command your companion to take the Attack action, using your spell attack modifier.
* **Enhanced Strike (1 PE):** When your companion hits with an attack, you can spend 1 PE (no action required) to apply your Mark's Enhanced Strike effect. Both Command and Enhanced Strike can be used on the same trigger for a total of 2 PE.

**PILLAR II — ASPECT MUTATIONS**
As a Bonus Action, you can spend 1 PE to channel an animal trait into your own body for 1 minute. You can have a maximum of 1 mutation active at a time (this increases to 2 at 11th level). Until you gain a subclass at 3rd level, you know two basic mutations:
* **Primal Senses:** You gain Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell or hearing, and you gain Darkvision out to a range of 60 feet. Duration: 1 hour.
* **Beast Hide:** If you are not wearing armor, your AC equals 13 + your Dexterity modifier.

**PILLAR III — WILDSHAPE**
As an Action, you can spend 2 PE to assume your Mark's Wildshape Form (defined at 3rd level; this feature is inactive until then).
* You gain Temporary Hit Points as defined by your Wildshape Form.
* You retain your mental ability scores (Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma), class features, and spellcasting ability. 
* Your equipment merges into your new form and has no effect until you leave the form.
* You can speak and cast spells normally while in this form.
* The transformation ends when your Temporary Hit Points from this feature drop to 0, after 1 minute has passed, or if you use a Bonus Action to dismiss it.
* You can spend 1 PE (no action required) to extend the duration by 1 minute.

### **Mark of the Wild**
*3rd-Level Beast Master Feature*

You choose a Mark of the Wild that defines your spirit companion, your aspect mutations, and your wildshape. Your choice grants you features at 3rd level and again at 6th, 7th, 10th, and 14th level.

### **Ability Score Improvement**
*4th-Level Beast Master Feature*

When you reach 4th, 8th, 12th, and 16th level, you can increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or you can increase two ability scores of your choice by 1. As normal, you can't increase an ability score above 20 using this feature. Alternatively, you can take a Feat from the Player's Handbook for which you qualify.

### **Extra Attack**
*5th-Level Beast Master Feature*

You can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn.

### **Enhanced Pillars**
*11th-Level Beast Master Feature*

Your mastery over the primal engine reaches new heights:
* **Pillar I:** When your Spirit Companion scores a Critical Hit, the Enhanced Strike triggered by that hit costs 0 PE. Additionally, your companion's damage increases to 1d8 + your Wisdom modifier.
* **Pillar II:** You can now maintain up to 2 Aspect Mutations simultaneously. The duration of all your mutations increases to 10 minutes.
* **Pillar III:** Your Wildshape duration increases to 10 minutes (requiring no PE to extend). The Temporary Hit Points you gain from your Wildshape increase by 50 percent (rounded down). Furthermore, you can now cast your Primal Arcanum spells while in Wildshape form.

### **Primal Arcanum**
*13th-Level Beast Master Feature*

Your primal mastery reaches beyond normal limits. Choose one 4th-level or 5th-level spell you know or one from the Savaroen spell list that is thematically appropriate to your element and Mark. You can cast that spell a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus per Long Rest without expending a spell slot. This spell is always prepared and does not count against your Spells Known.

**Primal Arcanum (2nd)** 
*17th-Level Beast Master Feature*

Choose a second 4th-level or 5th-level spell, different from the one chosen at 13th level, from the Savaroen spell list that is thematically appropriate to your element and Mark. You can cast that spell a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus per Long Rest without expending a spell slot. It is always prepared and does not count against your Spells Known.

### **Epic Boon of the Wild**
*19th-Level Beast Master Feature*

You achieve an epic milestone in your primal connection:
* **Arcanum Ascendant.** You gain one Epic Boon feat of your choice from the Player's Handbook (2024) for which you meet the prerequisites.
* **Primal Mastery:** Your PE pool maximum increases by 6. Additionally, when you activate your Avatar of the Mark feature, its duration is 2 minutes instead of 1.

### **Avatar of the Mark**
*20th-Level Beast Master Feature*

As a Bonus Action, you can embody the absolute pinnacle of your Mark for 2 minutes (duration enhanced by Epic Boon of the Wild). You can use this feature once per Long Rest. While active, you gain the following benefits:
* **Primal Surge:** At the start of each of your turns, you regain 3 Primal Essence points.
* **Transcendent Form:** You gain 50 additional Temporary Hit Points when you enter your Wildshape Form during this feature (this does not stack with the normal Wildshape Temp HP; it replaces the doubling). Your Spirit Companion's Hit Point maximum is doubled, and it acts on your initiative each round without requiring commands.
* **Primal Emanation:** Each time you enter Wildshape or trigger an Enhanced Strike, all enemies within 15 feet of you must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw against your Spell Save DC or be Frightened until the end of their next turn.
* **Elemental Apotheosis:** Your attacks and spells that deal your primary elemental damage ignore Resistance, and treat Immunity as Resistance.

## **Marks of the Wild**
At 3rd level, you choose a Mark of the Wild that defines your spirit companion, your aspect mutations, and your wildshape. Your Mark grants you features at 3rd, 6th, 7th, 10th, and 14th level.

Each Mark provides at Level 3:
* **Mark Spells:** Bonus spells always prepared, 1 per spell level as you unlock them (1st at level 3, 2nd at level 5, 3rd at level 9, 4th at level 13, 5th at level 17). These do not count against your Spells Known.
* **Companion Form:** Defines the visual form, passive traits, and movement of your Spirit Companion.
* **Enhanced Strike:** The 1 PE effect applied when your companion hits.
* **Wildshape Form:** Defines Temp HP, speed modifications, and passive buffs in beast form.
* **Aspect Mutations:** The 4 specific 1-PE mutations available to you.

### **Mark of the Wolf**
*Speed, pack coordination, pursuit, hunting*

**Mark Spells**
* **1st-Level:** *Hunter's Mark* (Level 3)
* **2nd-Level:** *Pass Without Trace* (Level 5)
* **3rd-Level:** *Fear* (Level 9)
* **4th-Level:** *Death Ward* (Level 13)
* **5th-Level:** *Hold Monster* (Level 17)

**Companion Form: Wolf**
* **Speed:** 40 ft.
* **Passive Traits:** Pack Tactics (advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the wolf's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated), Keen Hearing and Smell (Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or smell).

**Enhanced Strike: Hamstring Bite (1 PE)**
On a hit, the target must make a Strength saving throw against your Spell Save DC or have its Speed reduced by 15 feet and be knocked Prone.

**Wildshape Form: Dire Wolf**
* **Temp HP:** 3 × your Beast Master level
* **Speed:** 50 ft.
* **Passive Buffs:** You gain Pack Tactics, which applies to your attacks in this form.
* **Ferocious Charge:** Once per Wildshape, if you move at least 20 feet straight toward a target before attacking, the target must make a Strength saving throw against your Spell Save DC or be knocked Prone (in addition to the attack's damage).

**Aspect Mutations**
These mutations cost 1 PE and last for 1 minute unless otherwise noted:
1. **Predator's Nose:** You gain Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks using smell, and you detect the location of any creature within 30 feet that is hidden or invisible (you know the space, not the creature's exact details). Duration: 1 hour.
2. **Hunter's Howl:** All allies within 30 feet gain Advantage on their next attack roll before the end of your next turn. This mutation is an instant trigger and has no sustained duration.
3. **Fleet Paws:** Your Speed increases by 15 feet and you ignore difficult terrain. Duration: 1 minute.
4. **Shadow Runner:** You become Invisible while in dim light or darkness. The invisibility ends if you attack or cast a spell. Duration: 1 minute.

**Pack Leader**
*6th-Level Mark of the Wolf Feature*
You and your Spirit Companion both have the Pack Tactics trait. Additionally, when you or your companion damages a creature, allies within 10 feet of that creature can use their Reaction to move up to 10 feet toward it without provoking opportunity attacks.

**Cull the Weak**
*7th-Level Mark of the Wolf Feature*
When you or your Spirit Companion reduces a creature to 0 Hit Points, you can move up to 15 feet as a free action (not requiring an Action or Bonus Action). This movement does not provoke opportunity attacks.

**Alpha's Command**
*10th-Level Mark of the Wolf Feature*
As a Bonus Action, you can let out a commanding howl. All friendly creatures of your choice within 30 feet can immediately move up to their Speed as a Reaction. This movement does not provoke opportunity attacks. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus per Long Rest.

**Feral Coordination**
*14th-Level Mark of the Wolf Feature*
You and your Spirit Companion share a single devastating rhythm. When you hit a creature with a weapon attack or a spell attack, your Spirit Companion has Advantage on all attack rolls against that creature until the start of your next turn (and its next attack requires no PE cost). Additionally, when your Spirit Companion is reduced to 0 Hit Points, you can expend 2 Primal Essence as a Reaction to prevent it from vanishing. It returns at the start of your next turn with Hit Points equal to your Wisdom modifier × 2. You can use this Reaction once per Long Rest.

### **Mark of the Dragon**
*Elemental dominance, breath weapons, fear, scales, power*

**Mark Spells**
* **1st-Level:** *Absorb Elements* (Level 3)
* **2nd-Level:** *Dragon's Breath* (Level 5)
* **3rd-Level:** *Fear* (Level 9)
* **4th-Level:** *Fire Shield* (Level 13)
* **5th-Level:** *Destructive Wave* (Level 17)

**Companion Form: Wyrmling**
* **Speed:** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft.
* **Passive Traits:** Elemental Breath (once per Short Rest, as a Bonus Action on your command, the wyrmling exhales a 15-foot cone of your primary element. Each creature in that area must make a Dexterity saving throw against your Spell Save DC, taking 2d6 elemental damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one).

**Enhanced Strike: Dragonfear Strike (1 PE)**
On a hit, the target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw against your Spell Save DC or be Frightened of you and your companion until the end of your next turn.

**Wildshape Form: Dragonkin**
* **Temp HP:** 4 × your Beast Master level
* **Speed:** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft.
* **Passive Buffs:** You gain Resistance to your primary elemental damage type. If you already have Resistance to this type from another source, the damage is halved twice (treating it as double Resistance; see the 2024 rules for halving damage). You also gain Natural Armor, ensuring your AC cannot be lower than 15 while in this form.
* **Elemental Aura:** Creatures that start their turn within 5 feet of you take 1d6 damage of your primary elemental type.

**Aspect Mutations**
These mutations cost 1 PE and last for 1 minute unless otherwise noted:
1. **Draconic Scales:** If you are not wearing armor, your AC equals 13 + your Wisdom modifier. If you are already wearing armor, you add a +2 bonus to your AC. Duration: 10 minutes.
2. **Dragon's Breath:** You exhale a 15-foot cone of your primary element. Each creature in the cone must make a Dexterity saving throw against your Spell Save DC, taking 3d6 elemental damage on a failure or half as much on a success. This mutation is an instant trigger and has no sustained duration.
3. **Dragon Sight:** You gain Truesight out to a range of 30 feet and Advantage on Charisma (Intimidation) checks. Duration: 1 minute.
4. **Wyrm's Wings:** You gain a Fly speed of 30 feet. Duration: 1 minute.

**Draconic Presence**
*6th-Level Mark of the Dragon Feature*
When you enter your Wildshape Form or when you use Dragonfear Strike, each creature of your choice within 15 feet of you must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw against your Spell Save DC or be Frightened of you until the end of its next turn.

**Elemental Scales**
*7th-Level Mark of the Dragon Feature*
While you are not in your Wildshape Form, you gain Resistance to your primary elemental damage type. While you are in your Wildshape Form, this Resistance is already granted, so instead you gain Resistance to one additional damage type of your choice: Bludgeoning, Piercing, or Slashing.

**Elder's Breath**
*10th-Level Mark of the Dragon Feature*
Your Dragon's Breath Aspect Mutation and your companion's Elemental Breath both increase to a 30-foot cone, and their damage increases to 5d6. Additionally, creatures that fail the saving throw against your companion's Elemental Breath are also Frightened until the end of their next turn.

**Ancient's Dominance**
*14th-Level Mark of the Dragon Feature*
Your draconic aura becomes overwhelming. Creatures Frightened by any of your features or spells have Disadvantage on all saving throws they make against your abilities. Additionally, once per Long Rest, when a creature fails a saving throw against one of your spells or Mark features, you can choose to have it be Stunned until the end of its next turn instead of suffering the normal effect.

### **Mark of the Bear**
*Endurance, damage mitigation, strength, guardian role, tanking*

**Mark Spells**
* **1st-Level:** *Thunderwave* (Level 3)
* **2nd-Level:** *Enhance Ability* (Level 5)
* **3rd-Level:** *Erupting Earth* (Level 9)
* **4th-Level:** *Stoneskin* (Level 13)
* **5th-Level:** *Maelstrom* (Level 17)

**Companion Form: Brown Bear**
* **Speed:** 40 ft., Climb 30 ft.
* **Passive Traits:** Powerful Build (counts as one size larger when determining carrying capacity and the weight it can push, drag, or lift, as well as for grappling and shoving). 
* **Maul:** When your companion hits the same target twice in one turn, the target must make a Strength saving throw against your Spell Save DC or be Grappled until the start of your next turn.

**Enhanced Strike: Crushing Maul (1 PE)**
On a hit, the target must make a Strength saving throw against your Spell Save DC or be Grappled by your companion (the companion holds the target and requires no action to maintain it). The Grappled target is also Restrained while your companion maintains the grapple. Your companion can hold only one creature this way at a time.

**Wildshape Form: Cave Bear**
* **Temp HP:** 3 × your Beast Master level
* **Speed:** 40 ft., Climb 30 ft. (If your normal walking speed was faster, it is reduced by 5 feet).
* **Passive Buffs:** When you take Bludgeoning, Piercing, or Slashing damage while in this form, you can use your Reaction to reduce that damage by your Proficiency Bonus. You can use this Reaction a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all uses when you finish a Long Rest.
* **Enduring Strikes:** Your melee attacks deal an extra 1d6 force damage while in this form.

**Aspect Mutations**
These mutations cost 1 PE and last for 1 minute unless otherwise noted:
1. **Bear Hide:** You gain Resistance to Bludgeoning damage. Duration: 1 minute.
2. **Iron Stomach:** You gain Immunity to the Poisoned condition and Resistance to Poison damage. Duration: 1 hour.
3. **Mighty Roar:** Each creature of your choice within 30 feet must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw against your Spell Save DC or be Frightened until they are more than 30 feet away from you. Creatures that succeed on the save are immune to this mutation for 24 hours. This mutation is an instant effect.
4. **Hibernation Surge:** You immediately regain Hit Points equal to 2d10 + your Wisdom modifier. You can use this mutation a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier per Long Rest (this mutation tracks specific uses, not PE). This mutation still costs 1 Primal Essence to activate, in addition to expending one of its uses.

**Ursine Endurance**
*6th-Level Mark of the Bear Feature*
When you are reduced to 0 Hit Points, you can make a Constitution saving throw with a DC of 10 + the damage taken. On a success, you drop to 1 Hit Point instead. You can use this feature once per Long Rest.

**Guardian's Presence**
*7th-Level Mark of the Bear Feature*
While you are conscious, allied creatures within 10 feet of you have Resistance to damage from effects that require a saving throw and affect multiple creatures or an area. 

**Immovable**
*10th-Level Mark of the Bear Feature*
You cannot be moved against your will. No forced movement, shoves, or spells that physically relocate you affect you unless you choose to allow them. Additionally, you have Advantage on Strength saving throws. Your spirit companion also gains this benefit while it is within 10 feet of you.

**Last Stand**
*14th-Level Mark of the Bear Feature*
While you are at half your maximum Hit Points or fewer (excluding Temporary Hit Points), your melee attacks deal an additional 1d8 damage of your primary elemental type, and you have Advantage on Constitution saving throws. Additionally, the aura of your Guardian's Presence feature increases to 15 feet and now applies to all damage, not just area effects (allies within 15 feet have Resistance to the first source of damage that hits them each round).

### **Mark of the Owl**
*Stealth, wisdom, observation, hexes, battlefield control*

The Mark of the Owl embodies the essence of stealth, wisdom, and precise observation. Beast Masters who bear this mark strike from the shadows, controlling the battlefield with curses and debilitating magic while soaring silently above the fray.

#### Mark Spells

You gain the following spells at the Beast Master levels listed. These spells are always prepared for you, and they do not count against the number of spells you know.

**Mark of the Owl Spells**

| Beast Master Level | Spell |
|:---:|:---|
| 3 | *Hex* |
| 5 | *Invisibility* |
| 9 | *Hypnotic Pattern* |
| 13 | *Arcane Eye* |
| 17 | *Scrying* |

#### Companion Form

*Level 3 Mark of the Owl Feature*

Your Spirit Companion takes the form of a Giant Owl. It gains the following traits:
* **Speed:** 5 feet, Fly 60 feet.
* **Flyby:** The companion doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks when it flies out of an enemy's reach.
* **Silent Flight:** The companion's movement makes no sound; enemies cannot use hearing to locate it.
* **Keen Hearing and Sight:** The companion has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or sight.

#### Enhanced Strike

*Level 3 Mark of the Owl Feature*

When your Spirit Companion hits a creature with an attack, you can expend 1 Primal Essence to apply the **Owl's Hex**. The target is Hexed until the end of your next turn. While Hexed, the target has Disadvantage on the next saving throw it makes against one of your spells or Beast Master class features before the end of your next turn. Only one creature can be Hexed by this feature at a time.

#### Wildshape Form: Owlkin

*Level 3 Mark of the Owl Feature*

When you assume your Wildshape Form, you take on the traits of an Owlkin. You gain the following benefits:
* **Temporary Hit Points:** You gain Temporary Hit Points equal to 3 × your Beast Master level.
* **Speed:** Your walking speed becomes 10 feet, and you gain a Fly speed of 40 feet.
* **Silent Movement:** You make no sound while moving in this form. Enemies cannot use their Passive Perception to detect your movement.
* **Darkvision:** You have Darkvision with a range of 120 feet while in this form.
* **Spellhunter Gaze:** Once per Wildshape, when a creature you can see casts a spell, you can use your Reaction to make an Intelligence (Arcana) check (DC 10 + the spell's level). On a successful check, you learn what spell is being cast before it takes effect.

#### Aspect Mutations

*Level 3 Mark of the Owl Feature*

You gain access to the following Aspect Mutations. As a Bonus Action, you can expend 1 Primal Essence to manifest one of these mutations:

* **Owl Eyes:** You gain Darkvision with a range of 120 feet. You can see through magical darkness within 30 feet of you. *Duration: 1 hour.*
* **Silent Wings:** Your movement makes no sound, and you have Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks. *Duration: 1 minute.*
* **Night Shroud:** You cast *Darkness* centered on yourself without expending a spell slot or requiring components. You can see normally through this darkness. *Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute.*
* **Witchlight:** You can sense the surface emotions (such as fear, aggression, grief, or joy) of all creatures within 30 feet of you. You also know whether each creature in range is Charmed, Frightened, or Hexed. *Duration: 10 minutes.*

#### Wisdom's Eye

*Level 6 Mark of the Owl Feature*

Your primal awareness expands. You cannot be Surprised. Additionally, once per Short or Long Rest, you can grant yourself or one creature you can see within 30 feet the ability to act first in Initiative. You do not roll; the chosen creature simply takes the first turn in the order. Other creatures that have tied for first are resolved normally after this creature's turn.

#### Moonlit Observer

*Level 7 Mark of the Owl Feature*

As a Bonus Action, you can extend your primal sight to pierce through illusions. You gain Truesight with a range of 30 feet until the start of your next turn. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

#### Spellhunter

*Level 10 Mark of the Owl Feature*

When you deal damage to a creature with a spell or cantrip, that creature can't take a Reaction until the start of its next turn. Additionally, creatures Hexed by your Owl's Hex feature have Disadvantage on Constitution saving throws made to maintain Concentration.

#### Predator's Hex

*Level 14 Mark of the Owl Feature*

Your Owl's Hex deepens into a lethal curse. While a creature is affected by your Owl's Hex, the next time you deal damage to it with a spell, you deal additional damage equal to your Beast Master level. 

Additionally, the duration of Owl's Hex is extended: it lasts until the end of your next turn, or until the creature succeeds on a Wisdom saving throw against your Spell Save DC. A creature can make this saving throw as an Action on its turn. A creature that successfully breaks the Hex with this save cannot be Hexed again for 24 hours.

#### Companion Form

*Level 3 Mark of the Eagle Feature*

Your Spirit Companion takes the form of a Giant Eagle. It gains the following traits:
* **Speed:** 10 feet, Fly 80 feet.
* **Keen Sight:** The companion has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.
* **Swooping Strike:** Once per round, when the companion flies at least 20 feet straight toward a target before hitting it with a melee attack, the attack deals an extra 1d6 Piercing damage. The target must succeed on a Strength saving throw against your Spell Save DC or be knocked Prone.

#### Enhanced Strike

*Level 3 Mark of the Eagle Feature*

When your Spirit Companion hits a creature with an attack, you can expend 1 Primal Essence to apply the **Talon Mark**. The target is Marked until the start of your next turn. Your Spirit Companion has Advantage on all attack rolls against a Marked target. Additionally, the Marked target's Speed is reduced by 10 feet.

#### Wildshape Form: Eagleborn

*Level 3 Mark of the Eagle Feature*

When you assume your Wildshape Form, you take on the traits of an Eagleborn. You gain the following benefits:
* **Temporary Hit Points:** You gain Temporary Hit Points equal to 3 × your Beast Master level.
* **Speed:** Your walking speed becomes 10 feet, and you gain a Fly speed of 60 feet.
* **Hawkeye Precision:** You have Advantage on ranged weapon attacks and ranged spell attacks while in this form.
* **Diving Strike:** Once per Wildshape, if you move at least 20 feet in a straight line and then hit with a melee attack, you deal an extra 2d8 damage of the attack's type, and the target must succeed on a Strength saving throw against your Spell Save DC or be knocked Prone.

#### Aspect Mutations

*Level 3 Mark of the Eagle Feature*

You gain access to the following Aspect Mutations. As a Bonus Action, you can expend 1 Primal Essence to manifest one of these mutations:

* **Eagle Eyes:** You have Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks. You ignore penalties from weather conditions (such as fog, rain, or wind) for Perception checks or ranged attack rolls. You see normally in dim light. *Duration: 1 hour.*
* **Wind Rider:** You gain a Fly speed equal to your walking speed. *Duration: 1 minute.*
* **Piercing Screech:** You emit a shattering cry. Each creature within 20 feet of you must make a Constitution saving throw against your Spell Save DC. On a failed save, a creature is Deafened until the end of its next turn and has Disadvantage on its next attack roll. *Instant effect.*
* **Precision Dive:** Until the start of your next turn, if you move at least 20 feet before making a weapon attack or ranged spell attack, that attack is made with Advantage and deals an extra 2d6 damage on a hit. *Duration: Until triggered or the start of your next turn.*

#### Aerial Mastery

*Level 6 Mark of the Eagle Feature*

You gain a Fly speed equal to your walking speed. While flying, Opportunity Attacks against you are made with Disadvantage.

#### Precision Hunter

*Level 7 Mark of the Eagle Feature*

When you make a ranged weapon attack or ranged spell attack against a creature that is 30 or more feet away from you, the attack deals an additional 1d6 Piercing damage on a hit. Additionally, ranged attacks you make do not suffer Disadvantage from being within 5 feet of a hostile creature.

#### Screaming Dive

*Level 10 Mark of the Eagle Feature*

Once per Short or Long Rest, when you take the Dash action as part of your movement and then make a melee attack on the same turn against a creature you flew toward, that attack automatically scores a Critical Hit if it hits. You still roll the attack to determine if you successfully hit the target.

#### Sky Sovereign

*Level 14 Mark of the Eagle Feature*

While you are flying (from any source), your first attack on each of your turns is made with Advantage. Additionally, Opportunity Attacks made against you while you are flying are made with Disadvantage. Your Spirit Companion's Fly speed increases by 20 feet, and it gains Advantage on all attack rolls while flying.

### **Mark of the Sheep**
*Support, restoration, guardian-healer, the unexpected iron shepherd*

The Mark of the Sheep embodies the endurance, quiet protection, and fierce guardianship of the flock. Beast Masters of the Sheep are the paladins of the wild — stalwart defenders who command restorative magic and weather the harshest storms to keep their allies safe. Do not mistake their gentle bearing for weakness; a Ram's charge has shattered armies. 

#### Mark Spells

You gain the following spells at the Beast Master levels listed. These spells are always prepared for you, and they do not count against the number of spells you know.

**Mark of the Sheep Spells**

| Beast Master Level | Spell |
|:---:|:---|
| 3 | *Cure Wounds* |
| 5 | *Lesser Restoration* |
| 9 | *Beacon of Hope* |
| 13 | *Death Ward* |
| 17 | *Mass Cure Wounds* |

#### Companion Form

*Level 3 Mark of the Sheep Feature*

Your Spirit Companion takes the form of a Great Ram. It gains the following traits:
* **Speed:** 50 feet, Climb 30 feet.
* **Charge:** If the companion moves at least 20 feet straight toward a target and then hits it with a melee attack on the same turn, the attack deals an extra 2d6 Bludgeoning damage. The target must succeed on a Strength saving throw against your Spell Save DC or be pushed 10 feet away and knocked Prone.
* **Flock Bond:** While the companion is within 30 feet of you, it has Advantage on saving throws against being Charmed or Frightened.

#### Enhanced Strike

*Level 3 Mark of the Sheep Feature*

When your Spirit Companion hits a creature with an attack, you can expend 1 Primal Essence to apply the **Shepherd's Blessing**. You and one ally you can see within 30 feet each immediately regain Hit Points equal to your Wisdom modifier + your Proficiency Bonus. The target of the companion's attack cannot benefit from this healing.

#### Wildshape Form: Giant Ram

*Level 3 Mark of the Sheep Feature*

When you assume your Wildshape Form, you take on the traits of a Giant Ram. You gain the following benefits:
* **Temporary Hit Points:** You gain Temporary Hit Points equal to 4 × your Beast Master level.
* **Speed:** Your walking speed becomes 50 feet, and you gain a Climb speed of 30 feet.
* **Aura of the Flock:** Allies within 10 feet of you regain Hit Points equal to your Wisdom modifier at the start of each of your turns.
* **Resilient Wool:** You have Resistance to Cold and Fire damage while in this form.
* **Horn Smash:** Your melee attacks in this form deal 1d8 + your Wisdom modifier Bludgeoning damage. On a hit, you can push the target 5 feet away from you.

#### Aspect Mutations

*Level 3 Mark of the Sheep Feature*

You gain access to the following Aspect Mutations. As a Bonus Action, you can expend 1 Primal Essence to manifest one of these mutations:

* **Shepherd's Call:** You cast *Healing Word* without expending a spell slot, using your Wisdom modifier as the spellcasting ability. The spell heals an amount equal to 1d4 + your Wisdom modifier. You can use this mutation once per Short Rest. Activating this mutation still costs 1 Primal Essence.
* **Wool of the Wild:** You gain Resistance to Cold and Fire damage, and you are immune to the effects of extreme cold or extreme heat environments. *Duration: 1 hour.*
* **Curling Horns:** You manifest a pair of massive horns. Your unarmed strikes deal 1d8 Bludgeoning damage. On a hit, you push the target 5 feet away from you, unless they succeed on a Strength saving throw against your Spell Save DC. *Duration: 1 minute.*
* **Verdant Field:** You create a 20-foot-radius sphere of primal energy centered on you that moves with you. Difficult terrain within the sphere does not affect you or allied creatures. Allied creatures within the sphere have Advantage on saving throws against the Frightened condition. *Duration: 1 minute.*

#### Shepherding Aura

*Level 6 Mark of the Sheep Feature*

While you are conscious and not in your Wildshape Form, allies within 15 feet of you who are below half their Hit Point maximum regain Hit Points equal to your Wisdom modifier at the start of each of your turns.

#### Stoic Resilience

*Level 7 Mark of the Sheep Feature*

You and allied creatures within 15 feet of you cannot be Frightened while you are conscious. If a creature is already Frightened when it enters this aura, the condition is suppressed while the creature remains within range.

#### Restorative Fleece

*Level 10 Mark of the Sheep Feature*

When your Wildshape Form ends (whether voluntarily or when your Temporary Hit Points are depleted), all allied creatures within 30 feet of you immediately regain Hit Points equal to 2d8 + your Wisdom modifier.

#### Nature's Pardon

*Level 14 Mark of the Sheep Feature*

Once per Long Rest, when an allied creature you can see within 60 feet of you drops to 0 Hit Points, you can use your Reaction to restore 1 Hit Point to that creature, preventing it from falling Unconscious. The creature also gains Temporary Hit Points equal to your Beast Master level + your Wisdom modifier.
