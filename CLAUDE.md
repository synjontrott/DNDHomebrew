# D&D Homebrew: Legends of the Elsaither (LotE) — Claude Guide

> **Cross-Reference:** This file works alongside [`GEMINI.md`](./GEMINI.md) and [`AGENTS.md`](./AGENTS.md). All three files share the same project mandates; refer to them together for full context.

## Project Objective
Create a perfectly balanced D&D Homebrew guidebook based on LotE Lore. The result should be fun to play and complement the standard D&D 2024 classes as if it were a canon rulebook.

## Core Mandates
- **2024 Compatibility:** All content must be built for and balanced against the D&D 2024 rules (PHB, DMG, MM).
- **Balance:** Prioritize mathematical fairness and progression consistency. Ensure homebrew options do not overshadow or underperform compared to official options.
- **Lore Fidelity:** All descriptions, names, and mechanics must align with the "Legends of the Elsaither" campaign setting.
- **Technical Writing:** Use the standard wording and syntax found in official D&D sourcebooks (e.g., proper capitalization of game terms, clear action economy).

## Subagent Roles (see [`AGENTS.md`](./AGENTS.md) for full definitions)
- **balance_expert** — Mathematical balance audits, comparison to 2024 PHB/DMG standards.
- **lore_keeper** — LotE world-building consistency, elemental systems, 'cc' currency.
- **rules_editor** — D&D 2024 technical writing style, action economy clarity.

- **Vanguard Class Added (August 2026):** A fourth homebrew class established in `Chapters/Chapter_02d_Vanguard.md`. The Vanguard is a STR/CON martial class with **no spell slots** — magical effects are achieved entirely through **Dakvura (The Shift)** and an **Elemental Arts** use-per-rest pool (2–10 uses, recover half PB on Short Rest). Dakvura grants BPS Resistance, +speed, and scaling enhanced weapon damage (1d4→1d10); it is counterbalanced early-game by **Feral Bloodlust** (forced nearest-target aggression with Charisma save override), which is permanently resolved at Level 9 via *Controlled Bloodlust*. The class features 1d12 Hit Dice, all armor and weapon proficiencies, Weapon Mastery (3), Fighting Style (L1), Extra Attack (L5), Elemental Strike (L6, magical weapons), Ruthless Onslaught (L11, cleave on kill in Dakvura), Relentless Shift (L13, free use on Initiative), Vanguard's Protection (L15, Reaction body-block intercept), Perfected Dakvura (L18, elemental type Resistance + Necrotic Resistance + speed increase), and *Abyssal Executioner* (L20 capstone: unlimited Dakvura; 2 Art uses → 6d10 Con save/Stun). **16 Elemental Disciplines** map one-to-one to the LotE elemental system: *The Infernal Discipline* (Fire), *The Tide Sovereign* (Water), *The Glacial Bulwark* (Ice), *The Stonewarden Discipline* (Earth), *The Gale Sovereign* (Air), *The Stormstrike Discipline* (Lightning), *The Radiant Sovereign* (Laser), *The Miasmic Discipline* (Gas), *The Ironclad Discipline* (Metal), *The Ossuary Tyrant* (Bone), *The Umbral Sovereign* (Shadow), *The Reaping Discipline* (Death), *The Venomblood Sovereign* (Poison), *The Razoreige Discipline* (Knife), *The Void-Iron Sovereign* (Darki), *The Stormcrown Discipline* (Sky). Each subclass provides 4 Elemental Arts at L3 and features at L3, L7, L10, L14, and L17, culminating in elemental Immunity and a free-cost Art at L17. Estimated power budget: 28.0–29.5/40.
- **Resistance & Immunity Progression Audit (August 2026):** Standardized elemental Resistance and Immunity across species, classes, and subclasses. (1) **1st-Level Class Bonds (Savaroen *Spiritual Awakening*, Beast Master *Primal Bond*):** Grant Resistance to the chosen class element if not already possessed (enabling dual resistances for hybrid builds), preventing early Level 1 damage Immunities. (3) **Mark of the Bear — Dragonkin Apex:** Replaced redundant resistance with *Tenacious Resilience* (Advantage on Con saves + once-per-short-rest Reaction to drop to 1 HP instead of 0). (4) **Vanguard Perfected Dakvura (L18):** Replaced redundant Immunity with *Elemental Supremacy* — weapon attacks and Elemental Arts that deal the primary elemental type ignore Resistance and treat Immunity as Resistance.
